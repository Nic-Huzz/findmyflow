How the Three Wheels Work Together

🎯 Skills Wheel (2D)

12 skill types × 5 role archetypes = 60 possible coordinates. Shows WHAT you do and HOW you apply it (Maker, Guide, Analyst, Strategist, Connector).

🌍 Problem Wheel (1D)

12 problem domains organized by Spheres of Impact (Self → Relational → Community → World). Problem TYPE captured separately in Offer Builder.

👥 Persona Wheel (1D)

12 psychographic types representing who you serve. Journey stage (Awakening/Struggling/Ready) captured separately + validated in Validation Flow.
