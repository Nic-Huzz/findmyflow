**Tags:** #origin #courage #career

### The 2-Year Stall
**Tags:** #resistance #subconscious #dome
**Use for:** "Why knowing isn't enough" content, procrastination/fear pieces
> From 2021-2023, wanted to quit his job and live overseas. Nothing changed. His "dome of safety" kept him exactly where he was. The desire existed outside the dome. Proof that intention without subconscious shift = zero movement.
