# Instagram Content Strategy — FindMyFlow / Huzz

## Positioning
**Not:** Another self-help guru account
**Yes:** The nervous system guy who makes transformation fun

**Bio:**
```
Nic "Huzz" Hurrell
The gap between knowing & doing = your nervous system
Healing But Fun 🎮 | Ex-corp → Bali
↓ Take the Earthquake Quiz (3 min)
findmyflow.nichuzz.com/try/earthquake
```

## Target Audience
The Caged Creator: High-performer who optimised their way into a life that looks successful but feels hollow. 28-42, tech/consulting/finance background, course consumer, secretly creative, craving something real.

## Content Pillars (5)

### 1. 🧠 Nervous System Lens (30%)
The differentiator. "It's not a motivation problem, it's a nervous system problem."
- Reframes common self-help advice through biology
- Makes invisible patterns visible
- Format: text-heavy carousels, talking head reels

### 2. 🎮 Healing But Fun (25%)
The vibe. Transformation through play, not suffering.
- Challenge stories (Groans), playful experiments
- "What if growth was actually fun?"
- Format: carousel stories, behind-the-scenes, memes

### 3. 🔄 The 4 R's Framework (20%)
Recognise → Release → Rewire → Reconnect
- Each post maps to one R
- Gives people a repeatable system
- Format: educational carousels, before/after narratives

### 4. 🎭 Anti-Broken (15%)
"You're not broken. You're protected."
- Challenges the entire "fix yourself" industry
- Protective Voices content lives here
- Format: bold text posts, carousels, controversial takes

### 5. 🏔️ Experience > Knowledge (10%)
Huzz's journey. Proof it works.
- Quit corporate, moved to Bali, got sober, built this
- Raw, honest, never preachy
- Format: story-time reels, photo carousels, personal essays

## Visual Style
- **Dark backgrounds** (deep purple #1a0533 to #2d1052) — matches the app
- **Gold accents** (#fbbf24) for highlights/emphasis
- **Clean sans-serif** (Inter or similar)
- **Mobile-first** — designed for vertical scroll
- **No stock photos** — illustrations, text, screenshots, real photos
- **Consistent template system** — recognisable in the feed

## Content Calendar (Weekly)
- **Monday:** Nervous System Lens carousel (educational)
- **Wednesday:** Anti-Broken or 4 R's post (provocative)
- **Friday:** Healing But Fun or personal story (relatable)
- **Stories daily:** Behind-the-scenes, polls, quiz teasers

## Engagement Strategy
- Reply to every comment for first 90 days
- DM anyone who shares a post with a genuine thank you
- Cross-post carousels to LinkedIn (different audience, same content)
- Every carousel ends with quiz CTA or save-worthy framework
