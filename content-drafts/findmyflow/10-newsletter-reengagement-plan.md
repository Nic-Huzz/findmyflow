## 3. RE-ENGAGEMENT SEQUENCE (Week 1-2)

### Email #1: "The Comeback" (Day 1)
**Subject:** "Wahoooo! I'm back. And I built something."
**Purpose:** Reconnect, explain what you've been up to, tease what's coming

**Structure (Huzz voice):**
- Open: "Wahoooo!!! It's been a minute."
- The cringe confession: "I went quiet because I was building something that scared me"
- Quick update: Silent disco → FindMyFlow → BuildWithAI → the whole arc in 6 paragraphs
- The pivot: "I'm starting a weekly letter. Here's what you'll get."
- CTA: "If you're still curious about finding your flow, you're in the right place. If not, no hard feelings — unsubscribe below."
- Groan sign-off

**Segmented P.S.:**
- For program participants: "P.S. — Remember when you shipped that app? I've been thinking about what comes next for builders like you."
- For event attendees: "P.S. — Remember that sunset silent disco at [venue]? That energy is what this whole thing is about."

### Email #2: "The Gift" (Day 4)
**Subject:** "I made you something. It takes 3 minutes."
**Purpose:** Drive them to the Earthquake Quiz — gives value immediately + captures their profile data

**Structure:**
- Hook: "Most people feel stuck but can't name why. I built a quiz that names it for you."
- Explain the Earthquake Score concept (brief — 3 sentences)
- CTA: [Take the Earthquake Quiz → earthquake-quiz.vercel.app]
- Social proof: "X people have taken it. The most common result is [voice type]. What's yours?"
- Groan sign-off

### Email #3: "The Framework" (Day 7)
**Subject:** "The 5 voices that keep you stuck (which one runs your life?)"
**Purpose:** Deliver the Protective Voices framework — this is the "shift" content that gets shared

**Structure:**
- Adapted from blog post `03-blog-five-protective-voices.md`
- Personal story (the CEO who didn't hire him for being "not serious enough")
- The 5 Protective Voices explained
- CTA: "Find yours" → Earthquake Quiz or FindMyFlow
- Groan sign-off

## 4. WEEKLY NEWSLETTER FORMAT (Week 3+)

### Name Options
1. **The Vibe Rise Letter** — ties to Rise & Vibe movement
2. **Puzzle Pieces** — continuity with existing Substack brand
3. **The Flow Letter** — ties to FindMyFlow
4. **The Earthquake Letter** — ties to quiz/diagnostic brand

### Cadence
**Weekly — Thursday morning WITA (Wednesday evening US)**
- Thursday = high open rates
- Morning WITA = catches Bali/APAC audience at breakfast, US audience at end of workday Wednesday

### Format (consistent structure)
Each issue has 4 sections, always in this order:

**1. 🌊 The Wave** (main piece — 500-800 words)
- One idea, one framework, one story
- Rotates between: healing, building, flow, manifestation
- Always starts with a cringe confession or "we think this is normal" reframe
- Always ends with the framework, not the other way around

**2. 🔨 Build Log** (100 words)
- What Huzz is building this week (FindMyFlow update, new feature, event, etc.)
- Radical transparency — include the failures too
- "This week I shipped X. It broke. I fixed it. Here's what I learned."

**3. 🎧 Vibe Check** (50 words)
- One recommendation: song, book, podcast, practice, or experience
- Personal connection: why it matters to Huzz right now

**4. 😬 The Groan** (50 words)
- The dumb/funny/embarrassing moment of the week
- Completely unrelated to the main topic
- This is the section people screenshot and share

### Content Calendar — First 8 Weeks

| Week | 🌊 Wave Topic | Funnel Target |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | Comeback + "What I've been building" | General re-engagement |
| 2 | The Earthquake Quiz + Protective Voices | → Quiz (email capture) |
| 3 | "Healing shouldn't feel like a colonoscopy" (adapted from post #43) | → FindMyFlow |
| 4 | The Wealth Ladder — why AI changes everything | → BuildWithAI |
| 5 | The Dome analogy — why knowing isn't doing | → FindMyFlow |
| 6 | "A Year 10 student shipped an app in 5 hours" | → BuildWithAI |
| 7 | The 4 R's — a framework for unfucking yourself | → FindMyFlow |
| 8 | Fantasy League announcement — "healing but competitive" | → FindMyFlow league signup |

**Pattern:** Alternates FindMyFlow (healing/growth) and BuildWithAI (building/business) every other week, with a constant through-line of personal storytelling.
