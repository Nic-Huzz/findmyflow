# The Earthquake Score: How Stuck You Actually Are (A 3-Minute Assessment)

*~2,000 words | Tim Urban meets Bill Simmons*

---

I'm going to give you a number. A single number that tells you how trapped you are in a career that doesn't fit.

Not a personality type. Not a colour. Not a spirit animal. A *number*.

And once you have it, you can't un-know it.

## Why Most Career Quizzes Are Garbage

Let me rant for a second.

The average career quiz asks you 47 questions like "Do you prefer working alone or in groups?" and then tells you you're an "ENFJ" or a "Blue" or an "Explorer-Architect" and sends you a PDF that says helpful things like "you thrive in collaborative environments."

Cool. Very useful. I'll bring that to my next existential crisis.

These quizzes measure the *surface*. They tell you what you're like at parties. They don't tell you what's *blocking* you from building the career you already know you want.

It's like going to the doctor because your back hurts and they measure your height. "You're 5'11"! Here's a report about being 5'11"." My back still hurts.

## The Three Things That Actually Matter

After five years of career changes, nervous system work, and documenting everything like a maniac, I've found that being "stuck" breaks down into exactly three dimensions:

### 1. Your Protective Voice (Which Cage Are You In?)

We covered this in the last piece. You have a dominant protective mechanism — Perfectionist, Rebel, Caretaker, Ghost, or Controller — and it's running your career from the backseat.

This dimension measures: **What's your brain's default way of keeping you safe?**

### 2. Your Awakening Stage (How Close Is the Edge?)

Not everyone is equally ready to change. There's a progression:

- **Asleep**: "This is fine." (Still fully bought into the Matrix.)
- **Stirring**: "Something's off but I can't name it." (The 2am feeling, but infrequent.)
- **Awake**: "I know this isn't working." (Clear awareness, no action yet.)
- **Rising**: "I'm starting to do something about it." (First steps, lots of false starts.)
- **Moving**: "I'm building the new thing." (In motion, needs momentum.)

This dimension measures: **How conscious are you of the problem?**

### 3. Your Primary Block (What's the Actual Wall?)

This is the most practical dimension. Forget feelings — what is the *specific thing* keeping you stuck?

- **Clarity**: You genuinely don't know what you want (rare, but real)
- **Fear**: You know what you want but can't act on it (most common)
- **Resources**: You know and you're willing, but money/time/support is the barrier
- **Skills**: You need to learn something specific before you can move
- **Environment**: The people around you actively resist your change

This dimension measures: **What would actually unblock you?**

## The Earthquake Score

Here's the formula. It's not complicated:

**Earthquake Score = Protective Voice Intensity × Awakening Stage × Block Severity**

Each dimension is scored 1-5. Your Earthquake Score ranges from 1 (you're fine, close this article) to 125 (your career is a building with structural damage and the tremors are getting stronger).

But here's the insight that makes this different from every other assessment: **the score isn't about how BAD things are. It's about how much POTENTIAL ENERGY is stored in your system.**

In earthquake terms: tectonic plates that move freely don't build pressure. Plates that are stuck build enormous pressure until they rupture violently.

A high Earthquake Score doesn't mean you're broken. It means you have a massive amount of energy locked up — energy that's *going* to release one way or another. The question is whether it releases as a controlled shift or an uncontrolled explosion.

## The Bill Simmons Version

Think of it like the NBA trade deadline.

Some teams are "fine." They're the 7th seed. They'll make the first round and lose in 5 games. Their fans are mildly satisfied. Nothing changes. This is a low Earthquake Score — not enough pressure to force a move.

Other teams are a DISASTER. They have a superstar trapped on a losing team, a coach who won't adapt, and a GM who keeps making lateral moves. Every game increases the pressure. Every loss makes the superstar more miserable. The fanbase is furious. Media is circling.

That team has a high Earthquake Score. The trade is *going* to happen. The only variable is: does the team make a smart, strategic move? Or does it collapse into chaos?

Your career is the same. High pressure demands a response. The smart move is to release it intentionally — with awareness, with a plan, with support. The alternative is burnout, breakdown, or a spectacular "screw it I quit" moment that feels amazing for 48 hours and then gets terrifying.

## How to Take the Quiz (For Real)

Here's a stripped-down version. Grab a pen.

**Question 1: Protective Voice**
When you think about pursuing your real career goal, what happens?
- A) I need to learn more first → Perfectionist (intensity varies)
- B) I reject the whole system → Rebel
- C) Other people need me more → Caretaker
- D) People might see me fail → Ghost
- E) I need a complete plan → Controller

Rate the intensity of that voice: 1 (whisper) to 5 (screaming)

**Question 2: Awakening Stage**
Which is closest to your current state?
- 1 = "My career is fine" (Asleep)
- 2 = "Something's off" (Stirring)
- 3 = "I know this isn't working" (Awake)
- 4 = "I'm starting to make moves" (Rising)
- 5 = "I'm actively building something new" (Moving)

**Question 3: Primary Block**
What is the MAIN thing stopping you?
Rate its severity 1-5.

**Your Earthquake Score:** Voice Intensity × Awakening × Block Severity

## What Your Score Means

**1-20: Low seismic activity.** You're either genuinely content (nice!) or still asleep (less nice). No immediate action needed — but bookmark this for later.

**21-50: Tremors.** You feel it. Something's shifting. This is the stage where most people buy courses, read books, and think really hard without doing anything. The pressure is building.

**51-80: Active fault line.** You are AWAKE and STUCK. This is the danger zone — enough awareness to be miserable, not enough momentum to change. Most people cycle between this zone and a temporary relief (new job, vacation, retail therapy) for years.

**81-125: Earthquake imminent.** Something is going to give. This score means: stop planning. Stop researching. The pressure in your system is too high for more accumulation. You need to MOVE — even if the move is tiny. Tell one person. Build one page. Take one step. Release the energy before it releases you.

## The Point

This isn't a parlour trick. It's a diagnostic.

Most people treat career dissatisfaction like a mood. A phase. Something that'll pass if they get a raise or take a holiday.

It's not a mood. It's *pressure*. And pressure that doesn't get released doesn't go away — it accumulates.

Your Earthquake Score tells you how much pressure is in your system *right now*. Not how smart you are, not what personality type you are, not what colour your aura is. Just: how much stored energy is sitting between where you are and where you know you should be?

And once you know that number, you can do something useful with it.
