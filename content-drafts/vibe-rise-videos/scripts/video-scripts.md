# Video Scripts — Series Intros

Film these as talking head (iPhone, good lighting, 9:16 vertical). Pop-up overlays will be composited on top in Remotion. Each video is ~45-60 seconds.

## 3. HOW THEY SCALED (Intro)

**Hook (0-5s)**
"Every creator you admire started in a room with five people. Here's how they got to five million."

> Pop-up: Series bumper "HOW THEY SCALED"

**The Pattern (5-12s)**
"We traced the evolution of 58 experience creators. Stage by stage. And they all follow the same pattern. Small room. Bigger room. Different medium. Mass reach."

> Pop-up: 4-step ladder graphic animates in:
> Step 1: "5-20 people" 
> Step 2: "50-200 people"
> Step 3: "1,000-5,000 people"
> Step 4: "Millions"

**Example 1 — Tony Robbins (12-25s)**
"Tony Robbins. Stage one, 1979: door-to-door sales, five to twenty people in hotel conference rooms. Stage two: fire-walking seminars, two hundred people in ballrooms. Stage three: arena events, five thousand people. Stage four: TV infomercials, millions of households. Stage five: stadium events and Netflix. Ten thousand plus live."

> Pop-up: Evolution timeline animates through stages 1→5
> Each stage: year range + format + audience size
> Numbers scale up with each step

**Example 2 — Esther Perel (25-35s)**
"Esther Perel. Started with one couple at a time in a therapy office. Then workshops for therapists, fifty to two hundred people. Then TED talks, millions of views. Then she did something genius: she turned her private sessions into a podcast. Real couples, real therapy, millions listening."

> Pop-up: Esther portrait + 4-stage evolution
> Highlight on Stage 4: "Podcast = Private sessions at scale"

**Example 3 — Jay Shetty (35-43s)**
"Jay Shetty went from living with monks in India, to coaching executives at Accenture, to one viral Facebook video with three hundred million views. Four stages in nine years. The fastest scale path of anyone we studied."

> Pop-up: Jay portrait + compressed 4-stage timeline

"The pattern is always the same. You start in a small room where you can feel the impact. You learn what works. Then you find the medium that carries your method to people who'll never be in that room. The room teaches you. The medium scales you."

> Pop-up: Text card: "The room teaches you. The medium scales you."

**CTA (52-60s)**
"What stage are you at right now? Follow for the full breakdown."

> Pop-up: CTA card + "viberise.nichuzz.com"

## Filming Notes

- Shoot each one 2-3 times. Best energy usually comes on take 2.
- Don't memorise word-for-word. Know the beats, improvise the words.
- Vary your energy: calm authority for the insight sections, excitement for the examples.
- Leave pauses where pop-ups appear. The visual needs 1-2 seconds to land.
- Shoot wider than you think. Pop-ups go in the margins.
- Good lighting > good camera. Natural light from a window works.
