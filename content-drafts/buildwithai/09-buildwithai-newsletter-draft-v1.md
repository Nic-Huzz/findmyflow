## Why This Matters (Even If You Don't Want To Be A Developer)

There's a framework called the Ladder of Wealth Creation. It goes like this:

**Rung 1:** Time for money. (Hourly work. The ceiling is how many hours you can stay awake.)

**Rung 2:** Your own service business. (More control, still trading time.)

**Rung 3:** Productised services. (Packaging your expertise. Better, but still requires you.)

**Rung 4:** Selling products. (Build once, sell forever. Income decoupled from time.)

Most service providers — consultants, coaches, freelancers — get stuck between rungs 2 and 3. They *know* they should build a product. But building software required skills or budgets they didn't have.

AI just deleted that barrier.

Now a landscaper can build a portal that manages 50 gardens without 50 phone calls. A consultant can turn their framework into a tool clients pay monthly to access. A coach can build an app that delivers their methodology on autopilot.

This isn't about replacing your services. It's about adding a **product layer** that multiplies your impact and income — without multiplying your hours.
