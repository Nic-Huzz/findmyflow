The Verdict

Option A wins for Dance Events. The zigzag from Guerilla Marketing (Pressure) is the exact signal: "this task type doesn't suit your nervous system." It's a single dot clearly in the wrong column. Option B smooths it into a vague leftward drift.

Breathwork is less dramatic because it's mostly a clean progression (learning → facilitating). But the two "peace" dots at the start still tell a useful story: you weren't in Vibe Rise when you were just training. The shift happened when you started facilitating for others.

The insight for both: When the line enters a quest's "predicted" column and STAYS there, that's Zone of Genius confirmation. When it keeps dipping into Pressure or Fun, those tasks might need delegating or restructuring.

Recommendation: Option A. Show every dot. The zigzag IS the diagnostic. For quests with 20+ challenges where it gets noisy, we could add a "trend mode" toggle later.
