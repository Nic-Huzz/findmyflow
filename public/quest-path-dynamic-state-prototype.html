Dance line tells a story I didn't expect. Starts vibe at L1 (events), dips to X=236 at L2 (sales + reachouts cross into Pressure column), recovers to X=335 at L3 (live events again), then drifts back to Fun at L4 (building apps isn't Vibe Rise). The building work is satisfying but doesn't hit the same way as performing.

Breathwork dips at L1 too. Daily Videos was stressful (X=208, into Pressure). Then it recovers into the Fun/Vibe border and stays there. Hosting breathwork is fun but not Vibe Rise, even at L3. The really intense personal practice (Water Rebirthing, 30-min CCB) is Vibe Rise, but the facilitation itself lands in Fun.

The insight: Vibe Rise comes from the ART (performing, edge-pushing practice), not from the CRAFT (building, facilitating, marketing). Both quests drift right toward Vibe Rise when the art is happening and left when the craft takes over.

Final Verdict

Rolling average (Option B) wins at scale. With 20+ dots per quest and merge lines between quests, Option A becomes visual noise. Option B tells the macro story: where are you trending, where did you dip, and how do your quests relate to each other.

Ghost dots still show individual data. The small faded dots at actual positions let you inspect specific challenges if you want, but the line itself is the smooth trend.

Window=5 feels right. Smooths out one-off outliers but still shows real trends. A single "anxious" dot doesn't move the line much, but three in a row (like the L2 admin cluster) creates a visible dip.
