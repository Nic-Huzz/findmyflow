The format

A Vibe Rise session.

90-120 minutes. 15-30 people. Weekly. Outdoors where possible.

01 Connection
Life path circle (20 min)
Wins. Learnings. "What felt safe this week that didn't before?" No small talk. Real progress, witnessed by your crew.
02 Healing
Facilitated experience (25-30 min)
Delivered by you or a special guest. Breathwork, somatic release, sound journey, cold exposure. Expands the cone from the inside.
03 Wahoo
Courage challenge (25-30 min)
Delivered by a COMMUNITY MEMBER. They share their experience, then lead the group through it. Cold plunge. Vulnerability share. First-time facilitation. Expands the cone from the outside.
04 Close
"I'm someone who ___" (15 min)
Circle. One sentence each. The identity forms here. This is where attendees become tribe.
The secret ingredient

The wahoo is led by a community member.

Not you. Not a professional. Someone from the group who's ready to face their next edge.

Why this works:

When a community member leads the cold plunge for the first time, it's more powerful than a professional doing it for the hundredth. "If they can do it, I can do it."

The person facilitating IS doing their courage challenge. Leading 20 people through something you've never led before is a wahoo on the Authority layer. They're expanding their cone while expanding the group's.

And every session produces someone who wants to facilitate next time. That's how Vibe Rise scales without you.

CrossFit scaled because members became box owners. Vibe Rise scales because attendees become hosts.
