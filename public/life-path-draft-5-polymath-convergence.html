The Polymath Thesis: People know what their curiosities are. The cone of safety is what stops them pursuing them. Common consensus says "pick one thing." Our argument: by following all genuine curiosities, you're more likely to identify rule breaks across industries.

The merge point (?) doesn't have a name yet. It emerges when enough branches grow far enough to intersect. The wider your cone, the more branches you can hold, the more merges you can see.

Keynote Speaker isn't converging with the others. It's a genuine curiosity but it doesn't share skills or audiences with the experience paths. Not every curiosity merges. That's fine. It might merge later, or it might stay a standalone branch.
