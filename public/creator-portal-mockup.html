How did they pay rent?

Most creators solve this wrong.

The creators who made it to full-time didn't wait for their dream model to work. They found bridge income that matched their archetype: rentals, freelance facilitation, corporate workshops, retreats alongside a day job. The ones who failed waited for permission. Which business models actually work?

3 layers. In the right order.

The most successful creators don't sell one thing. They build a 3-layer offer suite: an attraction offer (low-ticket, gets people in the door), a core offer (where the real transformation happens), and a continuity offer (recurring revenue that compounds). Most creators skip the attraction layer and wonder why nobody shows up.
