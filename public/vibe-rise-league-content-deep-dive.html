🔥 Wahoo Videos

Setup. Action. Reveal.

Every Wahoo video follows the same three-act structure. The audience watches the fear, the decision, and the aftermath.

Setup
What you're about to do
Film yourself explaining what the Wahoo is and why it scares you. Sitting in the car before. Pacing. The honest "I don't want to do this."
15-30s · talking to camera · raw
Action
Doing the thing
Footage of the Wahoo itself. Going live. Running the event. Sharing the post. Shaky cam is fine. Real is better than polished.
30-90s · BTS footage · real-time
Reveal
Was that a Wahoo?
Post-action debrief. How it felt. What shifted. The self-classification: "Hell yes" / "Felt alive" / "Just did it." The emotional payoff.
15-30s · talking to camera · immediate

💜 Healing Videos

Recognition. Not the wound.

Share what you recognised about yourself, not the trauma itself. The Queer Eye principle: empathy first, never exploitative.

The Pattern
Name it
"I recognised I've been using busyness as a Controller pattern." Name the defence pattern. Explain how it shows up. Make it relatable.
60-90s · reel or carousel
The Explainer
Teach it
The educational layer. What the pattern is. Why it exists. How it shows up for experience creators specifically. Framework content.
60-90s · talking head + text overlay
The Shift
What changed
"After recognising this, here's what I did differently this week." The actionable takeaway. Before/after in behaviour, not just feeling.
30-60s · reel or story

☀️ Tune Videos

The streak IS the content.

One daily Tune post is boring. 30 in a row is a transformation documentary. The 75 Hard effect.

Morning Practice
"Day 12. Cold plunge."
15s clip of the practice. Breathwork, movement, cold exposure. Same format every day. The repetition builds the narrative.
15s · story · daily
State Check-in
"Today: Vibe Rise."
Text overlay on selfie. One of four states: Vibe Rise, Safe, Activated, Shutdown. Tracks the emotional arc across the season.
Static · story · daily
Weekly Compilation
"7 days in 30 seconds"
Edit all 7 daily clips into one reel. Shows the week's arc. The compilation is the shareable piece.
30s · reel · weekly
