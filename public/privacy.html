Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026

Vibe Rise ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Nicholas Hurrell. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile app and website.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address when you sign up (via magic link authentication)
  • Profile data: Name, archetype results, skills, and other information you choose to provide through app flows
  • Usage data: Challenge completions, experience creation data, checklist progress, and content you generate
  • Device information: Device type and operating system for app compatibility

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Vibe Rise app experience
  • To personalise your content, recommendations, and AI-generated outputs
  • To send push notifications (only with your permission)
  • To track your progress across challenges, experiences, and business growth

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS). All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Vercel: Web hosting and analytics
  • Anthropic (Claude): AI content generation and coaching insights. Your responses are processed to generate personalised results but are not stored by Anthropic for model training.
  • Google (Gemini): AI image generation for avatars and visual content. Photo data is processed to generate artwork but is not retained by Google for model training.
  • OpenAI (GPT-4o): AI image generation (fallback provider). Photo data is processed to generate artwork but is not retained by OpenAI for model training.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data at any time through the app
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we remove all personal data within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Vibe Rise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.
