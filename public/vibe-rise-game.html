QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

You'll see a single question: "How are you right now?" Tap one of four nervous system states. That's your baseline. From there, three tabs, each with a list of activities to choose from: Tune (daily practices like breathwork, cold exposure, movement), Wahoo (edge-crossing challenges from your bucket list), and Healing (recognise, release, rewire). Pick what feels right for where you are today.
The system meets you where you are. If you're mostly in Shutdown, it starts with regulation, not activation. If you miss a day, the streak is forgiving. If you only do the daily check-in and nothing else, that's still data. There's no failing here. There's just checking in.
Yes. Always. No credit card, no trial that auto-charges, no paywall. The app is free.
Those apps train one state: Peace. That's good, but it's not growth. Vibe Rise trains the state above it: alive AND safe at the same time. We don't just teach you to relax. We train your nervous system to hold activation without crashing. That's the difference between calm and alive.
Your nervous system changes week by week, not day by day. Each 7-day cycle gives you a Capacity Score, a state distribution, and a weekly review. You can see what moved, what didn't, and what to adjust. Then the next cycle starts. It's ongoing, not a one-off challenge.
The daily check-in takes 60 seconds. A daily practice takes 5-10 minutes. A Wahoo challenge varies: some take 2 minutes (posting something publicly), some take an hour (showing up to a class). You choose. The minimum is 5 minutes. The sweet spot is 15.
