## About FindMyFlow

FindMyFlow helps burnt-out professionals discover their ideal career path through AI-guided self-discovery flows. The Money Model flows below help users design their business offer stack — from customer acquisition (Attraction) through revenue maximisation (Upsell, Downsell, Continuity) and lead generation strategy.

### Framework: Alex Hormozi's $100M Offers

Each flow asks 10 questions about the user's business, then scores their answers against multiple offer strategies. The highest-scoring non-disqualified offer becomes the recommendation. Users can then follow implementation checklists to build their offer.
