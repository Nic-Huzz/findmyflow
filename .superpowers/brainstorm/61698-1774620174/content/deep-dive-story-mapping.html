Proposed Story Sequence: The Healing Journey

Reordered by wound stage, not by 4 R's modality

1
Chapter 1: "Where Are Your Edges?"
Stage 1 (Nervous System) + Stage 2 (Attachment)
Flow: Map Nervous System Boundaries
Story: "Before anything else, let's find the invisible fence. Where does your nervous system say STOP? How much money feels safe? How much visibility? What beliefs are keeping the fence in place?"
Discovers: Earning edge, visibility edge, safety contracts, protective archetype
2
Chapter 2: "What Are You Actually Hungry For?"
Stage 2 (Attachment) + Stage 3 (School)
Flow: Healing Compass
Story: "Now let's go deeper. What need went unmet? What's the thing you've been craving that your protective pattern keeps blocking? And where does it live in your body?"
Discovers: Primary need, protective pattern, emotional splinter (6D body map)
3
Chapter 3: "What's The Code Running The Show?"
Stage 3 (School / The Factory)
Flow: Matrix Codes
Story: "School installed an operating system you didn't choose. Let's name the code. What are the 3 beliefs running your life? What archetype enforces them? This is the programming. And programming can be rewritten."
Discovers: Top 3 limiting beliefs, behavior patterns, dominant archetype + core wound
4
Chapter 4: "What Did You Hide To Fit In?"
Stage 4 (Constructed Life)
Flow: Shadow Work (needs un-archiving + update)
Story: "You took that operating system and built a life with it. What parts of yourself did you hide to make it work? What shadows did you disown? This is the constructed life audit."
Discovers: Shadow traits, suppressed essence, origin story, the authentic self that got edited out
5
Chapter 5: "What Finally Broke?"
Stage 5 (The Crack) — NEW CONTENT NEEDED
Flow: Limiting Belief Rewire (partial) + NEW: Crack Recognition Flow
Story: "Something cracked. Burnout, breakdown, quiet knowing, unexpected aliveness. What was it? This is the moment the constructed life stopped working. This is why you're here."
Discovers: Crack type, the safety contract that broke, origin event traced back, the first step of the rewrite

The Story It Tells

Chapter 1 finds the fence → Chapter 2 finds what's behind it → Chapter 3 names who built it → Chapter 4 shows what you traded to live inside it → Chapter 5 is the moment you decided the fence has to come down.
Each chapter builds on the last. By Chapter 5, the user has a complete map of how they got from 0,0 (pure self) to Unfulfilment (constructed self) — told through their own specific data, not generic theory.
