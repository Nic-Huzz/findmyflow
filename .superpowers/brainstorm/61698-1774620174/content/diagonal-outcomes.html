The 6 Diagonals: What Living There Looks Like

Each stage's diagonal position + what safely operating from that space produces

Stage 1
Identity Sweet Spot
Y: Authenticity | X: Belonging
THE DIAGONAL
Authentic expression and belonging move together. Accepted as yourself.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ You know who you are and can name it
✨ You show up as yourself without editing for the room
✨ People connect with the REAL you, not a performance
✨ Content, conversations, and offers feel genuine
✨ You stop second-guessing "is this me?"
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Can describe your essence archetype in your own words
● Screen layer challenges feel natural, not performative
● Voice check-ins show essence > protective
● Others start saying "you seem different"
Stage 2
Vulnerability Sweet Spot
Y: Shared | X: Readiness
THE DIAGONAL
What you share matches how ready the relationship is. Calibrated honesty.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ Genuine connections, not transactional ones
✨ You can ask for what you need without shame
✨ Your content has depth without being oversharing
✨ Discovery calls become real conversations
✨ People trust you because you trust them calibratedly
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Can go live without scripting every word
● Share struggles AND wins without performing either
● Validation conversations happen naturally
● Emotional splinter feels smaller or less charged
Stage 3 — THE HINGE
Good Enough Sweet Spot
Y: Effort | X: Quality
THE DIAGONAL
Effort matches quality standard. Ship, learn, iterate. Good enough IS enough.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ You ship things before they're perfect
✨ The inner critic is named and no longer runs the show
✨ You can quote your price without apologizing
✨ "Good enough" stops feeling like failure
✨ You actually START the business, the project, the thing
✨ This is where action begins. The permission gate opens.
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Product designed and price set (not perfect, but set)
● Money layer challenges completed without spiraling
● Can say "this is what I charge" out loud
● Inner critic voice recognized in real-time, not after the fact
Stage 4
Groan Zone
Y: Challenge | X: Ability
THE DIAGONAL
Challenge matches ability. Growth happens here. Not too safe, not overwhelming.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ You take on challenges calibrated to your actual ability
✨ Failure becomes data, not identity collapse
✨ You build offers, products, systems at the right level
✨ Impostor syndrome is present but doesn't stop you
✨ Each completed challenge ACTUALLY expands capacity
✨ Business grows at the pace your nervous system can hold
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Money models complete (offer suite designed)
● Grand Slam offer built and stack defined
● Vulnerable layer challenges stretching without breaking
● Earning edge has visibly expanded from NS Boundaries baseline
Stage 5
Living Zone
Y: Output | X: Emotional Wellbeing
THE DIAGONAL
Output matches emotional resource. Sustainable. Wellbeing is the engine, not the reward.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ You produce without burning out
✨ Rest is scheduled, not earned
✨ Weekly planning uses Push/Flow/Rest/Launch rhythm
✨ Campaign and funnel built from energy, not desperation
✨ Launch date exists AND you feel resourced for it
✨ Wellbeing comes BEFORE output, not after
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Campaign created without burnout cycle
● Launch date announced publicly
● Authority layer challenges feel earned, not performed
● Flow Compass shows North (ease + excited) as dominant
● Root belief rewired (traced + rewritten)
Stage 6 — BOSS LEVEL
Project Sweet Spot
Y: Risk | X: Passion
THE DIAGONAL
Risk scales with passion. The more it matters, the more you're willing to bet. Courageous, not reckless.
What Safely Operating Here Produces
✨ You're building from genuine passion, not obligation
✨ Risks feel natural because the fuel is real
✨ Not moving becomes the greater risk
✨ Business IS your essence expressed in the world
✨ You can't NOT do this — it's who you are
✨ The constructed agenda has been fully replaced by genuine fuel
Proof You're On The Diagonal
● Launched and tracking real metrics
● Cross-layer challenges from passion, not obligation
● Funnel metrics being reviewed with curiosity, not anxiety
● Business feels like play, not work
● Fun as a genuine signal — present without manufacturing it

Where Daily Healing + Play-List Fit At Each Stage

Each stage has three activity types running simultaneously:
Deep Dive
One-time per stage
The recognition moment. Complete once to unlock the rest of the stage. This is the X axis leap.
Play-List Challenges
Weekly at current layer
The courage embodiment. Scary + exciting actions at the visibility layer that matches your level. This is the Y axis in action.
Daily Healing
Ongoing practices
The nervous system maintenance. Breathwork, meditation, dance, prayer, trigger recognition, voice check-ins. Keeps the window of tolerance open so the deep work and challenges can land.
STAGE 3 (ENOUGH) — Example Week:

Mon: Matrix Codes deep dive (one-time, 15 min)              ← Deep Dive
Tue: Morning breathwork + trigger recognition              ← Daily Healing
Wed: Money layer challenge: "Quote price to a lead"       ← Play-List
Thu: Voice check-in (essence vs protective today)         ← Daily Healing
Fri: Meditation + Compass direction log                   ← Daily Healing
Sat: Money challenge: "Follow up on unpaid invoice"     ← Play-List
Sun: Weekly reflection + 3% improvement note            ← Daily Healing

The Diagonal as Graduation Criteria

"Nailing the diagonal" at a stage means the user can SAFELY OPERATE from that sweet spot position. Not perfectly. Not permanently. But as the default orientation. The proof isn't a quiz score. It's the combination of:

1. Deep dive complete (they see the pattern)
2. Essence zone challenges at that layer (they've acted from the diagonal)
3. Daily healing consistent (nervous system can hold the new position)
4. Deliverables produced (the external proof that internal shift happened)

All four together = the diagonal. Any one alone is insufficient.
