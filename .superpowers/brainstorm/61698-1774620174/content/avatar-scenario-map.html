Onboarding: Avatar + Scenario Map

Each wound stage has 3 zones. User's face appears in AI-generated scene for each choice.

The Vision

User uploads a photo. AI generates their hero avatar (0,0 — pure self). Then at each stage, they see three AI-generated scenes using their face in different situations. They pick the one that resonates. The wound announces itself through recognition, not self-report. By the end, they have a visual story of how they got from 0,0 to here.

Avatar Scene Generation Concept

Per stage: 3 template scenes
  • Each scene is a pre-designed template (art style, composition, mood)
  • User's face is composited in via AI (same tech as hero avatar)
  • Scene ages with the stage: infant → child → teenager → adult → current
  • Consistent art style across all 15 scenes (5 stages x 3 options)
Technical approach
  • OpenAI image generation (already have generate-avatar edge function)
  • Could generate all 15 scenes upfront after photo upload (parallel)
  • Or generate 3 per stage as they progress (sequential, faster start)
  • Selected scenes become their "journey gallery" — shareable

The Payoff: A Visual Story of How You Got Here

At the end of onboarding, the user has 5 selected scenes with their face — a visual journey from pure self to constructed self. This is the thing they screenshot. This is the "I feel so seen" moment. It's their story, told back to them through images they chose, with their face in every frame.
