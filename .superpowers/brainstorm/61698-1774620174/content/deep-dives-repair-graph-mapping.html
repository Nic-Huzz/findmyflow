What This Means for the Product

The deep dives aren't just healing exercises. They're the levels of the game. Each one unlocks the next graph. You can't do Growth work until Enough is addressed. You can't sustain Execution until the root belief is rewired. The sequence is built into the biology — and it's already built into the deep dives. They just need to be presented in this order, with this framing.
