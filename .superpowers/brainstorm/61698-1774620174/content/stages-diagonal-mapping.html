The Full Stage Map: What "Nailing the Diagonal" Requires

Mapping healing levels + business stages + deliverables into one coherent progression

The Diagonal = Self-Knowledge + Action Moving Together

"Nailing the diagonal" at each stage means: you've done the inner work (deep dive) AND you've taken aligned action (deliverables + challenges). Neither alone is enough. The deep dive without deliverables = Head Full of Dreams. The deliverables without the deep dive = Unfulfilment 2.0 — building the wrong thing again.

Users have freedom to explore within each stage, but each stage has a clear "you've nailed it" gate combining inner work + outer proof.

Guided Outcomes + Freedom: The Balance

What's GUIDED (non-negotiable)
  • Healing deep dive per stage (must complete)
  • Minimum essence zone challenges per layer
  • Stage sequence (1 before 2 before 3...)
  • Business stages don't unlock until Stage 3 gate
What's FREE (choose your adventure)
  • Which challenges to take at each layer
  • Which skills/problems/personas to challenge against
  • Pace (1 challenge/week or 3/week)
  • Challenges at any UNLOCKED layer (not just current)
  • Daily healing practices (breathwork, meditation, etc.)
  • Business deliverable order within a stage
  • When to engage Zarlo for coaching

What About Non-Business Users (Vibe Seekers)?

The healing journey (Stages 1-6) works without any business deliverables. Vibe seekers skip the business deliverables but still progress through healing deep dives + visibility layer challenges. The diagonal for them is self-knowledge + courage (showing up as yourself), not self-knowledge + business building. Same graph, different Y axis definition.
