Play-List + Progression: How It All Fits Together

The deep dives are the RECOGNITION. The Play-List challenges are the EMBODIMENT.

The Two Halves of Each Level

Deep Dive = Recognise
The deep dive gives you the MAP. You see the wound, name the pattern, understand where it came from. This is X-axis work (self-knowledge).
Play-List Challenges = Embody
The Play-List challenges make you LIVE it. You take the scary action at the right visibility layer. This is Y-axis work (action from self-knowledge).
Recognition without action = Head Full of Dreams. Action without recognition = Unfulfilment. Both together = the diagonal.

Each Level Has: Deep Dive → Play-List Challenges → Gate

The Visibility Layers = The Embodiment Sequence

The 5 visibility layers already escalate in the same order as the healing sequence:
SCREEN   → Identity    "Can I show up as me?"         (lowest stakes)
LIVE     → Vulnerability "Can I be honest in real time?"
MONEY    → Enough      "Am I worth it?"
VULNERABLE → Growth      "Can I grow past the fence?"
AUTHORITY  → Execution   "Can I sustain being the real me?" (highest stakes)
This wasn't designed. It emerged. The visibility layers were built to escalate fear. The healing graphs were built to escalate capacity. They landed in the same order because they're measuring the same thing from two angles.

Where Business Stages Unlock

Level 3 (Enough SS / Money Layer) is the hinge. The framework says "Enough is the permission gate before any action becomes possible." This is where the Y-axis activates. Business stages 1-7 unlock here — once the user has permission to move, they can start building.

Levels 1-3 = Head Full of Dreams (X axis building, repair phase)
Level 3 gate = The transition from repair to building
Levels 4-6 = Self-Actualisation beginning (Y axis activates, both axes moving)
