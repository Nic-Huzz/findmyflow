Onboarding: Walking the Wound Stages

User creates avatar, then locates themselves at each stage of 0,0 → Unfulfilment

1

Create Your Hero Avatar

Upload a photo, AI generates their hero avatar. This is "you at 0,0" — before the wounds were installed. The pure version. Sets the emotional anchor for the entire journey.

Then: Walk the 5 Wound Installation Stages

At each stage, the user chooses where they land on the graph. This builds their personal map of how life shaped them.

Stage 1 — Infancy
Nervous System
"Were your signals met with warmth or anxiety?"
High Y
Fight/flight on high alert. Always scanning for danger.
On the Line
Mixed signals. Safe enough, but learned to read the room.
High X
Shutdown mode. Learned to disappear when things got loud.
Stage 2 — Childhood
Attachment
"When did love start to feel conditional?"
High Y
Performer. Learned love = achievements, gold stars, being good.
On the Line
Knew you were loved, but also knew the rules.
High X
Ghost. Learned to not need anything from anyone.
Stage 3 — School
The Factory
"When did enough stop being enough?"
High Y
Perfectionist. Grades = worth. Never good enough.
On the Line
Played the game, knew it was a game.
High X
People Pleaser. Kept the peace, lost your voice.
Stage 4 — Adulthood
The Blueprint
"Whose life did you build?"
High Y
Controller. Built the empire, forgot who for.
On the Line
Some of it's yours, some of it isn't. You can tell.
High X
Ghost. Never built anything, because nothing felt like you.
Stage 5 — Now
The Crack
"What finally broke?"
High Y
Burnout. Got everything you wanted and it means nothing.
On the Line
Quiet knowing. Something has to change.
High X
Paralysis. Know exactly what's wrong but can't move.

What This Produces

Personal Wound Map
5 data points showing their pattern across life stages. "Mostly High Y = Performer/Controller pattern. Shifted to High X at School = learned to hide."
Journey Starting Point
Their Crack type tells us where to start. The pattern across stages tells Zarlo and the challenge system what to prioritize.
