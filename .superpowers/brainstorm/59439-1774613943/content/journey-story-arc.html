The Onboarding Experience Must Deliver:

😱

"I feel so seen"

The diagnostic mirrors them so accurately they screenshot it. Observer/projection format bypasses the analytical brain. The wound announces itself.

💡

"Now I have words for it"

The framework gives them language they've never had. "I'm in Head Full of Dreams." "My Performer is running the show." Articulation = empowerment.

🌟

"This is the coolest thing ever"

The visual experience is so premium, the interactions so surprising, that they're genuinely excited. Not another boring quiz. A mirror they've never seen.

The Key Design Question

The onboarding needs to locate the user on this journey AND give them the language AND blow their mind visually. The current 4-tension-layer diagnostic (Discover/Regulate/Reveal/Value) is elegant but doesn't tell this story. How do we redesign onboarding to do all three?
