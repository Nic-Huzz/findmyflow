## The Three Models

Each represents a fundamentally different philosophy:

| | Duolingo | Hades | Celeste |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Philosophy** | "You'll feel bad if you stop" | "Failure feeds growth" | "You're enough. Keep going." |
| **Primary motivation** | Loss avoidance (Black Hat) | Curiosity + creativity (White Hat) | Epic meaning (White Hat) |
| **Engagement driver** | Fear of losing streak | Desire to see what's next | Desire to prove something to yourself |
| **Failure model** | Punishment (streak resets to ZERO) | Reward (death = new content) | Neutral (death = instant retry, no cost) |
| **Best for** | Daily habit formation | Sustained creative engagement | Therapeutic/wellness contexts |

**Critical insight for Vibe Rise:** You're building a wellness app for burnt-out people. Duolingo's model would make them MORE anxious. Celeste's model would be too passive. Hades' model is the sweet spot: failure feeds progress, every "death" brings you home to warmth and growth.

## 1. DUOLINGO (Score: 525)

### What It Is
A language-learning app used daily by 50M+ people. The most successful gamified habit app ever built. DAU grew 10x in 4 years (5M to 50M+).

### Octalysis Breakdown

**CD1: Epic Meaning (5/10)**
- "Join 500M learners" social proof. "Free education for the world" mission.
- But most users don't feel epic purpose. They're doing it because they feel guilty NOT doing it.
- The owl is not inspiring. It's nagging.

**CD2: Development & Accomplishment (10/10)**
- XP per lesson (10-40 XP), combo bonuses, level progression (25 levels per course)
- 10-tier league system (Bronze to Diamond) with weekly promotion/demotion
- Daily quests (3/day), monthly challenges (20-100 tasks), achievement badges
- Immediate audio/visual feedback on every answer (satisfying dings, green flashes)
- Lesson completion = 25% more lessons overall (Duolingo's own data)

**CD3: Empowerment of Creativity (5/10)**
- Minimal. You're doing preset exercises, not creating language.
- Some sentence construction is creative, but feedback is binary (right/wrong).
- No meaningful choices. The "Path" is linear.
- Analytics dashboard provides some self-reflection.

**CD4: Ownership & Possession (8/10)**
- The streak IS the product. Users own their streak like a pet.
- Gems/Lingots as currency. Badge collection (monthly exclusives).
- Avatar customization (Duo outfits). Course progress as "mine."
- The Streak Society (7/30/100/365 day milestones) creates tiers of ownership.

**CD5: Social Influence (9/10)**
- 30-person leagues refreshing weekly. Users with leagues complete 40% more lessons.
- Friend Streaks (mutual accountability, shared streaks break if EITHER misses).
- Friend Quests (cooperative weekly challenges). Friends Clash (1v1 competitive).
- Nudge system (remind friends to practice). Social feed showing friend progress.
- Users who follow friends are 5.6x more likely to finish their course.

**CD6: Scarcity & Impatience (9/10)**
- Hearts/Energy system: limited attempts before you're locked out.
- Timed XP boosts (15 min windows). Daily quests that expire at midnight.
- Monthly badges that vanish if not completed. Streak freeze limited to 2 (or 5 for Society).
- Early Bird / Night Owl chests create appointment dynamics (morning/evening windows).
- League resets every Monday (weekly urgency cycle).

**CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity (7/10)**
- Variable XP rewards. Random league opponents each week.
- Notification variety (bandit algorithm selects from hundreds of templates, learns what works).
- Surprise chests. Random quest assignments.
- But: the actual learning content is predictable. You know what you're getting.

**CD8: Loss & Avoidance (10/10)**
- **Streak resets to ZERO.** Not minus one. Total wipe. A 180-day streak becomes 0.
- League demotion (visible animation, drops one full tier).
- Heart depletion (can't learn when hearts = 0, must wait 6 hours or pay).
- Friend streak breaking (YOUR friend also loses THEIR streak because of you).
- "Duo is sad" notifications (emotional guilt). 62% of users report feeling "guilty."
- 48-hour repair window creates urgency after break.
- Listed on deceptive.design for pushy dark patterns.

### The Duolingo Flywheel
```
Streak builds → Creates daily habit → Generates league XP → 
Creates social competition → Drives more lessons → Deepens sunk cost → 
Makes streak more valuable → Makes loss more painful → REPEAT
```

### What Vibe Rise Should STEAL from Duolingo
1. **The single target behaviour.** Everything pushes toward ONE action: "complete one short lesson today." For Vibe Rise: "Do one thing in the app today."
2. **Journey-stage calibration.** Different mechanics activate at different maturity points. Day 1 = achievements. Day 7 = streak protection. Day 30 = leagues. Day 365 = VIP.
3. **The forgiving streak (already have this).** Vibe Rise's 1-day-miss allowance is smarter than Duolingo's zero-to-zero reset for a wellness context.
4. **Weekly competition cycles.** The Monday reset is genius. Fresh competition every week prevents the "I'm too far behind" despair.
5. **AI-driven notification variety.** The bandit algorithm (testing which notification works best per user) is directly applicable to Zarlo's nudge engine.

### What Vibe Rise Should AVOID from Duolingo
1. **The guilt owl.** Making anxious people feel guilty for missing a day is counterproductive for a healing app. 62% feeling "guilty" is a FEATURE for Duolingo, a BUG for Vibe Rise.
2. **Streak reset to zero.** Losing 180 days of progress because of one bad day would be devastating for someone in sympathetic/dorsal state. The "forgiving streak" is correct.
3. **Hearts/energy as punishment for mistakes.** Punishing errors in a healing context is toxic. Learning from mistakes is the POINT.
4. **Overly Black Hat balance.** Duolingo's CD8 at 10/10 works for habit formation but creates anxiety. Vibe Rise's users already HAVE anxiety.

## 2. HADES (Score: 498)

### What It Is
A roguelike action game where you play Zagreus, son of Hades, trying to escape the Underworld. When you die, you return home. Death feeds progress. 300,000+ words of voiced dialogue. Every run is different.

### Octalysis Breakdown

**CD1: Epic Meaning & Calling (7/10)**
- Zagreus escaping to find his mother (Persephone). Greek mythology framing.
- Personal quest, not save-the-world. But deeply emotional.
- The House of Hades characters make you feel PART of something.

**CD2: Development & Accomplishment (9/10)**
- Mirror of Night: 12 permanent stat upgrades purchased with Darkness (earned every run).
- Weapon Aspects: 6 weapons x 4 aspects x 5 levels = long-term progression.
- Keepsake ranks: 25 trinkets, each with 3 levels (earned by using them in combat).
- Heat system: 32 self-imposed difficulty levels after first clear. Fresh rewards per Heat level.
- Fated List: 55 prophecies serving as achievement/quest log.
- Every run yields SOMETHING permanent, even if you die in 2 minutes.

**CD3: Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback (9/10)**
- 10 gods x dozens of boons x 6 weapons x 4 aspects = near-infinite build combinations.
- Duo Boons (require specific prerequisites from 2 gods) reward knowledge and creativity.
- Chaos Gates: accept a curse (temporary debuff) for a powerful permanent buff. Risk/reward.
- Build diversity means no two runs feel the same. You're constantly experimenting.
- Immediate combat feedback (damage numbers, sound design, visual effects).

**CD4: Ownership & Possession (8/10)**
- House customization (rugs, furniture, music, decorations). Makes "home" feel like YOUR space.
- Keepsake collection (gifts FROM characters you love = emotional ownership).
- Codex completion (bestiary/encyclopedia). Weapon collection.
- The House of Hades itself is your "home base" that you personalise over time.

**CD5: Social Influence & Relatedness (9/10)**
- The relationship system IS the game's core loop. Nectar/Ambrosia gifting.
- 3 romance paths. 25+ characters with deep, evolving dialogue.
- Characters react to HOW you died, what you encountered, your progress.
- Hypnos comments comedically on each death (social witness without judgment).
- 300,000 words of unique dialogue means characters never repeat. They feel ALIVE.
- Context-sensitive: if you die to Meg, she'll mention it when you see her at home.

**CD6: Scarcity & Impatience (6/10)**
- Titan Blood, Diamonds, Ambrosia are rare currencies (soft-gated).
- Some characters won't accept gifts until story conditions are met (locked dialogue).
- Boon choices force you to pick 1 of 3 (can't have everything per run).
- But: the game is GENEROUS. You always earn SOMETHING. Nothing feels withheld.

**CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity (9/10)**
- Randomized boons, room orders, enemy compositions, and rewards per run.
- Chaos Gates (mystery risk/reward). Fish spawns (random relaxation breaks).
- New dialogue EVERY time you return home. 50+ hours before any repetition.
- "What will the gods offer me this run?" drives each new attempt.
- Boss dialogue changes based on your win/loss record against them.

**CD8: Loss & Avoidance (5/10)**
- **DELIBERATELY MINIMIZED.** Death is a seamless transition home. No "Game Over" screen.
- Zagreus quips about dying (models healthy attitude toward failure).
- Characters WELCOME you back. Hypnos is funny, not shaming.
- Death unlocks MORE content than survival. Returning home = new dialogue, new story, new shop items.
- God Mode: incrementally increases damage resistance with each death (no stigma).
- Death Defiance gives 3 extra lives per run, purchasable/upgradeable.
- The only "loss" is temporary boons from the current run. Everything permanent persists.

### The Hades Loop
```
Attempt escape → Fight → Die → Return home (REWARD) → 
New dialogue + story + upgrades + gifts → Attempt again with new builds → 
Die differently → New dialogue acknowledges HOW you died → REPEAT
```

### What Vibe Rise Should STEAL from Hades

1. **Death = Coming Home.** When a user "fails" (breaks streak, drops to dorsal state, misses a wahoo), the return should feel like the House of Hades: warm, welcoming, full of new content. Not punishment. Homecoming.

2. **"No wasted runs."** Even a 2-minute failed run in Hades yields Darkness, story, and keepsake XP. Even a "bad day" in Vibe Rise should yield SOMETHING. "You checked in as Shutdown today. That's data. That's self-knowledge. +2 RP for honesty."

3. **Story requires failure.** Hades' plot ONLY advances when you die. What if Vibe Rise's deepest insights only unlocked after setbacks? "You've dropped to Activated 3 times this week. A pattern is emerging. Zarlo has something to show you."

4. **Relationship-as-progression.** The Nectar/Ambrosia system makes relationships feel like levels. Zarlo's relationship with the user should DEEPEN over time, unlocking new capabilities, new honesty, new vulnerability. "Zarlo Trust Level 3: Now asks harder questions."

5. **Context-sensitive reactions.** Hades characters respond to HOW you died. Zarlo should respond to WHAT happened. "You skipped the Healing tab after that Pressure wahoo. Want to talk about it?" Not generic. Specific.

6. **Randomized builds per "run" (week).** Each week in Vibe Rise could offer a different "boon set" — randomly selected focus areas, practices, or challenges. "This week's offering: Vulnerability + Connection + Rest. Choose 2."

7. **The House Contractor model.** Permanent unlocks that make your "home base" (the app) more beautiful/functional over time. Cosmetic + functional upgrades purchased with earned currency.

8. **The "true ending" requires BOTH success AND failure.** Hades' true ending needs ~10 successful escapes AND dozens of deaths between them. Vibe Rise's journey should require the full spectrum: both Vibe Rise moments AND Shutdown moments contribute to reaching insight. Not just the wins.

9. **God Mode: shame-free difficulty reduction.** Hades gives 2% cumulative damage resistance per death. It's visible, it's opt-in, no stigma. Same philosophy as Celeste's Assist Mode. Vibe Rise should have its own: "If this week was hard, next week's challenges scale gentler. Automatically."

### What Vibe Rise Should AVOID from Hades
1. **Nothing major.** Hades' philosophy is almost perfectly aligned with a wellness app. The only caution: don't make "returning home" SO rewarding that users prefer failure to courage.

### Notable: Hades' CD5 is entirely NPC-driven
Hades is a SINGLE-PLAYER game. Its 9/10 Social score comes entirely from AI characters, not real people. This is directly relevant: Vibe Rise is also primarily solo. Zarlo CAN be the social layer without requiring real community infrastructure. The relationships feel real because they're context-sensitive and evolving, not because they're with real humans.

## 3. CELESTE (Score: 312)

### What It Is
A precision platformer about a young woman named Madeline climbing a mountain while battling anxiety and self-doubt. The game's "dark reflection" antagonist (Badeline) is literally a protective voice trying to keep Madeline safe by preventing her from taking risks.

### Why the Low Score IS the Point

Celeste deliberately suppresses Black Hat drives (CD6-8) that most games exploit. It achieves incredible emotional impact and player retention with almost ZERO loss aversion. This is revolutionary for game design and DIRECTLY applicable to a wellness app.

**The lesson:** You don't need to make people afraid of losing to keep them engaged. You can keep them engaged through meaning, mastery, and self-determined challenge.

### Octalysis Breakdown

**CD1: Epic Meaning & Calling (8/10)**
- "The mountain is not the obstacle. You are." Climbing = overcoming your own mind.
- The narrative delivers genuine meaning without lecturing. The mechanics ARE the lesson.
- Players report feeling genuinely proud, genuinely moved. Not just "entertained."
- The game communicates: this struggle matters. YOUR struggle matters.

**CD2: Development & Accomplishment (7/10)**
- Death counter framed as learning: "Be proud of your death count! The more you die, the more you're learning."
- B-Sides and C-Sides provide progressive mastery challenges (optional, never required).
- Golden Strawberries (complete chapter deathless) = ultimate skill proof.
- No XP, no levels, no score. Progress = "I can do things I couldn't before."

**CD3: Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback (7/10)**
- Multiple solutions per room. Speedrunners find entirely different routes.
- Assist Mode lets players CUSTOMIZE their difficulty (speed, dashes, invincibility). This IS creativity.
- Instant respawn = instant feedback. No delay between attempt and retry.
- The dash mechanic (limited resource) forces creative decision-making every second.

**CD4: Ownership & Possession (6/10)**
- 175 strawberries as optional collectibles (proof of skill, no gameplay function).
- Death count as YOUR story (no one else's).
- Hair colour as visible "energy state" (dash available vs. spent).
- But minimal: no inventory, no customisation, no currency.

**CD5: Social Influence & Relatedness (5/10)**
- Theo as companion (emotional support NPC in chapters 5-6).
- Community speedrun culture and Golden Strawberry challenge culture.
- But fundamentally a solo experience. No multiplayer, no leaderboards in-game.

**CD6: Scarcity & Impatience (6/10)**
- Limited dash (one per airborne sequence, refreshed on ground). Forces strategic use.
- Limited stamina on climbing. Can't hold on forever.
- B-Sides locked behind hidden cassette tapes (discovery-gated content).
- "One more try" compulsion from short, intense rooms.

**CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity (7/10)**
- Hidden cassettes in each chapter. Secret rooms with lore.
- B-Side/C-Side unlock surprises ("There's MORE?!").
- Farewell chapter discovery (post-credits content).
- But: each room is deterministic once you see it. No randomisation.

**CD8: Loss & Avoidance (2/10)**
- **REVOLUTIONARY SUPPRESSION OF LOSS.** This is Celeste's defining design choice.
- No "Game Over" screen. No lives. No punishment. Instant respawn.
- No lost progress. Checkpoints every single room (10-30 seconds of play max).
- No score that can decrease. No currency that can be spent/lost.
- No judgment language anywhere in the UI.
- Death counter framed POSITIVELY ("be proud").
- Assist Mode exists without shame or hidden-ness.
- Result: players feel empowered by difficulty, not terrorised by it.

### The Celeste Loop
```
Attempt room → Die (instant) → Respawn (instant) → 
Try different approach → Die → Learn → Succeed → 
Feel genuine pride → Next room (slightly harder) → REPEAT
```

### The Badeline Parallel (CRITICAL for Vibe Rise)

Badeline is the single most relevant game character for Vibe Rise's design thesis.

| Badeline | Vibe Rise Protective Voice |
|---|---|
| Dark reflection of Madeline | Dark reflection of user |
| Tries to PROTECT by preventing action | Tries to PROTECT by preventing courage |
| Dialogue: "I'm worried about us" | Dialogue: "You're not ready for that" |
| Motivation: genuine fear, not malice | Motivation: genuine fear, not malice |
| Madeline's instinct: fight/reject her | User's instinct: fight/reject the voice |
| Chapter 6 lesson: ACCEPT, don't fight | Healing flow lesson: recognise, don't fight |
| Acceptance = double dash (more capacity) | Integration = Vibe Rise state (more capacity) |
| Rejection = fall to bottom of mountain | Rejection = stuck in head (paralysis zone) |

**The genius:** Celeste delivers this lesson THROUGH MECHANICS, not exposition. Madeline literally gains a double-dash (doubled capacity) by accepting Badeline. The player FEELS the difference in their hands. Vibe Rise should deliver the same insight through EXPERIENCE, not explanation.

### What Vibe Rise Should STEAL from Celeste

1. **"Be proud of your death count."** Reframe setbacks as data. "You've been in Sympathetic state 4 times this week. That means you're pushing your edges. That IS the work."

2. **Assist Mode without shame.** Not everyone can do every challenge. Let users scale difficulty WITHOUT hiding it or making them feel less-than. "Adjust your pace. There's no wrong speed."

3. **The acceptance > rejection thesis.** The Healing Flow's core message (recognise the protective voice, don't fight it) should be felt mechanically, not just read. When users complete the integration, they should gain VISIBLE new capacity (unlock, ability, access).

4. **Instant retry, zero punishment.** If a user "fails" a wahoo (reports Pressure/Uninterested), there should be ZERO friction to trying again. No cooldown, no shame screen, no "you failed" message. Just: "Want to try a different one?"

5. **Dash as limited energy metaphor.** The Capacity Score already maps to this. When your capacity is low (post-drain, sympathetic state), you literally HAVE fewer "dashes" available. The app should reflect this: fewer challenges offered, gentler options surfaced.

6. **Strawberries: optional proof of courage.** Create challenges that have NO required function but exist purely for users who want to prove something to themselves. Golden Strawberry = "Complete 30 days without missing a single check-in." No reward except knowing you did it.

7. **Chapter 6 as design template for the Healing Flow.** The 7-step healing flow should have a "Chapter 6 moment" where the user is guided to say something like "It's okay that you're scared. Let's work together." To their own protective voice. And after that moment, something tangible unlocks.

### What Vibe Rise Should AVOID from Celeste
1. **The solo experience.** Celeste is powerful but lonely. Vibe Rise needs the social warmth of Hades' House alongside Celeste's empowerment.
2. **Over-reliance on intrinsic motivation.** Works for a $20 game people chose to buy. An app needs SOME extrinsic hooks to establish the daily habit before intrinsic meaning takes over.

## Comparative: Which Model for Which Phase?

The three models map to different phases of a user's journey:

| User Phase | Best Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1-2** (habit formation) | Duolingo | Need extrinsic hooks to establish the daily pattern. Streaks, points, quick wins. |
| **Week 3-8** (deepening) | Hades | Setbacks start happening. Need "death feeds progress" reframe. Relationships deepen. |
| **Month 3+** (integration) | Celeste | User is now self-motivated. Protective voice work becomes central. Meaning > mechanics. |

**The ideal Vibe Rise arc:**
- **Onboarding** uses Duolingo patterns (clear path, immediate rewards, streak establishment)
- **Scaffolding** uses Hades patterns (failures become content, Zarlo deepens, builds vary weekly)
- **Endgame** uses Celeste patterns (self-determined challenge, acceptance thesis, intrinsic pride)

## Key Learnings (Synthesised)

### From Duolingo: The Habit Machine

**Duolingo's actual product IS the streak, not the language learning.** The streak is what users talk about, protect, photograph, and share. The language is the excuse for the streak to exist.

Applied to Vibe Rise: what if the STREAK is the product, and the healing/growth is "just" what happens along the way? Users come back for the streak. They stay for the transformation. You don't need to sell "heal your inner child." You sell "keep your fire going."

**Journey-stage calibration.** Duolingo doesn't show you leagues on Day 1. It waits until you have enough investment to care. Vibe Rise's features should unlock based on TENURE, not just completion. A user at Day 45 needs different mechanics than Day 3.

### From Hades: Failure as the Curriculum

Hades proves that **the "bad" outcome can be the MORE rewarding path.** Dying in Hades gives you MORE content than surviving. This completely inverts the punishment model.

For Vibe Rise: a user who checks in as "Shutdown" should get RICHER content than one who checks in as "Vibe Rise." Not because we want them to be Shutdown, but because that's when they need the most, and that's when the deepest insights live. The app should feel WARMEST when you're at your lowest. Like coming home to the House of Hades.

**Relationship-as-progression:** Zarlo shouldn't be a static chatbot. Zarlo should have TRUST LEVELS that unlock over weeks/months of interaction. At Trust Level 1, Zarlo asks surface questions. At Trust Level 5, Zarlo says things like "I notice you always avoid vulnerability challenges after Tuesday evening calls with your parents. Want to talk about that?" The relationship deepens through ACCUMULATED CONTEXT, not just time.

**The "NPC social layer" insight:** You don't need real users to have a 9/10 social score. Hades is single-player. Its entire social drive comes from AI characters who remember, react, and evolve. Zarlo + the protective voices + the archetypes CAN be the social layer, if they feel alive enough.

### From Celeste: The Wellness Game Already Exists

Celeste is basically Vibe Rise in platformer form:

- Badeline = Protective Voice (same psychology, same arc, same lesson)
- The mountain = The Quest (external challenge as mirror for internal work)
- Double-dash after acceptance = Vibe Rise state after integration (more capacity)
- Death counter = Days in Sympathetic state (data, not failure)
- Assist Mode = Adjustable challenge intensity (no shame)
- B-Sides = Optional deeper work for those who want it

**The deepest learning:** Celeste delivers its therapeutic message through MECHANICS, not words. The player doesn't READ about acceptance. They EXPERIENCE doubled capacity the moment they accept Badeline. Their hands feel the difference. One dash becomes two.

Vibe Rise's Healing Flow should do this. When someone completes the integration step (Step 5: Rewire), something should TANGIBLY change in the app. Not just a message. A visible, felt shift. Maybe Zarlo's tone changes. Maybe a locked area opens. Maybe their Capacity Score jumps. The app needs its own "double-dash moment."

### The Meta-Learning Across All Five Sources

The through-line across DCC, RPO, Duolingo, Hades, and Celeste:

**What keeps people coming back is NOT the mechanics. It's the FEELING the mechanics create.**

- DCC's mechanics create the feeling of being SURPRISED and RESOURCEFUL
- RPO's mechanics create the feeling of being SMART and VALIDATED
- Duolingo's mechanics create the feeling of being CONSISTENT and ACCOUNTABLE
- Hades' mechanics create the feeling of being WELCOMED and CURIOUS
- Celeste's mechanics create the feeling of being CAPABLE and SEEN

**Vibe Rise's mechanics should create the feeling of being BRAVE and UNDERSTOOD.**

That's the north star. Every feature, every notification, every celebration should ask: "Does this make the user feel brave? Does this make them feel understood?" If the answer is yes to both, ship it.
