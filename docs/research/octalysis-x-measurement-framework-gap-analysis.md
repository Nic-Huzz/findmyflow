## The Two Systems

| | Measurement Framework | Octalysis Research |
|---|---|---|
| **Asks** | Where is the user? What's blocking them? | How does the user FEEL? What pulls them back? |
| **Strength** | Diagnosis (stage, depth, protective voice) | Experience (celebration, curiosity, social warmth) |
| **Structure** | 12 hero stages + L0-L4 depth + 5×5 matrix | 8 Core Drives + 3-phase model + specific mechanics |
| **Metaphor** | The SKELETON (structure and progression) | The SKIN (how it feels to touch) |

Neither is complete without the other.

## Strong Alignments (5 items — already fulfilled)

### 1. "Story requires failure" → Stage 7 (Protective Voice Identified)

**Octalysis source:** Hades (death feeds story), recommendation #9
**Framework match:** Stage 6→7 graduation requires "one protective voice identified 5 times across healing flows + wahoo patterns."

This IS the Hades model. Setbacks create the data that unlocks the deepest insight. The framework already requires accumulated failure as the graduation trigger. Clean alignment.

### 2. "Self-knowledge = keys" → L0-L4 Depth Scale

**Octalysis source:** RPO (knowledge unlocks power)
**Framework match:** Depth scale (L0 Education → L4 Teaching) per curiosity.

Your depth of self-knowledge about each life path IS the progression system. The deeper you go, the more you unlock. Depth = power. Already aligned.

### 3. "Acceptance = double-dash" → Stage 8→9 Transition

**Octalysis source:** Celeste Chapter 6 (acceptance of Badeline doubles capacity)
**Framework match:** Stage 8 (Reconsolidation of root trauma) → Stage 9 (Flow Statement emerges)

Stage 8 is Celeste's Chapter 6: you accept the wound. Stage 9 is the double-dash: you gain new capacity (the Flow Statement, a clear sense of direction that wasn't possible before). The structure is there. What's missing is the FELT experience of that moment (see Gap 1).

### 4. "Dash = limited energy" → Depth × Visibility Mismatch Detection

**Octalysis source:** Celeste (limited dash as energy metaphor)
**Framework match:** The mismatch table in Section 4.

| Position | What it means | Protective Voice |
|---|---|---|
| High Depth, Low Visibility | "It's part of my life but nobody knows" | Ghost |
| Low Depth, High Visibility | "I claim this but barely do it" | Performer |
| Sharing but can't reach Money | "People know me for this but I can't charge" | People Pleaser |
| Can't move from L1 to L2 | "I tried once but can't commit" | Controller |
| Sharing but can't reach Vulnerable | "I do it publicly but not imperfectly" | Perfectionist |

This IS the "limited dash" mechanic applied to courage. You can only stretch in so many directions at once. The matrix shows where your energy is going and where it's being avoided. Excellent alignment.

### 5. "Context-sensitive reactions" → Figurine Journey-Aware Speech

**Octalysis source:** Hades (Hypnos comments on how you died), recommendation #5
**Framework match:** Section 7 figurine speech examples.

"Stage 3: I notice you've been browsing but haven't tried anything yet. That's the Refusal." This IS Hades' context-sensitive NPC dialogue. Good start, needs expansion (see Partial Alignment #1).

## Partial Alignments (3 items — need tweaks)

### 6. Zarlo Personality → Figurine Design (Section 7)

**Octalysis source:** Hades (Hypnos), DCC (Donut), recommendation #7
**Framework state:** Figurine has journey-aware speech and mirror→mentor progression.
**What's missing:** The OPINIONATED companion. The figurine is described as observational ("I notice...") but not challenging. Donut argues. Hypnos teases. Mordecai whispers cryptic warnings.

**Tweak:** Add a "challenge" mode to figurine speech. Not just "Here's where you are" but:
- "You've been at L1 Testing in breathwork for 3 months. The Controller is keeping you from L2. Do you see it?"
- "You marked Safe today. Your Tune tab says Sympathetic. What happened?"
- "You keep creating Screen wahoos. When's the last time you went Live?"

The figurine should occasionally DISAGREE with the user's self-assessment. That's what makes it feel alive.

### 7. "No Wasted Runs" → Graduation Triggers

**Octalysis source:** Hades (every run yields something), recommendation #8
**Framework state:** All graduation triggers are BINARY (did/didn't). No reward for partial progress.
**What's missing:** A user stuck at Stage 3 for months gets nothing. No acknowledgment, no interim rewards.

**Tweak:** Add INTERIM milestones between stages:
- "You've identified 3 of 5 protective voice patterns. Two more and something will become clear." (Stage 6→7)
- "You've completed 2 of 4 Fantasy League weeks. Keep going." (Stage 5→6)
- Award RP for progress TOWARD graduation, not just the graduation itself.
- Even failed wahoos should yield something: "+2 RP for honesty" when checking in as Shutdown.

### 8. Wahoo Creation Cleanup → Zero Punishment for Failure

**Octalysis source:** Celeste (no punishment on death), recommendation #4
**Framework state:** Section 8 removes scary/wahoo scores (correct, aligns with Celeste).
**What's missing:** Post-completion "How did that feel?" responses for Pressure/Uninterested aren't specified.

**Tweak:** Define the response per state:
- **Vibe Rise:** Gold celebration + identity statement prompt. (Already exists.)
- **Fun:** Warm acknowledgment. "That landed. Good."
- **Pressure:** "That wasn't fun. It wasn't supposed to be. You did it anyway. +3 RP for pushing your edge."
- **Uninterested:** "This one didn't land. Good data. Let's find what does. Want to try a different one?"

Zero judgment. Every outcome yields insight + RP. No state is "failure."

## Gaps (7 items — new additions needed)

### Gap 1: How Are Hero Stage Graduations CELEBRATED?

**Problem:** The framework defines WHEN you graduate (triggers). It doesn't define HOW that moment FEELS.

**What Octalysis says:** DCC's boss reveals (screen freeze, mugshots, music, dramatic description) turn every significant moment into a cinematic event. Celeste's Chapter 6 delivers its thesis through a FELT mechanical change (double-dash), not just dialogue.

**Recommendation:** Each stage graduation gets a signature moment:

| Stage | Graduation Celebration |
|---|---|
| 3→4 (Mirror) | Essence avatar appears for the first time. The archetype is named. "You've been called this your whole life without knowing it." |
| 4→5 (First Vibe Rise) | Screen pulses gold. Zarlo: "There it is. You felt it. Remember this next time the voice gets loud." |
| 5→6 (Daily Loop) | Fantasy League invitation. "You're ready for the arena. Time to train with others." |
| 6→7 (Pattern Revealed) | Screen dims to protective voice colour. Voice silhouette appears. "The [Perfectionist]. You've met it 5 times now. It's been running your show since [origin]. You're ready to face the root." |
| 7→8 (Ordeal) | Calendly integration. "This one needs a human. Not an app. Book the session." |
| 8→9 (Flow Statement) | Convergence animation. Life paths visually merge. "Your curiosities share something. There it is." New capability unlocks (the "double-dash"). |
| 9→10 (Aligned Action) | Path selection (A/B/C). "Three doors. All lead forward. None lead back." |

Each should feel like a moment the user screenshots and shares.

### Gap 2: What Happens When You're STUCK?

**Problem:** The framework has graduation triggers but no "stuck" mechanics. Someone at Stage 3 for 4 months gets no special content. Silence = abandonment.

**What Octalysis says:** Hades' story ONLY advances when you die. Stuckness should unlock MORE content, not less. DCC's Mordecai drops cryptic wisdom that creates open loops.

**Recommendation:** Stuckness triggers escalating Zarlo engagement:

| Duration Stuck | Zarlo Response | Octalysis Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2 weeks | Acknowledges. "You've been here a while. That's not wrong. The Refusal is part of the journey." | Celeste (no shame) |
| 4 weeks | Gets curious. "I've been looking at your patterns. The thing you're avoiding isn't what you think it is." | Hades (death feeds story) |
| 8 weeks | Drops a cryptic hook. "There's something you haven't tried. It's in the Courage tab. But you're not ready to hear that yet." | DCC (Mordecai) |
| 12 weeks | Direct. "Want to know what the 5×5 shows? Your [life path] is at L2 depth but you've never gone Vulnerable. That's the gap." | Hades (context-sensitive) |

Stuckness is NOT failure. It's a different type of content delivery. The longer you're stuck, the richer Zarlo becomes.

**NOTE:** These generic timings (2/4/8/12 weeks) were SUPERSEDED by per-stage calibrated timings in `docs/features/octalysis-alignment-implementation-notes.md` Section 9 (Gap 2). The spec doc is the source of truth for stuck thresholds.

### Gap 3: Social Validation Layer is Absent

**Problem:** The 5×5 matrix, depth scale, and hero stages are all INDIVIDUAL measurements. CD5 Social is Vibe Rise's biggest gap (4/10). No mention of how others witness, validate, or participate in your journey.

**What Octalysis says:** Strava's one-tap Kudos (low friction, high impact). Peloton's "Here Now" counter (ambient community). Hades' NPC relationships (social without multiplayer). DCC's viewer system (feeling witnessed).

**Recommendation:** Add a social dimension to key transitions:

| Stage | Social Element |
|---|---|
| 5 (First Vibe Rise) | Option to share the moment. Community Kudos. |
| 6 (Daily Loop) | Fantasy League IS the social container. Frame explicitly as hero stage. |
| 7 (Pattern Revealed) | "3 other people identified the same protective voice this month." Anonymous solidarity. |
| 8 (Ordeal) | Human-facilitated session (already social). Post-session option to share reflection. |
| 9 (Flow Statement) | Share with community for Kudos/validation. "12 people resonated with your Flow Statement." |
| 12 (First Graduate) | Inherently social (testimonial). The measurement IS social proof. |

Plus ambient features:
- "Here Now" counter: "[N] people are in the app right now." (Note: needs a minimum threshold. "2 people are in the app" feels sad, not communal. Consider showing only when N > 10, or use cumulative: "47 people used the app today.")
- One-tap Kudos on shared reflections and wahoos.
- Anonymous stat sharing: "34 people completed a Vibe Rise wahoo today. You were one of them."

### Gap 4: Feeling Targets Per Stage

**Problem:** Each hero stage has a metric target but no FEELING target. The framework measures WHERE; it doesn't address how the user FEELS.

**What Octalysis says:** The north star is "BRAVE and UNDERSTOOD." Every feature should ask: does this make the user feel brave? Does this make them feel understood?

**Recommendation:** Add a feeling target alongside each metric:

| Stage | Metric Target | Feeling Target |
|---|---|---|
| 2. Earthquake | Account + first check-in | "Something just shifted. I can't go back." |
| 3. Head Full of Dreams | Life paths complete | "I can see it but I can't reach it." |
| 4. Mirror/Mentor | Essence complete + avatar | "I feel so SEEN. I have words for this now." |
| 5. First Vibe Rise | Wahoo = Vibe Rise state | "I didn't know I could feel this alive." |
| 6. Daily Loop | Fantasy League season | "I'm actually doing it. Every day." |
| 7. Pattern Revealed | Voice identified 5x | "Oh. THAT'S what's been stopping me." |
| 8. Ordeal | Root reconsolidation | "That hurt. But something released." |
| 9. Flow Statement | AI merge + confirmation | "Of course. This was always my path." |
| 10. Aligned Action | Career decision | "I'm doing the thing. For real." |
| 11. Structural Commitment | Life restructures | "There's no going back now." |
| 12. First Graduate | Someone transforms | "It worked for someone else. It's real." |

These feeling targets inform celebration copy, Zarlo's tone, visual treatment, and success criteria. A user might hit the metric without hitting the feeling (completing Essence Mirror but not feeling "seen"). The feeling target tells us the EXPERIENCE isn't complete yet.

### Gap 5: The 5×5 Matrix Should Be Partially Visible

**Problem:** Section 4 says the user never sees the grid. The full analysis stays hidden with Zarlo. But DCC shows that SOME transparency feeds CD4 (ownership) and CD2 (accomplishment). Carl can see his stats, skills, and inventory.

**What Octalysis says:** Ownership requires seeing what you own. Accomplishment requires visible progress. Hiding everything removes two core drives.

**Recommendation:** Show a simplified surface layer per life path:
- "Breathwork: L2 Practising. You've gone Screen and Live. Money, Vulnerable, and Authority are unexplored."
- Visual: 5 dots per life path, filled or empty. Tap for detail.
- The DETAILED analysis (mismatch → protective voice detection) stays hidden with Zarlo.
- The simplified view is the "stat screen" the user can check. The hidden analysis is the AI doing its work behind the scenes.

This gives users a sense of progress and ownership while keeping the diagnostic intelligence private.

### Gap 6: No Curiosity/Mystery Mechanics in the Framework

**Problem:** The framework is logical and structured (its strength as a measurement system). But the Octalysis research's biggest finding is that CD7 (Curiosity) should be the daily engagement engine. The framework has no deliberate mystery elements.

**What Octalysis says:** DCC's achievement descriptions, mystery locks, and mentor cryptic hooks. Hades' randomised boons per run. Celeste's hidden cassettes.

**Recommendation:** Add mystery elements to the framework:
- **Locked stages:** Show silhouette + hook, not just lock icon. "Level 7: Something is keeping you stuck. You're not ready to see what yet."
- **Graduation approaching:** Zarlo drops a teaser. "Something's about to change. You're one pattern away."
- **5×5 fog of war:** Unexplored cells in the matrix reveal themselves as you attempt new visibility layers. Like DCC's minimap.
- **Flow Statement tease:** From Stage 6 onward: "Your curiosities share something. I can almost see it." Gets clearer each week.
- **Randomised weekly offerings (Hades boons):** "This week's focus: Vulnerability + Rest. Choose your balance." Different emphasis each week, not the same routine.

### Gap 7: The Live Session Bridge Isn't Addressed

**Problem:** The measurement framework defines app features and hero stages but doesn't describe how weekly sessions connect to the digital layer.

**What Octalysis says:** The live experience apps research (NRC, Peloton, Zombies Run!) defines three bridges: pre-session (anticipation), post-session (visibility), between sessions (connection).

**Recommendation:** Add session measurement to the framework:

| Session Element | Measurement | Hero Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter number (per person) | Session attendance count | All |
| Pre-session intention | Text capture in app before session | All |
| Post-session courage score | Self-reported 1-10 stretch | 5-8 |
| Milestone celebration | Nth session announced to group | All |
| Loyalty tier | Cumulative attendance (Spark→Inferno) | All |
| Group wahoo completion | Binary (done in session together) | 5-6 |
| Post-session reflection | Text capture in app within 24 hours | All |

Session data should feed hero stage progression. Attending sessions = Stage 6 training evidence. Post-session reflections = data for Stage 7 pattern detection. Session courage scores = Capacity Score input.

## Octalysis Phase Model × Hero Stages

The three-phase model from the daily engagement analysis maps cleanly:

| Octalysis Phase | Model | Hero Stages | Engagement Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1-2** (Habit Formation) | Duolingo | Stages 2-4 (Awakening) | Clear path, immediate rewards, streak establishment, quick wins |
| **Week 3-8** (Deepening) | Hades | Stages 5-7 (Training → Pattern) | Setbacks become content, Zarlo deepens, builds vary weekly, relationships grow |
| **Month 3+** (Integration) | Celeste | Stages 8-12 (Ordeal → Return) | Self-determined challenge, acceptance thesis, meaning over mechanics, intrinsic pride |

This means:
- Stages 2-4 should feel EASY and REWARDING (Duolingo energy)
- Stages 5-7 should feel WARM and CURIOUS (Hades energy)
- Stages 8-12 should feel MEANINGFUL and SELF-DIRECTED (Celeste energy)

The TONE of the app should shift as the user progresses through hero stages.

## Additional Note: Pre-Wahoo Capture as CD3 (Creativity)

The measurement framework's "Pre-Wahoo Capture" flow (Section 4) — "Where are you with this?" (depth) + "What part pushes your boundary?" (visibility multi-select) — IS a creativity mechanic. The user makes meaningful choices about WHAT to attempt and WHERE to stretch. This partially addresses the CD3 gap already. The Octalysis recommendation for "randomised weekly offerings" would complement this by adding variety to the options surfaced.
