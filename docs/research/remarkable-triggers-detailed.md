# Remarkable Triggers — Detailed Evidence Per Creator
*Working notes. April 2026. Confidence ratings per entry.*

---

## Updated Definitions

**Rule Broken:** Challenging a cultural standard or norm in your field. Not just doing something different. Changing the STANDARD of what's acceptable or expected. The rule break becomes the movement.

**Unexpected Combination:** Two domains, methods, or identities brought together that the field hasn't seen combined. Often the combination IS what reveals the rule that needs breaking.

**Extreme Action:** A specific visible behavior so far beyond normal that it becomes the thing people talk about. Often the extreme action IS the rule break made visible (Dawnbreaker example: the rule break is "sobriety can be social and fun" and the extreme action IS "sober dance party at 5am").

**Stupid Simplicity:** A conclusion or method so simple it feels insulting, arrived at through years of depth.

**Impossible Result:** Evidence (data, scale, outcome) that defies what the field believed was possible.

## Summary: Extreme Action ↔ Rule Breaking Connection

| Connection type | Creators | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Inseparable** (extreme action IS the rule break) | Wim Hof, Myles Horton, Tenzin Palmo, Esther Perel | The ongoing practice IS the norm violation. Every repetition breaks the rule again. |
| **Same moment** (one event is both) | Lin-Manuel Miranda, Glennon Doyle, Dave Ramsey, Tony Robbins | The project/life event is both audacious and norm-violating. |
| **Action provides evidence for rule break** | John Gottman, Phil Jackson | The extreme setup (Love Lab, meditation in NBA) proves the rule wrong through results. |
| **Separate** (rule break exists without extreme action) | Brene Brown, Marie Forleo, James Clear, Simon Sinek, Adriene Mishler, Priya Parker | Rule break is conceptual or quiet. Remarkability comes from simplicity, combination, or results instead. |
| **Sequential** (extreme action preceded rule break) | Jay Shetty, Gabor Mate, Elizabeth Gilbert, Holly Whitaker | Extreme experience happened first, rule break emerged from it later. |

**Key finding:** 9 of 20 have inseparable or same-moment connection between extreme action and rule breaking. 5 of 20 have separate paths (rule break without extreme action). 4 of 20 are sequential. 2 provide evidence.

**This means extreme action is NOT required for remarkability, but when present it almost always serves the rule break.** The Dawnbreaker pattern (extreme action = rule break made visible) is the strongest form, but not the only form.
