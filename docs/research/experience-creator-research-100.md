# Experience Creator Research: 100 Profiles

Status: Phase 1 complete (84 profiles). Phase 2 in progress (borderline + peaked/stalled expansion).
Related: `experienceCreatorDNA.json` (32 in-app profiles), `docs/blow-up-brand-implementation-plan.md`

## The 3-Dimension Model

Every creator who sustained their blow-up built all three dimensions. The question isn't "which type are you?" but "which dimension are you missing?"

| Dimension | What it means |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Create** | Made something new. A new category of experience. |
| **Capture** | Got the work seen. Content, social, podcast, press. |
| **Portable** | Method works without them. Book, framework, certification. |

Bridge Amplification is a flag (not a dimension): a bigger platform/person amplified them at a key moment.

---

## Key Findings Across 84 Profiles

### 1. The model holds
Every sustained creator eventually built all 3 dimensions. Stalled creators are missing at least one.

### 2. Portability is the differentiator
- Personality-dependent brands (Adriene Mishler, Kino MacGregor, Rachel Brathen, Jesse Israel) score weak on Portable. When the person stalls, everything stalls.
- Method-dependent brands (Byron Katie, Marisa Peer, Ana Forrest, Baron Baptiste, Wim Hof) score strong on Portable. The method outlives the founder.

### 3. Social media compresses timelines
- Pre-social creators: avg ~30 years to blow-up
- Post-2015 creators: avg ~5 years
- 9D Breathwork, Dawnbreak, Nicole LePera, Jesse Israel: 3-4 years

### 4. Bridge amplification is common
Most common bridges: platform algorithms (YouTube, Instagram), Mindvalley, Oprah, festival circuits, corporate wellness trend.

### 5. Peaked/Stalled has 5 distinct causes
1. Sexual misconduct / criminal (Bikram, John Friend, Russell Brand)
2. Conspiracy radicalization (Guru Jagat, Russell Brand)
3. Personality without method (Tara Stiles, Preston Smiles, Rachel Brathen, Gabby Reece)
4. Voluntary evolution (Danielle LaPorte, Mastin Kipp)
5. Platform/geography dependency (Tara Stiles, Rachel Brathen)

### 6. Closest proof points to Vibe Rise
- **9D Breathwork**: Facilitator-as-SaaS. Pre-created sessions, facilitators hold space. 2,000+ in 80+ countries in 3-4 years.
- **Sanctum**: Headphones-as-container. Amsterdam to Auberge Resorts in 3 years. App with felt-state tracking.
- **Dawnbreak Australia**: 5am sober silent disco. 35 to 13,000 in 2 years. Category creation through constraint stacking.

## Peaked / Stalled (10 profiles)

### Tara Stiles (Strala Yoga)
- **Type**: Yoga
- **Peak**: ~2013. NYT "rebel yoga" profile, Deepak collaboration, W Hotels, multiple books.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~4
- **What happened**: Intense backlash from yoga community for stripping spirituality. Studio closed. Shifted online-only.
- **Stall cause**: Community rejection + niche ceiling + platform dependency. "Easy yoga without spirituality" is a POSITION, not a METHOD. Yoga establishment actively worked against her.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=starting, Portable=starting
- **Missing**: Differentiated methodology. No "Strala teachers" movement.

### Mastin Kipp (Daily Love)
- **Type**: Motivational / coaching
- **Peak**: ~2013. Kim Kardashian tweet, Oprah Super Soul Sunday, massive email list.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~3
- **What happened**: Pivoted from Daily Love to Functional Life Coaching. Less visible but more substantive.
- **Stall cause**: Voluntary pivot from commoditized genre. Daily Love was content platform in genre that got saturated. Chose depth over breadth.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=strong, Portable=not_yet
- **Missing**: Portable methodology at peak. Brand equity couldn't transfer to new direction.

### Preston Smiles (Love Louder)
- **Type**: Motivational speaking
- **Peak**: ~2016. Viral YouTube, Simon & Schuster book deal, Lewis Howes/Tom Bilyeu podcasts.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~3
- **What happened**: Inspirational YouTube wave ended. Pivoted to couples coaching with wife Alexi Panos.
- **Stall cause**: Genre collapse. "Love louder" is a vibe, not a framework. When viral era ended, nothing proprietary underneath.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=starting, Portable=not_yet
- **Missing**: Any portable methodology. Entire brand was energy and charisma on camera.

### Guru Jagat (RA MA Institute)
- **Type**: Kundalini yoga
- **Peak**: ~2018. THE celebrity Kundalini studio in Venice. Fashion + music expansion.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~5
- **What happened**: Defended accused guru Yogi Bhajan. Spread conspiracy theories (QAnon, Pizzagate). @ramawrong documented abuse. Died age 41 (2021). HBO "Breath of Fire" documented rise and fall.
- **Stall cause**: Conspiracy radicalization + guru model collapse. Didn't own the methodology (Kundalini is Bhajan's). When his legacy collapsed, she had no IP. Guru model = no governance, no accountability.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=starting, Portable=not_yet
- **Missing**: Own methodology + governance structure.

### Bikram Choudhury (Bikram Yoga)
- **Type**: Hot yoga
- **Peak**: ~2006. 1,500-1,650 studios in 40+ countries. $4.15M per teacher training cohort.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~30
- **What happened**: Multiple rape accusations (2011-2013). $7M+ judgment. Fled US. Arrest warrant. Bankruptcy.
- **Stall cause**: Serial sexual predation. Full stop. Analytically: the PRACTICE survived because it was genuinely portable. Studios renamed to "hot yoga" and kept teaching. Method outlived the monster.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=strong, Portable=strong
- **Missing**: Ethics. Governance. Had all 3 business dimensions. Only criminal behavior broke it.

### John Friend (Anusara Yoga)
- **Type**: Yoga certification
- **Peak**: ~2010. Fastest-growing yoga style. ~1,500 certified teachers, ~600,000 practitioners.
- **Years to blow-up**: ~13
- **What happened**: 2012: Sexual misconduct, financial fraud, Wiccan coven. Lied when caught. 90% teacher attrition. Tried Bowspring rebuild — never recovered.
- **Stall cause**: Misconduct + lying when caught. The LYING is key. Some leaders survive scandal through transparency; Friend minimized. Method too intertwined with his philosophy to survive.
- **At peak**: Create=strong, Capture=strong, Portable=strong
- **Missing**: Ethics. Unlike Bikram, method didn't survive because it was philosophically entwined with his character.

### Rachel Brathen (Yoga Girl)
- **Type**: Instagram yoga / retreats
- **Peak**: ~2017. 2M+ followers. Bestselling book. Island Yoga studio.
