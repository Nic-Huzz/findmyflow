# Zone Diagnosis Image Prompts

Pixar-style scene prompts for each level's zone diagnosis. 3 images per level (top left, diagonal, bottom right). Same format as onboarding scene cards. Each level uses a different adult character for diversity.

---

## Level 1: Identity Sweet Spot
**Character: Woman, early 30s, South Asian, long dark hair, warm brown eyes**
**Y axis: Authenticity | X axis: Belonging**

### Top Left — Outcast Zone
```
Pixar-style 3D animated illustration of a woman in her early 30s, South Asian with long dark hair, standing alone on a stage at an open mic night, speaking her truth boldly into a microphone. The audience looks uncomfortable, some turning away, some whispering. She's fully authentic but the room is rejecting it. Her expression is brave but isolated. She chose herself and lost the room. Warm spotlight on her, cold blue tones in the audience. Horizontal landscape orientation, 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no words, no watermark.
```

### Diagonal — Identity Sweet Spot
```
Pixar-style 3D animated illustration of a woman in her early 30s, South Asian with long dark hair, at a casual dinner table with friends, laughing mid-sentence while gesturing expressively. Everyone is engaged, leaning in, genuinely delighted by her. She's being completely herself and the group loves it. No performance. No filtering. Just her, accepted. Warm golden candlelight, cozy restaurant setting. Horizontal landscape orientation, 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no words, no watermark.
```

### Bottom Right — Chameleon Zone
```
Pixar-style 3D animated illustration of a woman in her early 30s, South Asian with long dark hair now styled conservatively, at a corporate networking event. She's smiling perfectly, mirroring the energy of the group, dressed exactly like everyone else in business attire. Popular, included, fitting in seamlessly. But her eyes have a subtle emptiness, a flicker of "is this me?" She belongs completely but at the cost of herself. Cool professional lighting, blues and grays. Horizontal landscape orientation, 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no words, no watermark.
```
