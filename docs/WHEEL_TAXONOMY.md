# Competence Wheels Taxonomy

Three wheels that map a user's "Founder DNA":
- **Skills Wheel**: What capabilities you bring
- **Problems Wheel**: What problems resonate with you
- **Personas Wheel**: Who you're drawn to serve

## How They Connect

A user's Founder DNA is the intersection of:
1. **Skills** they bring (what they're good at)
2. **Problems** they care about (what they want to solve)
3. **Personas** they serve (who they help)

Example: A user with `clarifying` skills solving `mental_wellbeing` problems for `seekers` might be described as:
> "The Translator Mind Guardian - A Translator who helps seekers restore inner peace."

## Flow Questions

### Skills Flow (5 Questions)

The Skills flow collects "dots" from different life periods, then AI clusters them into skill themes.

| # | Question | Prompt |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Childhood** | "Thinking back to Pre-school & Primary: What did you love doing most? What activities did you gravitate towards during free-time? The weekend? Holidays?" |
| 2 | **High School** | "What did you enjoy doing most? What extra-curricular activities did you do? Any subjects or assignments that you loved? What did you do on weekends and after school for fun?" |
| 3 | **Post-School** | "What activities, projects or creative outlets do you enjoy the most?" (After school, before full-time work) |
| 4 | **Work** | "Across your jobs, projects, or creative pursuits, what have you enjoyed doing most? Think of times you felt in flow or energized by what you were doing." |
| 5 | **Skills** | "What skills have you loved to develop? Think about skills you've intentionally worked on — through courses, practice, or personal curiosity." |

**Final Step - Rate Proficiency:**
For items from Q3-Q5, user rates each: *"Could I confidently teach this to someone else?"*
- **Emerging** - Still learning
- **Establishing** - Can do well
- **Mastering** - Could teach it

---

### Problems Flow (7 Questions)

The Problems flow explores learning interests, life chapters, struggles, and future vision to discover problem themes.

| # | Question | Prompt |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Topics** | "What topics have you loved learning about? What are the topics of your favourite non-fiction books or podcasts? What feels like fun to learn about?" |
| 2 | **Impact** | "What impact have you enjoyed making for others? What difference have you made? How have you helped others?" |
| 3 | **Chapters** | "If you saw your life as a story, what are the chapters? Think of major phases or turning points in your journey." |
| 4 | **Struggles** | "For each chapter, what struggle did you face?" (Pre-filled with chapter names from Q3) |
| 5 | **Role Models** | "Who has inspired you the most? Include both the person and why they're meaningful to you." |
| 6 | **Future** | "What do you feel called to create, experience, or change? What impact do you want to make? What do you want to exist?" |
| 7 | **Pulls** | "What are your top 3 future pulls? From everything you shared, what feels most energizing?" |

**Final Step - Rate Problem Themes:**
For each AI-generated problem cluster, user rates their stage:
- **Exploring** - New opportunity, curious but haven't pursued yet
- **Pursuing** - Currently working on this problem
- **Proven** - Previously pursued, have results and experience

---

### Persona Flow (AI-Generated)

The Persona flow **does not ask questions**. Instead, it analyzes the user's Problems clusters to identify personas they're qualified to serve.

**Key Insight:** *"You're most qualified to help former versions of yourself. The struggles you've overcome, the growth you've experienced — that's your superpower."*

**What It Analyzes:**
- Life chapters and struggles
- Role models and their impact
- Learning topics and interests
- Impact created
- Future vision and desires

**Final Step - Rate Journey Stage:**
For each AI-generated persona, user rates where they are on their journey:
- **Awakening** - Just realized they have this problem
- **Struggling** - Actively trying to solve, hitting walls
- **Ready** - Have budget, urgency, seeking solution
