# CATEGORY 1: CORE DISCOVERY FLOWS

## 1.1 Nervous System Map

**ROUTE:** `/nervous-system`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
This is where we catch your nervous system in the act. Through some surprisingly simple body-based tests (you'll literally sway), we find the invisible line where your system says "nope, too scary" — even when your brain says "let's go."

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Here's the sneaky truth: you don't rise to the level of your ambitions — you fall to what your nervous system thinks is safe. It's like having an overprotective bodyguard who won't let you into the VIP section of your own life.

This flow shows you exactly where that bouncer is standing. Once you see the line, you can start moving it.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "How does the sway test work?"
- "What's a safety contract?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: How does the sway test work?
A: Okay, this might sound a little woo-woo, but stick with me — it's actually based on muscle testing that kinesiologists have used for decades.

Here's the deal: your conscious mind only runs about 5% of the show. The other 95%? Your subconscious, running on autopilot. The sway test is a way to ask your body directly what it believes is true or safe.

Stand up, close your eyes, and say "Show me a YES." Notice which way you lean. Then say "Show me a NO." You'll feel the difference. Your body doesn't lie the way your brain does.

Q: What's a safety contract?
A: Think of it like a deal your younger self made without telling you. Something happened — maybe you got rejected, laughed at, or hurt when you put yourself out there — and your nervous system said "okay, never doing THAT again."

Now it runs in the background like invisible software:
- "If I'm too successful, people will leave me"
- "If I'm visible, I'll be attacked"
- "If I charge real money, people will think I'm greedy"

These aren't logical. They're survival code. And they've been quietly vetoing your dreams ever since. Time to renegotiate.

## 1.2 Healing Compass

**ROUTE:** `/healing-compass`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
This is where we trace a limiting belief back to its origin — the moment your nervous system first decided "this isn't safe." We're looking for the emotional splinter that's been stuck in there, quietly running the show.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
The Nervous System Map shows you WHAT beliefs are holding you back. The Healing Compass shows you WHERE they came from.

Here's the thing: you can't think your way out of a body-based response. That belief isn't in your head — it's in your nervous system. To change it, we have to go back to where it started and finally let that part of you heal.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "Do I need to have done the Nervous System Map first?"
- "Is this going to be emotionally heavy?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: Do I need to have done the Nervous System Map first?
A: Yep! The Healing Compass works with the safety contracts you identified in the NS Map. We need to know which beliefs are active before we can trace them back to their origin.

Think of it like this: NS Map = diagnosis. Healing Compass = treatment. Gotta know what we're treating first.

Q: Is this going to be emotionally heavy?
A: It can bring stuff up, yeah. That's actually part of the process — we're giving your nervous system permission to finally process what it couldn't process at the time.

Find a quiet, private space. Give yourself 15-20 minutes. And be gentle with yourself. This is deep work, not a productivity hack.

If it feels like too much, you can pause anytime. There's also the option to do this with 1:1 support if you want someone to guide you through.

## 1.5 Flow Finder: Persona

**ROUTE:** `/nikigai/persona`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
A conversation to get crystal clear on WHO you're meant to serve. Not "everyone." Not a vague "target market." Real humans with specific struggles, desires, and situations.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
"Everyone" is terrifying. Your nervous system can't relax when you're trying to please the entire internet.

But "people like Sarah, who are 18 months into their corporate burnout and desperately want out" — that's specific enough to actually approach. Clarity on WHO reduces the overwhelm of visibility.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "Do I have to pick just one type of person?"
- "What if I want to help people like my past self?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: Do I have to pick just one type of person?
A: For now, yes. I know, I know — you want to help everyone. But specificity is power.

Start with ONE persona you deeply understand (often a version of yourself). Build something real for them. You can expand later once you have momentum.

Trying to serve five different personas from the start dilutes your message and makes everything ten times harder.

Q: What if I want to help people like my past self?
A: That's often the most powerful place to start! You understand their pain intimately. You know what they're searching for at 2am because you were searching for it too.

The only danger is assuming everyone's journey is identical to yours. Use your past self as a starting point, then stay curious about how others' experiences might differ.

## 2.2 Groans Tab

**ROUTE:** `/7-day-challenge` (Groans tab)

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Groans are the things that make you go "ughhhh" — stuff you KNOW you can do, but your body is absolutely not thrilled about. Posting that thing. Sending that message. Raising your prices. You know the ones.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Here's the magic: every time you groan AND do it anyway, your nervous system learns something new. "Oh wait... we didn't die? Interesting."

That's literally how we expand. Groan by groan, your comfort zone gets bigger. It's like strength training for your visibility muscles.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What's the difference between Recognise, Rewire, and Reconnect?"
- "What if the groan feels too big?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What's the difference between Recognise, Rewire, and Reconnect?
A: **Recognise** — Notice your patterns. What protective voice showed up? What fear was underneath? This is awareness mode.

**Rewire** — Take action to create new patterns. Challenge the belief. Do the scary thing your protective voice said not to do.

**Reconnect** — Come back to your body and essence. Breathwork, movement, grounding. This is integration.

Ideally, you cycle through all three: notice the pattern, challenge it, reconnect to yourself. Rinse and repeat.

Q: What if the groan feels too big?
A: Then it's too big! We're not looking for panic attacks here. We want the "groan zone" — that sweet spot where it's uncomfortable but you're not literally running away.

Good groan: "Ugh, I really don't want to do this... but fine."
Too big: "I think I'm going to throw up and also move to another country."

If it's too much, shrink it. Post to 1,000 people? Try 100. Still scary? Try stories. Find the version that makes you groan but not spiral.

## 2.3 Healing Tab

**ROUTE:** `/7-day-challenge` (Healing tab)

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Release practices for processing stuck emotions that keep your nervous system on high alert. Includes daily micro-releases (90 seconds!), emotion processing, and deeper weekly practices when you're ready.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Trapped emotions = trapped potential. If your body is holding anger, grief, or shame from the past, your nervous system is using energy to keep that stuff down — energy that could be going toward creating.

Releasing isn't just "woo-woo self-care." It's making space for what you actually want to build.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What's the difference between Release and Recognise?"
- "Do I have to do the deep release every week?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What's the difference between Release and Recognise?
A: **Recognise** (in Groans) is noticing patterns — observing what protective voice showed up, what fear was triggered. It's cognitive.

**Release** (in Healing) is moving energy through your body — breathwork, shaking, crying, movement. It's somatic.

You can recognise a pattern without releasing the emotion. And you can release emotion without fully understanding where it came from. Both are valuable. They work together.

Q: Do I have to do the deep release every week?
A: Nope, totally optional. The Big Release (45+ minutes of intensive practice) is powerful stuff, but it's not required.

If you're drawn to it, try it. If it feels like too much right now, stick with the daily 90-second releases. They add up more than you'd think.

Listen to your body. Some weeks want intensity. Others want gentleness. Both are valid.

## 2.5 Tracker Tab

**ROUTE:** `/7-day-challenge` (Tracker tab)

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Log your daily energy using the Flow Compass — a simple N/E/S/W system that tracks how you're feeling. Over time, patterns emerge that help you understand your rhythms.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Your nervous system speaks through your body. Tracking helps you notice: When do you flow? When do you drain? What correlates with good days vs. hard days?

This data becomes the foundation for working WITH your system instead of constantly fighting against it.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What do the directions mean?"
- "How often should I log?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What do the directions mean?
A: **North (Green)** — Flow state. Excited + Ease. Everything clicks. This is the zone.

**East (Blue)** — Growth edge. Excited + Resistance. It's hard but you feel alive. You're stretching.

**South (Red)** — Drain. Tired + Resistance. Hard and exhausting. Time to rest or reassess.

**West (Yellow)** — Coasting. Tired + Ease. Things are smooth but energy is low. Might need more challenge, or might need more rest.

Q: How often should I log?
A: Once a day is ideal — takes like 30 seconds. Just a quick "how am I feeling right now?"

The value comes from consistency. A week of data is interesting. A month is revealing. Three months shows you patterns you'd never see otherwise.

Don't stress about perfection. Miss a day? Just log the next one.

## 3.2 Upsell Flow

**ROUTE:** `/upsell-offer`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Design what you offer AFTER someone buys your main thing. What's the natural next step? How do you deepen the relationship and create even more value for people who want to go further?

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Upsells aren't about squeezing more money out of people — they're about serving the people who WANT more. If someone gets great results from your core offer, they'll often ask "what's next?"

Having something to offer them isn't pushy. It's helpful.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What's the difference between upsell and downsell?"
- "Do I need an upsell right away?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What's the difference between upsell and downsell?
A: **Upsell** — More expensive, more comprehensive. For people who want to go all-in. "Want the VIP version with 1:1 support?"

**Downsell** — Less expensive, more accessible. For people who can't afford the main thing yet but still want help. "Not ready for the full program? Start with this mini version."

Both serve different segments of your audience. Neither is better — they're just different entry points.

Q: Do I need an upsell right away?
A: Nope. Start with your core offer. Get people results. Learn what they want next.

Your best upsell ideas will come from actual customers asking "What's next?" or "Do you have something for [specific problem]?"

This flow helps you brainstorm, but don't stress about having everything figured out on day one.

## 3.3 Downsell Flow

**ROUTE:** `/downsell-offer`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Design an accessible entry point for people who can't afford (or aren't ready for) your main offer. This is your "gateway" — something valuable at a lower price that lets people experience you.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
Not everyone is ready to buy your main thing. A downsell lets you serve them anyway AND build trust for when they ARE ready.

It also creates a customer relationship even with people who can't pay full price right now. Some of those people will become your biggest fans later.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "Isn't low-ticket a lot of work for little return?"
- "What makes a good downsell?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: Isn't low-ticket a lot of work for little return?
A: It can be, if you build it wrong. The key is creating something that requires minimal ongoing effort — a course, a template pack, a recorded workshop. Build once, sell forever.

Think of it as a trust-builder, not a profit center. The real value is that they experience you, see results, and become warm leads for your core offer.

Q: What makes a good downsell?
A: A good downsell solves ONE specific problem completely. Not a watered-down version of your main thing — a focused solution to a focused problem.

Example: If your core offer is "Complete Business Launch Program," your downsell might be "The 30-Minute Clarity Session Recording" or "Offer Creation Template Pack."

Specific and complete beats vague and partial every time.

## 3.4 Continuity Flow

**ROUTE:** `/continuity-offer`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Design your recurring revenue — something people pay for monthly or annually. Could be a membership, ongoing coaching, software access, or community.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
One-time sales = hamster wheel. You have to keep finding new customers every month just to stay afloat.

Continuity = stability. Recurring revenue you can count on. Even a small amount ($1-2K/month) dramatically changes how your nervous system feels about money. You're not starting from zero every time.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "Do I need recurring revenue to have a real business?"
- "What if my thing doesn't seem like a subscription?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: Do I need recurring revenue to have a real business?
A: No — plenty of successful businesses run on one-time sales. But continuity does create a different kind of stability.

When you know $2K is coming in every month regardless of new sales, you can breathe easier. You can take creative risks. You're not in desperation mode.

It's not required. But it's really, really nice.

Q: What if my thing doesn't seem like a subscription?
A: Get creative! Almost anything can have a continuity element:

- **Community access** — monthly membership
- **Ongoing support** — monthly retainer
- **Fresh content** — new resources each month
- **Accountability** — regular check-ins
- **Tools** — software or template access

What would your customers want ongoing help with? Start there.

## 3.5 Leads Strategy Flow

**ROUTE:** `/leads-strategy`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Figure out how you'll attract people into your world. Where will they come from? What will draw them in? This is your lead generation strategy — the top of your funnel.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
No leads = no business. But trying to be everywhere at once = burnout.

A clear lead strategy helps you show up consistently in places where YOUR people already hang out. Instead of random acts of marketing, you have a plan.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "Where should I be generating leads?"
- "How many lead sources do I need?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: Where should I be generating leads?
A: Wherever your ideal customers already spend their time. Not where you think you "should" be — where THEY actually are.

If your people are on LinkedIn, be on LinkedIn. If they're in Facebook groups, go there. If they listen to podcasts, pitch yourself as a guest.

Also: pick a platform you'll actually enjoy using. Consistency beats strategy. A mediocre plan you stick with beats a perfect plan you abandon.

Q: How many lead sources do I need?
A: Start with ONE. Just one. Master it. Then maybe add another.

Most people spread themselves across 5 platforms and suck at all of them. One focused channel done well beats five channels done half-assed.

Once your first source is generating consistent leads (give it 3-6 months), consider adding a second.

## 3.6 Lead Magnet Flow

**ROUTE:** `/lead-magnet`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Create your free offer — the thing you give away in exchange for someone's email address. Could be a PDF, video, quiz, template, or mini-course.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
A lead magnet lets people experience your value before they pay. It builds trust, shows your expertise, and gives them a taste of what working with you is like.

It's also how you build an email list — still one of the most valuable assets you can own.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What makes a good lead magnet?"
- "Does it have to be perfect?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What makes a good lead magnet?
A: It solves a small but urgent problem. Not "Ultimate Guide to Everything" — more like "5 Scripts for Cold Outreach" or "The 10-Minute Morning Routine."

Good lead magnets are:
- **Specific** (not vague and broad)
- **Quick to consume** (value in under 10 minutes)
- **A taste of your paid stuff** (so they want more)
- **Easy for you to create** (don't overcomplicate)

The best ones are often something you already have — a process you use, notes you've compiled, a template you've made.

Q: Does it have to be perfect?
A: God, no. Done beats perfect. Every time.

A "pretty good" lead magnet that's actually live will outperform a "perfect" one that's stuck in your head forever. You can always improve it based on feedback.

Ship something simple. See if people want it. Make it better later.

## 4.2 Flow Compass Page

**ROUTE:** `/flow-compass`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
A visual map of your energy over time. See your N/E/S/W logs plotted on a timeline, with patterns highlighted. This is where tracking becomes actual insight.

**WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**
One data point is just a moment. Many data points reveal patterns.

This page shows you: When do you consistently flow? When do you consistently drain? What circumstances correlate with each? Understanding your patterns lets you design life around them instead of fighting against them.

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**
- "What patterns should I look for?"
- "How do I use this information?"

**FAQ ANSWERS:**

Q: What patterns should I look for?
A: - **Weekly rhythms** — Are certain days consistently better or worse?
- **Activity correlations** — What were you doing on North days? South days?
- **Streaks** — How long do you sustain flow before dipping?
- **Recovery time** — After a South day, how long until you're back to North?

Look for patterns to replicate (what creates flow) AND patterns to interrupt (what creates drain).

Q: How do I use this information?
A: Design your week around your patterns:

- **Schedule important work on your typical North days**
- **Batch admin tasks on West days** (low energy, low resistance)
- **Protect rest when you notice South patterns emerging**
- **Lean into East moments** — that's growth happening

You're not trying to be in North 24/7. You're trying to work with your natural rhythms instead of against them.

## 7.1 Validation Flows Manager

**ROUTE:** `/validation-flows`

**WHAT IS THIS?**
Create and manage customer surveys to get real data from real humans. Two main flavors: validation surveys (understand pain points, psychographics, demographics before building) and feedback surveys (improve what you've already created).

**CONTEXTUAL PROMPTS:**

Q: What's the difference between validation and feedback surveys?
A: **Validation surveys** — Before you build. Testing if the problem is real, if people care enough to pay, if your assumptions about your audience are even close.

**Feedback surveys** — After you build. Learning what's working, what's not, and what people want next.

Launching soon? Validation. Already have customers? Feedback.

Q: How many responses do I need?
A: Quality beats quantity. 10 thoughtful responses beat 100 one-word answers.

For feedback: Even 5 detailed responses can show you what to improve. Look for themes, not individual opinions.

Q: What are the stages of a funnel?
A: The basic flow:

1. **Awareness** — They discover you exist (content, ads, referrals)
2. **Interest** — They want to learn more (lead magnet, email list)
3. **Consideration** — They're evaluating if you're right for them (nurture emails, free content)

Minimum viable funnel:
- One place you show up consistently
- One lead magnet that captures emails
- One offer you can sell

That's it. Add complexity later when you actually need it. Most people overcomplicate way too early.

# ZARLO VOICE GUIDE

## The Hybrid Tone

**Core vibe:** Light on the surface, real talk underneath. Like a wise friend who uses humor to deliver truth bombs.

### Do's

- **Use "you" and "your"** — Direct, personal
- **Use contractions** — "You're" not "You are", "don't" not "do not"
- **Name the feeling** — "That ugh feeling", "the spiral", "the panic"
- **Make it relatable** — "Classic.", "Join the club.", "We've all been there."
- **Use metaphors** — "Secret bouncer", "invisible software", "survival code"
- **Acknowledge resistance** — "I know, I know..." before asking something hard
- **Celebrate simply** — "Yes!", "Look at you go!", "That's huge."

### Don'ts

- **Don't be preachy** — No lectures, no "you should"
- **Don't over-explain** — Trust them to get it
- **Don't be fake-positive** — Acknowledge when things are hard
- **Don't use corporate speak** — No "leverage", "optimize", "synergy"
- **Don't be sarcastic** — Playful, not mocking
- **Don't minimize** — "Just do it" dismisses real struggle

### Phrase Bank

**Opening lines:**
- "Here's the sneaky truth..."
- "Okay, real talk..."
- "Here's what most people miss..."
- "Classic [problem]. Let me explain..."

**Validating:**
- "That's actually super common."
- "You're not broken — your nervous system is just doing its job."
- "Join the club — there are millions of us."

**Encouraging action:**
- "Ready to find out?"
- "Let's catch it in the act."
- "Time to renegotiate."
- "The work will still be here when you're ready."

**Celebrating:**
- "Look at you go!"
- "Your nervous system is like 'wait, we survived that?!'"
- "That's how we expand. One groan at a time."

**When they're struggling:**
- "No shame — let's figure out what happened."
- "The groan won today. Tomorrow's another round."
- "Your tank is empty. Rest is part of the work."
