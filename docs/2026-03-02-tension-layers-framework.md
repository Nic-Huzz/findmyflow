# The 4 Tension Layers — FindMyFlow Growth Framework

> The core insight: personal and professional growth follows a predictable inside-out sequence. Each layer must be at least partially resolved before the next can sustainably unlock. Most people are stuck at one specific layer — if you can identify which, you can prioritise the right challenges.

---

## The Framework: KNOW → SAFE → SEEN → OWN

| # | Layer | Tension | The Voice In Their Head | Resolved By |
|---|-------|---------|------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Discover** | Identity | "I know I'm meant for more, but I can't name what" | Flow Finder (skills, problems, persona) |
| 2 | **Regulate** | Nervous System | "I know what to do but my body won't let me" | Protective voice work, Healing Compass, rewiring quests |
| 3 | **Reveal** | Visibility + Action | "I have gifts but I'm terrified to show up and do the thing" | Groan Matrix / Play-List (graduated courage challenges) + Business stages |
| 4 | **Value** | Worth | "Who am I to charge for this?" | Money model flows, offer building, proof collection |

### Why "Action" isn't its own layer

Visibility and action are the same moment. Every groan challenge IS an action — posting, going live, having a sales conversation, claiming authority. And when someone "can't take action," the real block is always one of the other layers:

- Don't know what to build → **Discover**
- Freeze when they try → **Regulate**
- Scared to ship because people will see it → **Reveal**
- Can't charge for it → **Value**

There is no scenario where action is blocked but all four others are resolved. Inaction is always a symptom, never the root cause.

## The Tree Analogy

Each layer maps to a part of a tree. This explains both the progression and why people get stuck.

```
                    🍎 FRUIT — Worth
                   "The harvest — what others receive and pay for"
                  /    |    \
            🌿 BRANCHES — Visibility + Action
           "Reaching outward, doing the work in the open"
                  |
                🪵 TRUNK — Nervous System
               "Structural stability under pressure"
                  |
                🌱 ROOTS — Identity
               "Hidden, foundational — who you are underneath"
```

### Why people get stuck

They try to grow **fruit** (charge money, build offers) from a tree with:
- **Shallow roots** — no identity clarity, building the wrong thing
- **A cracked trunk** — dysregulated nervous system, burning out or freezing
- **No branches** — invisible to the world, great work nobody knows about

Going "backwards" to do root and trunk work **isn't regression** — it's the thing that makes everything above it stronger and more sustainable.

### Why the order matters

| Skipping... | Leads to... |
|-------------|-------------|
| Roots → straight to Branches | "I'm putting myself out there but I don't even know what I stand for" |
| Trunk → straight to Branches | Burnout, freeze response, self-sabotage at the moment of visibility |
| Roots + Trunk → straight to Fruit | Imposter syndrome, undercharging, giving it away free |
| Branches without Roots | Building the wrong thing, pivoting endlessly |

## The River Analogy — "Find My Flow"

The app name IS the framework. "Find My Flow" isn't just a brand — it's the diagnostic question: **"Where in the river are you stuck?"**

Each tension layer maps to a stage of a river system, from source to sea:

```
  💧 THE SPRING — Discover (Identity)
     "Where the water first emerges"
         |
         ↓
  🪨 THE RIVERBED — Regulate (Nervous System)
     "The channel that holds the water"
         |
         ↓
  🌊 THE CURRENT — Reveal (Visibility + Action)
     "Water moving into the open — visible, felt, carving new paths"
         |
         ↓
  🌏 THE OCEAN — Value (Worth)
     "Where the river meets the world"
```

| Layer | River Element | What It Means | When It's Blocked |
|-------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Discover** | **The Spring** | Where the water first emerges — you can't have a river without finding the source | No water. Dry ground. "I don't know what my thing is." |
| **Regulate** | **The Riverbed** | The channel that holds the water — without it, flow floods, freezes, or dries up | Water with no channel. It pools, stagnates, or floods everywhere at once. "I know what to do but I can't make myself do it." |
| **Reveal** | **The Current** | Water moving into the open — visible, felt, carving new paths as it flows | A river running underground. Powerful but hidden. "I have gifts but nobody sees them — and nothing gets built." |
| **Value** | **The Ocean** | Where the river meets the world — vast, deep, receiving and giving back | A river that never reaches the sea. All that energy, going nowhere. "Who am I to charge for this?" |

### Why "Find My Flow" works as the framework

The journey through the app is literally finding your flow:

1. **Find the spring** — discover where your water comes from (Flow Finder)
2. **Carve the riverbed** — build the nervous system capacity to hold it (Healing / Rewiring)
3. **Let the current run** — show up, be visible, do the work in the open (Groan Matrix + Business stages)
4. **Reach the ocean** — release your value into the world at full price (Money models)

### What being stuck looks like in river terms

| Stuck Pattern | River Translation | What They Need |
|--------------|-------------------|----------------|
| "I found my spring but there's no riverbed" | Identity without nervous system regulation — water pools and stagnates | Regulate: healing, rewiring, nervous system work |
| "I have a riverbed but the spring dried up" | Doing the work but lost connection to why | Discover: revisit Flow Finder, reconnect with identity |
| "My current runs underground" | Capable and regulated but invisible — nothing shipped, nobody knows | Reveal: graduated visibility challenges + business stages |
| "I'm trying to be an ocean with no spring" | Charging for something they haven't found yet | Discover: go back to the source |
| "The current flows but never reaches the sea" | Visible and building but can't own the value | Value: money model work, proof collection |

### The diagnostic reframed

Instead of "what's your tension layer?", the question becomes:

> **"Where in the river is your flow getting stuck?"**

And the 4 diagnostic questions become a river map — each one checks whether that section of the river is flowing or blocked. The user's **Priority Layer** is the first blockage point. Clear that, and everything downstream starts moving.

- **Visibility challenges** (Screen → Authority) = showing up with increasing courage
- **Business stages** (Validation → Launch) = building and shipping with increasing complexity
- Both are forms of "doing the thing in the open" — you can't validate without being seen, you can't launch without being visible

## Diagnostic: The 4-Layer Check-In

A quick self-identification that surfaces which layer is the user's current priority. One question per layer, each designed to measure how resolved/unresolved that tension feels.

### Question Design Principles

- Each question should feel like it **names something the user has been feeling but couldn't articulate**
- Use "which feels most true" framing — no right answers, no judgement
- The options within each question range from "stuck" to "solid" on that layer
- The user's **Priority Layer** = the lowest layer where they still feel significant tension

## Scoring & Priority Layer

**Priority Layer** = the lowest-numbered layer with a score of 0 or 1.

If all layers score 2+, priority = the lowest-scored layer (the weakest link).

## How This Drives the App

### 1. Priority Section in 7-Day Challenge

Top of the challenge feed shows a "Your Priority" card based on their current layer. Contains the most relevant quest type:

| Priority Layer | Highlighted Challenges |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Discover | Flow Finder quests (skills, problems, persona) |
| Regulate | Healing quests (Recognise, Release, Rewire, Reconnect) |
| Reveal | Groan Matrix challenges at their visibility edge + business stage quests |
| Value | Money model / offer building quests |

### 2. Progress Visualisation

The tree metaphor could become a visual progress indicator — roots fill in, trunk strengthens, branches extend, fruit grows. Each section lights up as the user completes challenges in that layer.

### 3. Re-assessment

Periodic re-check (monthly? seasonally?) or on-demand via Play Profile. As users progress, their priority layer shifts upward. The app adapts.

### 4. Onboarding Integration

These 4 questions replace the current Q1-Q3 onboarding. They create tension ("I score 0 on identity — that's why I feel stuck"), then immediately show the user what the app does about it.
