# The Experience Creator Capability Map

**What this is:** Every capability an experience creator needs to make more money and save more time, mapped to the tools that power each one. Use this to audit your current stack, find gaps, and choose what to connect next.

**Who this is for:** Yoga teachers, breathwork facilitators, retreat leaders, workshop hosts, ecstatic dance organisers, coaches, sound healers, fitness instructors — anyone who creates transformational experiences and needs to fill the room, run the event, and grow.

**How to use it:** Go to the section that matches your biggest pain. See what tools exist. Pick one. Connect it.

---

## Make More Money

### 1. Package Your Offer

*"I don't know how to price or structure what I sell."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Value Stack Analysis** | Identifies gaps in your offer layers (attraction, core, upsell, continuity) | Claude / ChatGPT with offer framework prompts, Hormozi Value Equation worksheet, dedicated offer builder app |
| **Pricing Sanity Check** | Compares your price to your income target and tells you if the math works | Spreadsheet (PTUF calculator), Claude with your numbers, Stripe revenue data |
| **Offer Scoring** | Rates your offer against proven frameworks (Value Equation: Dream Outcome x Likelihood / Time x Effort) | Claude / ChatGPT with the framework, `/offers` skill (Claude Code), GrandSlam flow (Vibe Rise) |

### 2. Choose Your Hook

*"People see my offer but don't buy."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Offer Hook Selection** | Scores 6 pricing psychology hooks against your specific offer (Win Your Money Back, Decoy, Pay Less Now, Buy X Get Y, Giveaway, Free With Consumption) | Claude / ChatGPT with Hormozi's 6 hooks + your offer details, custom scoring prompt |
| **Video Hook Creation** | Creates scroll-stopping openings for video ads and social content | Higgsfield Marketing Studio (hook templates + 9 video modes), manual (study top-performing hooks in your niche) |
| **Hook Testing** | Scores hook strength before you publish | Higgsfield Virality Prediction (hook strength, retention, neural engagement), A/B test tools (PostHog, VWO, Optimizely) |

### 3. Create Content

*"I know I should post but I don't know what to say or don't have time."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Social Post Drafting** | Generates posts in your voice from your offer/brand context | Claude / ChatGPT + voice guide, Jasper, Copy.ai, Content Generator (Vibe Rise edge function) |
| **Visual Content** | Creates images, carousels, graphics for posts | Canva (templates), Higgsfield (AI product shots, UGC), Orior AI (AI persona, €15/mo), Midjourney, DALL-E |
| **Video Content** | Short-form video for Reels/TikTok/Shorts | Higgsfield Marketing Studio (URL-to-ad, 9 modes), Orior AI (lip-sync persona), CapCut, InVideo AI |
| **AI Persona** | Consistent virtual presenter across all platforms. Auto-posts. | Orior AI (photorealistic character + 6-platform auto-post), Higgsfield Soul ID (consistent character), HeyGen |
| **Blog / SEO** | Long-form content that ranks in search and drives organic leads | Claude / ChatGPT, Surfer SEO (optimization), Firecrawl (keyword research), WordPress / Ghost / Webflow |
| **Newsletter** | Regular email to your list with updates, stories, offers | Resend (API), beehiiv (newsletter platform), Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Mailchimp, Substack |
| **Content Calendar** | Plans what to post and when | Notion, Trello, Claude (generate a month of content ideas from your offer), Later, Buffer |

### 4. Find Leads

*"I don't know where my next customer is coming from."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Social Listening** | Monitors platforms for people asking about what you offer | Reddit monitoring (Claude Code triggers, n8n templates), SparkToro, Mention, Brand24 |
| **SEO / Organic Traffic** | People find you via search | Blog + SEO tools (Surfer, Ahrefs, Semrush), Firecrawl (competitor gaps), programmatic pages |
| **Warm Outreach** | Personal messages to people who already know you | Gmail (Composio), your phone, CRM contact list, handwritten notes |
| **Cold Outreach (Email)** | Reach people who don't know you yet with a personalised email | FullEnrich (email enrichment, waterfall), Clay (enrichment pipelines, from $185/mo), Apollo.io (database + sequences, free tier available), Instantly, Smartlead |
| **Cold Outreach (DM)** | Personalised DMs on Instagram or LinkedIn | Manual (safest), agent-drafted messages, Expandi (LinkedIn, cloud-based, use with caution) |
| **IG Comment / DM Flows** | Automated responses when people comment or DM keywords | ManyChat (keyword triggers, welcome flows, story replies, free for IG), manual |
| **Paid Ads** | Pay to reach your target audience | Meta Ads (best for wellness/experiences), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Higgsfield (ad creative) |
| **Referrals** | Your past participants bring new ones | Referral program (manual or ReferralCandy, Viral Loops), affiliate commissions, "bring a friend" offers |
| **Partnerships** | Other creators promote you to their audience | Interview flywheel, co-hosted events, guest teaching, cross-promotion |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Broadcast updates, share event links, build community via the app your audience already uses daily | WhatsApp Business (free), WhatsApp Business API (via Twilio, 360dialog), community groups |
| **Directory Listings** | Get found on platforms people already browse | Google Business, Eventbrite, Retreat Guru, BookRetreats, Mindbody, ClassPass |

### 5. Capture & Convert

*"People see my stuff but don't sign up."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Booking / Scheduling** | Let people book your experience directly | Cal.com (free, API-first, group booking), Calendly, Acuity, Talkspresso (creator-focused: booking + video + payments), Mindbody |
| **Landing Page** | A single page that explains your experience and has a CTA | Carrd ($19/yr), Webflow, Squarespace, Notion + Super, Typedream, WordPress |
| **Payment Processing** | Take money | Stripe (2.9% + 30c US, varies by country), Square, PayPal, Wise (international transfers), bank transfer |
| **Email Sequences** | Automated emails that nurture leads toward booking | Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Mailchimp, Resend (API), ActiveCampaign, beehiiv, manual follow-up |
| **Lead Magnet** | Free thing that captures email addresses | PDF guide, quiz, mini-course, free class, checklist, template, Canva (design), Claude (draft) |
| **Waitlist / Pre-launch** | Build hype and capture demand before your experience launches | Carrd (with email form), Notion + Super, Launchrock, beehiiv landing page, simple Google Form |

### 6. Deliver & Grow

*"I run the experience but then what?"*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Post-Event Follow-up** | Automated messages after an experience to deepen the relationship | Email sequences (Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Resend), WhatsApp group, Telegram channel, manual personal messages |
| **Testimonial Collection** | Gather social proof from participants | Senja, VideoAsk, Typeform, Google Forms, manual ask (best conversion: ask in-person right after the event) |
| **Upsell / Next Step** | Guide participants to your next offering | Email sequence with next offer, 1:1 follow-up call, in-experience mention of what's next |
| **Community** | Keep participants connected between events | WhatsApp group, Telegram, Discord, Circle, Mighty Networks, Facebook Group |
| **Repeat Booking** | Get participants to come back | Early bird for returning participants, subscription/membership, punch cards, loyalty program |

*"I have no idea if what I'm doing is actually working."*

| Capability | What It Does | Tool Options |

*"I'm maxed out. I can't do more without help."*

## The Stack Tiers

Not everyone needs everything. Here's what to connect based on where you are.

### Tier 0: Just Starting (free)
You have an idea for an experience but haven't run one yet.

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Booking page | Cal.com | Free |
| Payment | Stripe | 2.9% + 30c per transaction (US, varies by country) |
| Email list | Mailchimp or beehiiv | Free tier |
| Content | Your phone + Instagram | Free |
| Landing page | Carrd or Notion + Super | Free-$19/yr |

**Total: $0-19/yr + Stripe fees**

### Tier 1: Running Events ($0-50/mo)
You're running experiences and need to fill the room consistently.

Add to Tier 0:

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Email sequences | Kit (formerly ConvertKit) or Resend | Free-$29/mo |
| Content scheduling | Buffer or Later | Free tier |
| Analytics | Fathom | $14/mo |
| CRM | Notion database or Google Sheets | Free |
| Social listening | Reddit monitoring (Claude Code) | Claude Code subscription (~$20/mo) |

### Tier 2: Growing ($50-200/mo)
You're running regular events and want to scale without working more hours.

Add to Tier 1:

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| AI content creation | Orior AI (persona + auto-post) | €15/mo |
| Video ads | Higgsfield Marketing Studio | Varies |
| Paid ads | Meta Ads | Ad spend |
| Lead enrichment | FullEnrich or Apollo | Pay-per-lead or free tier (Apollo) |
| Workflow automation | n8n (self-hosted) | Free |

### Tier 3: Scaling ($200+/mo)
You're running a real business and want systems that run without you.

Add to Tier 2:

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Full automation stack | Claude Code + n8n + Composio | Subscriptions |
| AI video at scale | Higgsfield Supercomputer | Varies |
| Competitor intelligence | Firecrawl + monitoring | Varies |
| Equipment (own or rent out) | Headsets, sound systems, AV gear | Capital investment |
| Team / VA | Belay, Fiverr, local | $500+/mo |
