# /get-started Onboarding Slides

*4 slides. Huzz's personal story, compressed. No quiz, no diagnostic. The emotional splinter story told simply enough that anyone stuck recognises themselves in it. Adapted from workshop intro.*

---

## The Arc

Remember → Name it → Feel it → Act

Under 15 seconds of swiping. One idea per slide. The "seen" moment is Slide 3 (the rainbow clothes story). Ends with stakes + promise → sign up.

---

## Slides

### Slide 1: Remember

"Think about you as a kid. How playful. How full of love. How care-free.

So where did they go?"

---

### Slide 2: Name It

"Somewhere along the way, we collect emotional splinters. Experiences that made being ourselves feel unsafe.

Our nervous system = our safety system. So to stop this from happening again, it created protective patterns."

---

### Slide 3: Feel It (Huzz's Story)

"At 14 I was a playful, dancing, rainbow. I wore fluro pinks, blues, oranges, purples and uploaded videos of me dancing on Facebook.

Then I got teased for it.

So for the next TWELVE YEARS I never wore rainbow clothes or danced sober again. Basically the things that make me feel most like me."

---

### Slide 4: Close + CTA

"This is what breaks my heart about emotional splinters. They leave us scared to be ourselves.

And this suppression causes depression.

Ready to reconnect to your essence and express again?"

→ [Sign up / Log in]

## Design Notes

- Purple (#5e17eb) → Gold (#E9A23B) gradient progression across slides (dark → warm)
- Mobile-first. Full-screen swipeable cards.
- Text centered, large, readable. Short lines.
- Gold text on purple background (matches workshop slide style).
- No buttons until Slide 4. Just swipe.
- Slide 4 has sign-up/log-in buttons below the text.
- No images needed. The words carry it. White space between lines creates the pacing.

## What Comes After Sign-Up

After authentication, user enters the main app experience. Play-list identification and deeper diagnostics happen inside the app (Level 0 quests, Curiosity Compass, etc.), not in this pre-auth onboarding.

This flow is strictly pre-auth: tell the story, create recognition, get them to sign up.

## Relationship to Book

These 4 slides are a compressed version of Book Section 1 (The Crack). The emotional splinter concept maps to the Installation Map model. The rainbow clothes story is a specific instance of: Essence → Wound → Protection → Suppression. A user who reads the book will recognise the onboarding. A user who sees the onboarding will recognise the book.

## Key Concepts Introduced

- **Emotional splinter**: The specific experience that made being yourself feel unsafe
- **Protective patterns**: What the nervous system built to prevent it happening again
- **Suppression → depression**: The cost of the protective pattern
- **Essence**: The playful, loving, care-free version that's still underneath
