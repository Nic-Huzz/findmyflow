# Life Map Exercise — Miro Reference

Original Miro board exercise that Huzz sends to clients. This documents all questions from the board for future reference. The app's Life Map flow is a condensed version of this.

## Structure

The Miro exercise has 5 sections, each with questions answered across **4 time periods**: Pre-School + Primary School, High School, 18–25, 25 to Now.

Users write answers on colour-coded sticky notes, then drag them onto a horizontal timeline (the "life map") at the end.

---

## Part 1 — Reflecting on Each Stage of Life (Yellow/Orange)

**Sticky colour:** Yellow

These questions trojan-horse into **Skills** — they surface what the user naturally gravitates toward, what they're good at, and what puts them in flow.

| # | Question | Stickies per period |
|---|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | What did you do in 'free-time' or what were your hobbies? | 6 |
| 2 | What achievements, things you've done, success you've experienced that you're proud of? | 6 |
| 3 | What moments have you felt absolutely high on life? | 3 |
| 4 | What activities have put you in flow? | 4 |

---

## Part 2 — Reflecting on Each Stage of Life (Blue/Pink)

**Sticky colours:** Pink (experiences/characters/role models), Blue (challenges/growth/traits)

These questions trojan-horse into **Problems** (challenges, what shaped you, what you care about) and **Personas** (who you relate to, who inspires you).

| # | Question | Category | Stickies per period |
|---|----------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | What experiences in your life are outside the ordinary and norm for those around you? OR What things about yourself have made you feel weird or different from your friends? | Problems | 3 pink |
| 2 | What are the biggest challenges you overcame in this period? | Problems | 3 blue |
| 3 | What areas of growth in your life are you most proud of? | Problems | 6 blue |
| 4a | Who were your favourite characters in movies, TV shows, books that you absolutely loved in this period of your life? | Personas | 3-6 pink |
| 4b | What about them made you feel connected to them, inspired you, felt aligned with? | Personas | 3-6 blue |
| 5a | Who are role models you've had throughout different periods of your life? | Personas | 3-6 pink |
| 5b | What did they do to make them role models? | Personas | 3-6 blue |

## The Life Map Output

After completing all sections, users drag every sticky onto a **horizontal timeline** organized by life period (with schools, jobs, and key events as anchors along the middle).

- **Top half:** Activities, hobbies, experiences (blue stickies) placed chronologically
- **Middle line:** Schools, jobs, key life events as timeline markers
- **Bottom half:** Achievements (green), challenges/struggles (pink), milestones

The timeline creates a visual "map" of the user's entire life that reveals patterns — recurring skills, persistent problems they care about, and the kinds of people/characters they're drawn to.

## Key Design Insight

The genius of the exercise is that users think they're just "reflecting on their life" — storytelling about their past. They don't realize they're systematically surfacing:
- **Skills** (what they naturally do, enjoy, and are good at)
- **Problems** (what shaped them, what they care about, what they want to change)
- **Personas** (who they relate to and want to help — usually former versions of themselves)

The AI clustering at the end reveals these patterns, creating an "aha moment" where the user sees their entire life has been pointing toward a coherent direction they couldn't see before.
