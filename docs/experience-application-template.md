# Experience Application Template

## Structure for Venue/Space Applications (250 words max)

This template was used for the Healing Compass application. Reusable for any experience designed using Shift Architecture.

### The 5-Block Structure

**Block 1: Personal Pain Point (2-3 sentences)**
Lead with YOUR story. What struggle did you have that this experience solves? This is not a sales pitch to participants. It's showing the venue owner that you built this from lived experience, not theory.

> "On my own [journey type], one of the biggest struggles was [specific pain point]. [What made it hard]. Inspired by that pain point, over the past [time period] I developed [Experience Name]..."

**Block 2: What It Is + Who It's For (1 sentence)**
Format, duration, group size, and the outcome in one line. This is what the venue owner will repeat when someone asks "what's that workshop?"

> "...a [duration]-minute workshop designed for groups of [size] that helps participants [specific outcome]."

**Block 3: How It Works (3-5 sentences)**
The method, described in felt language. Not clinical. Not vague. Enough that someone can picture what happens in the room. Include the mechanism (memory reconsolidation) without citations. Name the phases to show intentional design.

> "Participants [what they do]. The methodology is grounded in [framework], showing that [why it works]. The workshop creates these conditions through [specific structure]."

**Block 4: Safety + Format (2-3 sentences)**
What the experience physically involves. What it doesn't involve. Opt-out language. This is for the venue's liability comfort.

> "The experience includes [physical activities]. No [boundaries]. Clear opt-out language at each stage."

**Block 5: Social Proof (1-2 sentences)**
What participants say. Where you've run it. How long. Keep it factual and short.

> "Participants consistently describe it as [testimonial-level statement]. Running regularly at [venue] for [duration]."

### Facilitator Bio Template (150 words max)

**Line 1: The hook** — Your identity shift, not your credentials. "Former [old career] who [breaking point]."

**Line 2: The journey** — What you studied and why. "Spent [time] studying [what] because [personal reason]."

**Line 3: What you built** — The method, named. "Developed [Method Name], a [one-line description]."

**Line 4: Where you've done it** — Venues, group sizes, frequency. Factual.

**Line 5: Training/credentials** — Only if relevant and brief. "Trained in [modality]. Certified in [thing]."

### Space Requirements Block (optional, add if form allows)

> Open floor for [size] people to stand, move, and lie down. Sound system for music. Dimmable lighting preferred. [Duration] plus 15 minutes setup/packdown.

### Example: Healing Compass (228 words)

On my own healing journey, one of the biggest struggles was knowing I wanted to shift things but not knowing what needed to change, or which of the countless workshops and modalities would actually work. Inspired by that pain point, over the past three years I developed the Healing Compass: a 120-minute workshop designed for groups of 5-15 that helps participants identify exactly what's keeping them stuck and guides them through a process to shift it.

Participants locate the protective pattern running their life (the part that hides, performs, controls, or people-pleases), feel where it lives in their body, and experience memory reconsolidation, a neurological process that updates the original encoding. The old trigger stops firing the same way. Not through willpower. Through the body actually updating.

The methodology is grounded in memory reconsolidation, a well-established neuroscience framework showing that emotional memories can be updated when specific conditions are met. The workshop creates these conditions through a structured 6-phase arc: safety and connection building, somatic activation, experiential mismatch, physical discharge via breathwork and movement, inner child integration, and verification of shift.

The experience includes standing movement, seated visualization, lying breathwork, and voluntary group sharing. No physical contact between participants. Clear opt-out language at each stage.

Participants consistently describe it as the most impactful workshop they've attended in Bali. Running regularly at The Istana for 6+ months.
