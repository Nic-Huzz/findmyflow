# Phase 2 vs Phase 3 Brand Landscape — With Gaps

Revenue figures verified Jul 2026. All figures are 2025 estimates unless noted.

For each branch: the biggest Phase 2 brands (what people currently pay for), existing Phase 3 brands (the loop-back products), and the GAPS where no Phase 3 brand exists yet.

**NOTE: Phase 3 brands are growing FAST. Oura hit $1B in 2025 (was $200M in 2023). Whoop hit $1.1B run rate. The Phase 3 market is accelerating.**

## SUMMARY: Where the Biggest Gaps Are

| Branch | Gap Size | Why Nobody's There Yet |
|--------|----------|----------------------|
| **Bonds: "Friendship experience"** | MASSIVE | Nobody thinks you can DESIGN friendship. Bumble tried with an app. The ROOM is the answer. |
| **Healing: "Regulation franchise"** | MASSIVE | Breathwork is still "woo" to most. Needs the secular reframe. Othership is trying but one studio. |
| **Threat: "Courage training"** | MASSIVE | The category doesn't have a NAME yet. Napoleon Hill described it in 1937. Nobody has productised it. |
| **Story: "Story night franchise"** | LARGE | The Moth proves it works. Nobody has scaled it. |
| **Movement: "Sober dance mainstream"** | LARGE | Daybreaker proved the format. Silent disco proved the tech. Nobody merged them at CrossFit scale. |
| **Food: "Subtraction as service"** | LARGE | Weight Watchers tracks what you ADD. Nobody tracks what you REMOVE. |
| **Shelter: "Wellness architecture"** | MEDIUM | WELL Building exists as a standard. Nobody offers it as a consumer service. |
| **Fire: "Fire circle franchise"** | MEDIUM | Solo Stove proved product demand. Nobody facilitates the EXPERIENCE. |
| **Status: "Craft circle franchise"** | MEDIUM | Maker movement proved it. Nobody scaled community making. |
| **Tech: "AI-augmented mastermind"** | MEDIUM | The tools exist. The facilitation format doesn't. |

The three biggest gaps — Friendship Experience, Regulation Franchise, Courage Training — are ALL in your territory. All three use Shift Architecture. All three are 90-minute formats. All three need facilitators you can train.
