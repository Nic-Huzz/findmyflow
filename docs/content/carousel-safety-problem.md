# Carousel: It's a Safety Problem, Not an Inspiration Problem

**Pillar:** Safety as Mechanism
**Audience:** People stuck in Head Full of Dreams
**CTA:** /try/facilitator-score

## Slide 1 (Hook)
You don't need more inspiration.
You need more safety.

## Slide 2 (The Problem)
You've read the books.
You've watched the talks.
You know EXACTLY what you should be doing.
So why aren't you doing it?

## Slide 3 (The Wrong Answer)
Self-help says: "You lack motivation."
Gym culture says: "You lack discipline."
Productivity culture says: "You lack a system."

They're all wrong.

## Slide 4 (The Real Answer)
Your nervous system treats identity change as physical threat.

When you try to become "someone who does cold plunges" or "someone who meditates" or "someone who speaks on stage" — your survival brain scans for danger.

Social risk. Competence risk. Identity risk.

It doesn't distinguish between a leopard and an identity shift. Both trigger the same response: freeze, avoid, scroll instead.

## Slide 5 (The Data)
Prochaska's research: people cycle through contemplation 3-7 times before acting.

That's not failure. That's the nervous system testing whether the identity shift is survivable.

## Slide 6 (The Gap)
Self-help books create the DESIRE for change.
But desire without safety = Head Full of Dreams.

You know what to do. Your nervous system won't let you do it.

## Slide 7 (The Fix)
The fix isn't more motivation. It's building enough nervous system capacity that the identity shift feels SAFE.

Daily micro-practices that expand your window of tolerance.
One small "vote" for the new identity, celebrated.
Community that normalises the change.

Not inspiration, then action.
Safety, then action.

## Slide 8 (The Reframe)
93% of healthcare spending is reactive.
7% is prevention.

The self-help industry has the same ratio: 93% inspiration, 7% capacity building.

What if we flipped it?

## Slide 9 (CTA)
The question isn't "what should I do?"
You already know.

The question is: "does my nervous system feel safe enough to do it?"
