**Continuity offer.** What keeps them coming back. Monthly membership, recurring circle, subscription community.

Most creators skip the attraction offer and wonder why nobody shows up. Or they only have an attraction offer and wonder why they can't pay rent.

**Check yours:** Go to /create and open the Offer Architecture tab. See how your stack compares to creators who've already figured this out.

### Email V3 (Day 3): "Which type are you?"

**Subject:** There are 3 types of experience creators

After studying 100 experience creators, I noticed something.

They all fit into one of 4 types. And the type determines what they should build first.

**The Stream.** You know exactly what experience you want to create but you're early. Clarity on the what, not the how. Your move: run 10 experiences. Charge something. Learn by doing.

**The Lake.** You've been doing this for a while but you feel stuck. Lots of skills, lots of ideas, no clear direction. Your move: stop adding. Start subtracting. Pick the ONE experience that lights you up most.

**The Waterfall.** You have deep expertise in one area but your audience is too narrow. You keep going deeper when you need to go wider. Your move: find the adjacent audience who needs what you already have.

**The River.** You're already running experiences and people love your work. But you can't scale without burning out. Your move: build the system. Offer stack. Marketing checklist. CRM. The business behind the gift.

Most creators try to solve a River problem when they're actually a Stream. Or a Waterfall problem when they're actually a Lake.

Knowing your type saves you months of building the wrong thing.
