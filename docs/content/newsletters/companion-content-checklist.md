## Social Media Posts (3x/week minimum)

Required for the "3 things I learned this week" recap email format. One week of posting = one email written in 10 minutes.

### Content Calendar (Rotating Themes)

| Day | Theme | Example |
|-----|-------|---------|
| Mon | **Framework reveal** | Shift Architecture step. Scope Map quadrant. Zone Calibration axis. |
| Wed | **Personal story / facilitator moment** | "Last weekend a participant cried 3 minutes in. Here's what I did." |
| Fri | **Contrarian take or quick tip** | "Stop asking people to close their eyes." / "The best workshops have no PowerPoint." |

### Content Types That Feed Email

- [ ] Framework reveals (Shift Architecture, Scope Map, Zone Calibration)
- [ ] Personal stories (origin story beats, facilitation moments, the crack)
- [ ] Contrarian takes ("Why I don't use guided meditations")
- [ ] Quick tips (1 actionable facilitation skill per post)
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes (building Vibe Rise, designing experiences, testing headsets)
- [ ] Research/data posts ("59 experience creators taught me this")

**Platform:** Instagram @_huzz (primary) + LinkedIn (secondary for B2B creator audience)

## How It All Connects

```
Social Posts (3x/week)
    |
    v
Recap Email ("3 things this week")  -->  Email List
    |                                        ^
    v                                        |
YouTube Videos (1x/week)  ---------> Video Promo Email
    |                                        ^
    v                                        |
Weekly Hot Seat (1x/week)  --------> Event Promo Email
    |                                        |
    v                                        v
Clips + Highlights  ----------------> More Social Posts
                                             |
                                             v
                                     Lead Magnets (Scorecard, Mini-Course, PDFs)
                                             |
                                             v
                                     LAUNCH SEQUENCE (when ready)
```

Everything feeds into everything else. The email list is the center. Social feeds email. Video feeds email. Hot seat feeds clips which feed social which feeds email.
