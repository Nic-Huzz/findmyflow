## Manifesto Pieces (long-form, high-conviction)

### 1. The Regi-Beta Paradox
*Source: Thought Notes 2025-01-13*
"Stuck / comfortable numb in the middle. Things aren't too bad but they aren't that good." This is your ICP described in one sentence. Most wellness content targets people in crisis. This targets the silent majority who will waste 30 years in grey because things are "fine." The slow-burn Crack.
- Hook: "The most dangerous place to be isn't rock bottom. It's the middle."
- **Format:** Video (talking head) + carousel recap
- **Confidence:** 7/10 — Strong insight, needs building out. Pair with data (workplace disengagement stats, hedonic adaptation research). Hook is killer, middle needs work.
- **Tier:** 3

### 2. Why We Leave School Afraid to Be Ourselves
*Source: Thought Notes 2025-06-05 (Nikigai University thesis) + 2023-08-02 (school micro-traumas)*
The full thesis: banter creates micro-traumas → micro-traumas create triggers → triggers prevent self-expression → self-expression gets replaced by external achievement → we spend our adult lives performing instead of being. Your most developed written argument.
- Hook: "Every time your mate took the piss out of you at school, your nervous system learned a lesson it never forgot."
- **Format:** Blog/newsletter + video series (3 parts)
- **Confidence:** 10/10 — Full manifesto exists. Banter-to-armour pipeline fully mapped. Personal examples ready. The Ralph Lauren line is gold. Could be a TED talk.
- **Tier:** 1

### 3. The Water Model (Public Version)
*Source: The Water Model doc + Thought Notes 2025-04-12*
"Healing = river that flows. Trauma creates the flow to stop or rampage. Being whole = being able to flow without restraint." Simplify the Water Model for a general audience. The watcher is the water. Expression is the native state. Safety un-dams it.
- Hook: "You don't need to find yourself. You need to un-dam yourself."
- **Format:** Animated carousel (the 6-line compression)
- **Confidence:** 9/10 — Full philosophical document exists. Challenge is simplifying without losing it. Don't try to explain the whole model. Just the compression.
- **Tier:** 2

### 4. Hustle Culture Is Missing Half the Equation
*Source: Thought Notes 2025-05-18*
"You fall to the level of your habits + safety. If your ambitions feel safe you actually don't need habits because you'll make time to do the things you love. Habits feel forced. Feeling safe and allowing your love to guide is flow." A direct challenge to the productivity-bro content machine.
- Hook: "James Clear is right about habits. He's just missing the other half."
- **Format:** Carousel (framework) + reel (hot take)
- **Confidence:** 8/10 — The thought note IS a complete argument. Safety x Expression equation gives you the framework visual. Honour Clear's work while extending it. Two-part: reel as hot take, carousel as explanation.
- **Tier:** 2

### 5. We've Never Been More Accessible, Never Been Less Connected
*Source: Thought Notes 2024-04-12*
"Connection means being with someone in person. 'Connecting' to someone online isn't 'connecting' with them, it's accessing them." Redefine connection for the age of algorithms. This is Enemy #4 (Wellness-as-Calm) extended to technology.
- Hook: "You have 2,000 followers and no one to call at 2am."
- **Format:** Reel (spoken word, camera close, 30 seconds)
- **Confidence:** 6/10 — Strong one-liner but thin on supporting material. Don't stretch it. The power is in the brevity.
- **Tier:** 3

## Series Concepts (multi-post)

### "What 2021-Me Was Reading vs What 2026-Me Built"
5-part series mapping each Miro book cluster to the specific Vibe Rise feature it became. Visual: book cover → app screenshot. Shows the intellectual lineage.
- **Format:** Carousel (book cover → screenshot)
- **Confidence:** 9/10 — All material exists in [[Intellectual Lineage Map]]. 5 posts, minimal creation. Visual format designs itself.
- **Tier:** 1

### "Thought Notes: Raw Transmissions"
Weekly series sharing one unedited thought note with a single line of 2026 commentary. No polish. The messiness IS the point. Shows the journey.
- **Format:** Story/reel (screenshot of Apple Note + 30s voiceover)
- **Confidence:** 10/10 — Zero production needed. Screenshot the note, record commentary. Authenticity IS the format.
- **Tier:** 1

### "The Attachment Style You Don't Know You Have"
6-part series: one per domain (Money, Visibility, Rest, Intimacy, Authority, Creativity). Each post: scenario, 3 response options, reveal. Drives toward the Grip Map feature.
- **Format:** Carousel (interactive quiz style)
- **Confidence:** 7/10 — Framework built in [[Future Features]] doc. Need to write the specific scenarios for each domain. 6 posts, each standalone.
- **Tier:** 2

### "12 Ingredients of Wisdom" (School of Life remix)
Carousel series: one ingredient per post (Realism, Resilience, Envy, Folly, Regrets, Politeness, Humour, Self-Acceptance, Forgiveness, Calm, Appreciation, Success & Failure). Your take on de Botton's wisdom framework through the Vibe Rise lens.
- **Format:** Carousel (one per post)
- **Confidence:** 6/10 — Source material from [[School of Life - Culture]]. But 12 is a lot and risks repackaging de Botton. Cherry-pick 4-5 that connect to Vibe Rise.
- **Tier:** Park (cherry-pick only)
