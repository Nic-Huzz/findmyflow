# Carousel: The Installation Map

*7-slide carousel for Instagram. Part of the play-list challenge (one fun thing a day).*
*Follows the self-actualisation table post. Leads to the Belief Graph post next.*

---

## Context

This carousel introduces the Installation Map: the idea that your protective voice wasn't chosen, it was installed by events between birth and today. It walks through the four wound stages showing HOW the installation happened, then reveals the nervous system pattern underneath, and lands on hope.

**Arc:** Hook → Evidence (x4) → Pattern → Hope + CTA

**Story structure:** Same as Hormozi chapter pattern: Story → Lesson → Framework → Takeaway

## Slides

### Slide 1: The Hook

**Text:**
"You didn't wake up today and decide to have a protective voice and a limiting belief.

Something happened between the time you were born and today that installed it."

**Design:** Dark purple background. White text. No image. Let the words land.

---

### Slide 2: Stage 1 — You Arrive

**Text:**
"Stage 1: You arrive.

You needed two things: to be seen and to be safe.

If you got both, you could explore. If you got seen but never settled, your nervous system learned: the world is chaos, stay alert. If you were safe but never seen, it learned: I'm invisible. Don't bother."

**Design:** Pixar-style image of infancy. Three possible scenes: a child in a storm (sympathetic), a child in a warm home (ventral), a child alone in a quiet room (dorsal). Show all three or pick the most emotionally resonant single image.

---

### Slide 3: Stage 2 — You Learn What Works

**Text:**
"Stage 2: You learn what works.

You expressed yourself. The world responded.

If your full expression cost you connection, you learned: being myself is dangerous. Edit. Perform. Adapt."

**Design:** Pixar-style image of childhood. A child reaching out, one hand receiving warmth, the other being pushed away. Or: a child performing for applause vs. a child hiding behind a mask.

---

### Slide 4: Stage 3 — School Installs the OS

**Text:**
"Stage 3: School installs the operating system.

Sit down. Be quiet. Memorise. Perform. Get graded.

Some kids rebel. Their essence survives but at social cost. Most kids comply. They get praised. And lose themselves so gradually they don't notice."

**Design:** Pixar-style image of school. Rows of identical desks. One desk empty, chair pushed back. Or: a line of uniform kids with one glowing faintly different.

---

### Slide 5: Stage 4 — Your Friends Decide the Rest

**Text:**
"Stage 4: Your friend group decides the rest.

If your group accepted who you really were, you kept your shape. If belonging required you to change, you changed.

By the time you're done, three things have happened: your essence got buried, your direction got hijacked, and a protective voice got installed to make sure neither ever surfaces."

**Design:** Pixar-style image of teens. A group of silhouettes, one glowing faintly different. Or: a person looking in a mirror, reflection showing an edited version.

---

### Slide 6: The Pattern — Nervous System Graph

**Text:**
"Every graph. Every domain. The same three zones.

Your nervous system only has two threat responses. Fight, or disappear."

**Graph layout:**

Standard FindMyFlow graph format with diagonal.

**Y axis:** Energised (how alive you feel)
**X axis:** Safety (how safe your body feels)

| Zone | Position | Archetypes | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sympathetic** | Top-left | Controller (fight), Ghost (flight) | Energised but unsafe. Lots of energy because the body doesn't feel safe. |
| **Ventral** | Diagonal | — | Energised AND safe. Flow. Play. The destination. |
| **Dorsal** | Bottom-right | Perfectionist (freeze), Auto-Pilot (collapse) | "Safe" but dead inside. The absence of risk, not the presence of safety. |

**PROVISIONAL: This is a restructured archetype mapping (Apr 2026). Four primary archetypes replace the previous five (Performer merged into Controller, Auto-Pilot is new). Not yet validated against zone calibration framework or app codebase.**

**Design:** Clean graph on purple/dark background. Gold diagonal line. Energised (Y) and Safety (X) axis labels. Archetype names in their zones. Consistent with zone calibration framework visual style.

**Punchline for this slide:** "Your nervous system only had two options: activate or shut down. And within each, two directions: toward or away."

---

### Slide 7: The Reframe + CTA

**Text:**
"It was installed. Not chosen.

Your protective voice isn't who you are. It's software that was installed by an event. A bodyguard that doesn't know the war is over.

The essence is still underneath. Buried, not broken."

**CTA:**
"Find yours → findmyflow.nichuzz.com"

**Design:** Warm transition from purple to gold. The hope slide. Could include the Installation Map diagram (three boxes: Essence → Wound → Protection) as a small visual, or keep it text-only for maximum emotional impact. FindMyFlow logo + URL at bottom.

## Image Generation Notes

For slides 2-5, Pixar-style AI images should be:
- Warm, emotional, cinematic lighting
- Characters that feel universal (not specific gender/ethnicity)
- Each image captures the FEELING of the stage, not a literal scene
- Consistent art style across all four (same Pixar universe)
- Generated via Gemini 3.1 Flash (same pipeline as Essence Mirror images)

For slide 6, the graph should match the zone calibration framework visual style used in other FindMyFlow materials.
