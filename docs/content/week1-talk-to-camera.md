# Week 1: Talk to Camera (30-60 sec each)

Rules: No script. Read the prompt, think for 10 seconds, hit record, talk. One take. Post it.

---

## Day 1 (Mon) — The Thesis

**Hook:** "You don't rise to the level of your ambitions. You fall to the level that feels safe."

**Talk about:** You wanted to quit your job for two years. You had the savings. You had the plan. Nothing happened. Your dome of safety kept you exactly where you were. The desire existed outside the dome. Two years of "I'll do it next month" until a weekly fear challenge cracked it open.

**Land on:** "Your ambitions aren't the problem. Your nervous system's thermostat is."

*Source: Thought Notes 2025-05-18, Storybank "The 2-Year Stall"*

---

## Day 2 (Tue) — Why It Doesn't Feel Safe

**Hook:** "Let me tell you what happened the day I wore a rainbow to school."

**Talk about:** You were the kid who expressed joy through colour. Other boys laughed. You stopped. By your early 20s, no colour, no playfulness, no dancing. All the things that now define you were shut down by 14. That's not a choice you made. That's your nervous system learning "being yourself = danger."

**Land on:** "Every time your mate took the piss, your body learned a lesson it never forgot."

*Source: Storybank "Rainbow Clothes", Thought Notes 2023-08-02*

---

## Day 3 (Wed) — Procrastination Isn't Laziness

**Hook:** "Procrastination isn't a discipline problem. It's a safety problem."

**Talk about:** You procrastinate because taking action doesn't feel safe. You take action at the last minute because THEN the thought of not delivering becomes more unsafe than doing the thing. Your body is always choosing the safest option. It's not broken. It's protecting you from something you haven't processed yet.

**Land on:** "Next time you're procrastinating, don't ask 'why am I lazy.' Ask 'what about this doesn't feel safe.'"

*Source: Thought Notes 2025-01-08*

---

## Day 4 (Thu) — Your Nervous System Has a Price Tag

**Hook:** "There's a level of money your nervous system can handle. And you're probably sitting at it right now."

**Talk about:** You remember staring at a headset invoice and your business brain said it was fine but your body went into full alarm mode. Palms sweating over a number that made complete sense on paper. Your income ceiling isn't a strategy problem or a hustle problem. It's a nervous system problem. Your body has a set point for how much success it'll allow before it triggers a cool-down.

**Land on:** "You can't out-strategy a nervous system that doesn't feel safe earning more."

*Source: Thought Notes 2024-08-12*

---

## Day 5 (Fri) — What Safe Actually Feels Like

**Hook:** "I never understood quotes like 'do what sets your heart on fire' until I stood on a beach with headphones in, dancing while people stared."

**Talk about:** That was your first Wahoo. The first time you did something that scared you AND felt alive at the same time. Not comfortable. Not reckless. That exact edge where your body is saying "this is new" but not "this is danger." That's what safe enough to be yourself actually feels like. Not the absence of fear. The presence of aliveness alongside it.

**Land on:** "Safe doesn't mean comfortable. Safe means your body trusts you enough to let you be weird."

*Source: Thought Notes 2025-01-18, Content Topic #27*

---

## Day 6 (Sat) — The Mask You Forgot You're Wearing

**Hook:** "I was so agreeable I didn't know what music I liked. I just liked what the room liked."

**Talk about:** People-pleasing isn't kindness. It's identity erasure. You spent years fitting into every room so well that you lost track of who walks in when nobody's watching. You built products so people would like you, not pay you. You avoided hard conversations because their discomfort felt more dangerous than your own. That's not generosity. That's a survival strategy from when love felt conditional.

**Land on:** "You're not kind. You're safe. There's a difference."

*Source: Thought Notes 2022-11-01, 2024-07-27, 2025-05-31*

---

## Day 7 (Sun) — What I'd Tell Myself 4 Years Ago

**Hook:** "My search for purpose was a search to end my suffering."

**Talk about:** 4 years ago you were spraying energy in every direction trying to find happiness. $30K on courses. Quit your job. Moved to Bali. Tried to think your way to peace. Here's what nobody told you: you don't find yourself. You un-dam yourself. Everything you were looking for was already there. It was just buried under 25 years of "don't be too much, don't be too loud, don't be too you."

**Land on:** "You don't need another course. You need to feel safe being the person you already are."

*Source: Thought Notes 2022-09-26, 2022-11-01, Storybank "The Corporate Quit"*

## The Arc

| Day | Theme | Emotional beat |
|-----|-------|---------------|
| Mon | The thesis | Recognition ("that's me") |
| Tue | Where it started | Compassion ("oh, that's why") |
| Wed | How it shows up daily | Relief ("I'm not broken") |
| Thu | How it shows up in money | Confrontation ("damn") |
| Fri | What the other side feels like | Hope ("I want that") |
| Sat | The mask | Vulnerability ("I do that too") |
| Sun | Full circle | Permission ("maybe I can stop") |

## Posting notes

- Film vertical, natural light, no ring light. Look slightly messy.
- No captions on screen. Just you talking.
- Caption in the post = the hook line + "Day X/7" 
- No CTA for the first week. Just talk. Let people find you.
- If you stumble or restart mid-sentence, leave it in. The mess is the trust signal.
