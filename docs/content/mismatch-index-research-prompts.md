# Mismatch Index — Research Prompts for Perplexity

Use these prompts on Perplexity (free or Pro). Each is designed to extract SPECIFIC NUMBERS with citations needed to build the Mismatch Index.

**Goal**: For each branch, get the ancestral baseline number AND the modern average number so we can calculate deviation %.

**Tip**: On free plan, run 1-2 per session. On Pro, batch them. Follow up each answer with "What are the specific sample sizes and methodologies of the studies you cited?" to verify quality.

---

## 1. Movement (target metric: daily steps + movement types)

**Prompt:**
```
I need exact numbers from peer-reviewed studies on hunter-gatherer daily movement:

1. What is the measured average daily step count for the Hadza people? (Cite the specific Pontzer et al. study, year, sample size, and methodology — was it accelerometer-based?)
2. What types of movement do Hadza engage in daily and what percentage of time is each? (sustained walking vs sprinting vs carrying vs squatting)
3. What is the current average American daily step count? (Cite the specific CDC or NHANES study, year, sample size)
4. What percentage of Americans meet WHO physical activity guidelines?

I need actual numbers, not summaries. Include confidence intervals if available.
```

**Follow-up prompt:**
```
For the Hadza movement studies you cited, what were the limitations noted by the authors? Were there differences between dry season and wet season? Between men and women? What other hunter-gatherer groups have been studied with accelerometers and how do their results compare?
```

## 2. Food (target metric: dietary diversity + processing level)

**Prompt:**
```
I need exact numbers comparing ancestral vs modern diet:

1. How many distinct plant species do the Hadza consume annually? (Cite specific ethnobotanical studies with sample sizes)
2. How many distinct plant species does the average American consume annually? (Cite dietary survey data)
3. What percentage of American calories come from ultra-processed food? (Cite the most recent NHANES analysis — I believe Juul et al. or Martinez-Steele et al.)
4. What was the estimated dietary diversity of pre-agricultural humans based on archaeological evidence?
5. What is the NOVA classification breakdown of the average American diet (% unprocessed, % processed, % ultra-processed)?

Include study years, sample sizes, and any methodological notes.
```

**Follow-up:**
```
What is the relationship between dietary diversity and health outcomes? Is there a dose-response curve — does health improve linearly with more species consumed, or is there a threshold? Cite the most rigorous studies.
```

## 3. Bonds (target metric: close relationships + loneliness)

**Prompt:**
```
I need exact numbers on social connection baseline vs current:

1. What are the specific Dunbar number layers? (5, 15, 50, 150 — cite the original Dunbar 1992 paper and any updates)
2. What is the average number of close confidants reported by American adults? (Cite the GSS "important matters" question data, McPherson et al. 2006 and any more recent replication)
3. What percentage of Americans report zero close friends? How has this changed from 1990 to present?
4. US Surgeon General 2023 Advisory: what were the exact statistics on loneliness prevalence, health equivalence (cigarettes/day claim), and premature death risk increase?
5. What is the average Dunbar-layer composition of a typical modern American's social network?

I need the exact survey questions used, sample sizes, and response rates.
```

**Follow-up:**
```
Is Dunbar's number empirically validated or theoretical? What are the strongest criticisms? Has any study directly measured whether groups larger than 150 actually lose cohesion?
```

## 4. Story (target metric: narrative consumption pattern)

**Prompt:**
```
I need exact data on ancestral vs modern story consumption:

1. Wiessner 2014 PNAS study on Ju/'hoansi Bushmen: what exact percentage of evening conversation was storytelling vs other topics? What was the sample size and observation period?
2. What is the average daily screen time for American adults? (Cite most recent Nielsen or eMarketer data)
3. What is the average TikTok session length? Average video watch duration before scrolling?
4. What is the average podcast listening duration per session? Per week? (Cite Edison Research or Spotify data)
5. What is the average attention span for digital content? (Note: the "8 seconds" claim is widely cited but poorly sourced — what does the actual research say?)

I need primary sources, not secondary citations of secondary citations.
```

**Follow-up:**
```
Is there neuroscience research comparing brain activation during oral storytelling vs short-form video vs long-form podcast? Specifically looking at Uri Hasson's neural coupling research at Princeton — what were the findings on speaker-listener brain synchronisation?
```

## 5. Shelter (target metric: time indoors + thermal variation)

**Prompt:**
```
I need exact data on modern indoor living vs ancestral shelter patterns:

1. EPA "Report on the Environment": what percentage of time does the average American spend indoors? What was the study methodology and sample size?
2. What is the evidence on health effects of constant indoor temperature (thermoneutral zone) vs thermal variation? (Cite studies on brown fat activation, metabolic rate, and cold/heat exposure)
3. What percentage of modern homes maintain temperature within 2°C variation throughout the day?
4. What is the spectral composition of modern LED lighting vs firelight, and what is the measured effect on melatonin onset timing? (Cite specific lux and kelvin measurements)
5. What archaeological evidence exists on hunter-gatherer shelter design — thermal properties, ventilation, communal vs individual sleeping?
```

## 6. Status (target metric: basis of status signalling)

**Prompt:**
```
What anthropological research exists on how status is earned and signalled in hunter-gatherer societies vs modern industrial societies?

1. In Hadza, !Kung, and other studied groups: is status primarily based on skill demonstration, generosity, physical prowess, or social manipulation? Cite specific ethnographic studies.
2. What is Thorstein Veblen's "conspicuous consumption" thesis and when did consumption-based status emerge historically?
3. What survey data exists on what modern adults consider "high status"? Has this shifted in the last 10 years toward skill/authenticity vs material consumption?
4. What is the market size of the "conscious consumer" or "anti-fast-fashion" segment?
```

## 7. Threat (target metric: stress type + resolution)

**Prompt:**
```
I need data comparing ancestral vs modern threat/stress patterns:

1. What is the evidence that hunter-gatherer stress responses were acute and self-resolving vs modern chronic unresolvable stress? Cite Sapolsky's "Why Zebras Don't Get Ulcers" and any empirical studies.
2. What percentage of modern adults report persistent anxiety about threats they cannot personally act on? (terrorism, climate, economic collapse)
3. What is the physiological difference between acute stress (cortisol spike + clearance) and chronic stress (sustained cortisol)? Health consequences of each?
4. What percentage of adults report feeling they have "no personal control" over major threats in their life? (Cite Gallup, Pew, or similar)
```

## 8. Energy (target metric: energy relationship + circadian disruption)

**Prompt:**
```
I need data on the human relationship with energy sources, ancestrally vs modern:

1. When did humans first control fire? What is the current archaeological consensus? (Cite Berna et al., Wonderwerk Cave, or more recent findings)
2. What is the spectral profile of a campfire (colour temperature, lux at 1 metre) vs a 60W LED bulb? What is the measured difference in melatonin suppression?
3. What percentage of Americans use any form of circadian-appropriate evening lighting (warm spectrum, blue-light reduction)?
4. What is the global candle market size? What percentage of candle purchases are for "ambiance/relaxation" vs practical lighting?
5. What percentage of US homes have rooftop solar? How has this changed 2015-2025?
```

## 9. Healing (target metric: preventive vs reactive healthcare)

**Prompt:**
```
I need data comparing preventive vs reactive healthcare spending and practice:

1. What percentage of US healthcare spending goes to chronic disease management vs prevention? (Cite CDC or CMS data)
2. What percentage of hunter-gatherer healthcare practices were preventive/communal (daily movement, herbal tonics, social bonding, ceremony) vs reactive (wound care, bone setting)?
3. What is the current market size of the "preventive health" or "wellness" market globally? (Cite Global Wellness Institute or McKinsey)
4. What percentage of Americans engage in regular preventive health practices (meditation, breathwork, regular exercise, dietary management) vs relying solely on reactive medicine?
```

## 10. Meta-Theory: Evolutionary Mismatch

**Prompt:**
```
I need a comprehensive overview of "evolutionary mismatch" theory in academic literature:

1. Who are the key researchers? (Daniel Lieberman at Harvard, Herman Pontzer at Duke, Loren Cordain, Sebastian Junger on social mismatch)
2. What are the strongest empirical claims? Which mismatch domains have the most evidence?
3. What are the main academic criticisms of mismatch theory? (Is it unfalsifiable? Does it cherry-pick ancestral data? Is the "ancestral baseline" a myth?)
4. Has anyone proposed a quantitative "mismatch index" before? If so, what methodology did they use?
5. What peer-reviewed papers directly use the term "evolutionary mismatch" and have been cited more than 100 times?
```

## How to Use the Results

Once you have the numbers from all 10 prompts, the Mismatch Index for each branch is:

```
Deviation % = (Ancestral Baseline - Modern Average) / Ancestral Baseline × 100
```

Example: Movement = (12,000 steps - 4,000 steps) / 12,000 × 100 = 67% deviation

The hypothesis: higher deviation % correlates with larger Phase 3 market size and earlier Phase 3 tipping point.
