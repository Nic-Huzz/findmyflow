Somewhere along the way, we collect emotional splinters.

Experiences that made being ourselves feel unsafe.

Our nervous system = our safety system.

So to stop this from happening again, it created protective patterns.

3/4

At 14 I was a playful, dancing, rainbow. I wore fluro pinks, blues, oranges, purples and uploaded videos of me dancing on Facebook.

Then I got teased for it.

So for the next TWELVE YEARS I never wore rainbow clothes or danced sober again.

Basically the things that make me feel most like me.

4/4

This is what breaks my heart about emotional splinters.

They leave us scared to be ourselves.

And this suppression causes depression.
