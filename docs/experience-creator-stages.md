# Experience Creator Stages

## How FindMyFlow Maps to the Experience Creator Journey

The /7-day-challenge page handles Stages 1-5. The Create tab + group collective handles Stage 6+.

---

## Stage 1: "I want to create experiences but I don't know what mine is"

**Problems:**
- I have skills but no shaped experience
- I've attended 20 workshops, I know what I like, but I can't articulate MY thing
- I don't have a methodology, I have vibes

**App solution (7-day-challenge page):**
- PlaySkillsOnboarding discovers play-skills (placemakes)
- Play-List tab: Groan Matrix builds courage through visibility layers
- Level tab: Journey progression builds self-knowledge
- Essence Mirror: Archetype discovery ("I feel so seen")

---

## Stage 2: "I've designed something but I'm scared to put it out there"

**Problems:**
- I know what I'd run but I haven't told anyone
- Marketing feels gross, salesy, not me
- I keep postponing setting a date
- "What if nobody comes?"
- "What if they come and it doesn't work?"

**App solution (7-day-challenge page):**
- Play-List challenges at Money/Vulnerable/Authority layers
- Voice check-ins (essence vs protective pattern recognition)
- Groan completion flow: scary/wahoo scoring, 3% improvement
- The courage muscle IS the solution to Stage 2

---

## Stage 3: "I have an experience coming up and I'm drowning in the to-do list"

**Problems:**
- I don't know what order to do things
- I always forget something
- The admin overwhelms me and kills my creative energy
- I spend all my time on logistics and none on facilitation prep

**App solution (7-day-challenge page → Create tab):**
- Experience Blueprint: Shift Architecture designs the experience
- Experience checklist: 31 pre-seeded items (marketing + organisation)
- Checklist → Play-List bridge: passive to-dos become courage challenges with deadlines

---

## Stage 4: "I just ran my experience and now what?"

**Problems:**
- I forgot to collect feedback
- I didn't get testimonials while people were still buzzing
- I have no system for follow-up
- I don't know what worked and what didn't

**App solution (7-day-challenge page → Create tab):**
- Post-event checklist: follow-up (6 items) + reflection (4 items)
- 3% improvement note: "What one thing will you do differently?"
- Post-event checklist items as Play-List challenges (send thank-you, collect testimonials)

---

## Stage 5: "I want to do it again but better"

**Problems:**
- I can't remember what I'd do differently
- Each experience feels like starting from scratch
- I'm not compounding, I'm repeating
- I still hate the marketing part

**App solution (7-day-challenge page → Create tab):**
- 3% note from last experience surfaces when creating the next one
- Experience chain: each experience links to its predecessor
- Blueprint carries forward (edit, don't redesign)
- AI content generator creates marketing from the blueprint

---

## Stage 6+: "I want this to be a real thing, not a side project"

**THIS IS WHAT WE'RE BUILDING NOW.**

**Problems:**
- I have no data on my growth
- I can't see the trajectory
- I don't know my numbers (attendees, repeat rate, revenue)
- I'm isolated, no peers doing the same thing
- I'm still doing everything manually
- I want accountability but not a boss

**Solution: The Create tab as a full creator OS + Shift Architecture Collective (group)**

This is documented in `docs/experience-creator-os.md`.

---

## The Handoff Point

Stages 1-5 live in /7-day-challenge. They're about becoming an experience creator.

Stage 6+ lives in the Create tab + CRM + group collective. It's about growing as one.

The user doesn't "graduate" from one to the other. They coexist. A Stage 6 creator still uses Play-List for courage challenges. They still do healing work. But their primary operating mode shifts from self-discovery to professional facilitation with a system behind it.
