# Hero's Journey Workshop — 2-Hour Key Beats

**Created:** 2026-07-11
**Status:** Beats defined, needs structure writing
**Purpose:** A 2-hour live experience that walks participants through all 12 hero's journey stages. Each stage has a key beat the facilitator must hit.

---

## The Promise

"In 2 hours, you'll go from stuck to seeing your path clearly. Not through advice. Through experience."

---

## Stage Beats

### Stage 1: The Matrix (5 min)
**Beat:** Recognition. "This is where you've been."

- Name the constructed life without judging it
- Participants feel SEEN in their current reality ("successful on paper, empty inside")
- Language: "The life that looks right but doesn't feel right"
- The room goes quiet because they've never heard someone say it out loud
- NO solutions yet. Just mirroring.

**Must hit:** The collective exhale. "Someone finally said it."

---

### Stage 2: The Earthquake (5 min)
**Beat:** Permission. "Something cracked. That's why you're here."

- Acknowledge that something brought them to this room
- Don't ask them to share it yet (too vulnerable, too early)
- Normalise the crack: "Everyone in this room had a moment"
- Frame it as the beginning, not the breakdown: "The crack is where the light gets in"
- Establish safety: what happens here stays here

**Must hit:** The sense that being here isn't weakness. The earthquake was the first brave thing.

---

### Stage 3: Head Full of Dreams / The Refusal (10 min)
**Beat:** Diagnosis. "You can see it but you can't move toward it."

- Name the paralysis: "You know what you want. You've read the books. Watched the podcasts. But you can't DO it."
- Introduce the gap between knowing and doing
- Small exercise: "Write down one thing you KNOW you want but haven't acted on"
- Don't solve it. Just name it. The naming IS the work here.
- Introduce the concept: this gap has a guardian (protective voice) — we'll meet them later

**Must hit:** Participants write something real. The thing they've been avoiding. On paper, it becomes less scary.

---

### Stage 4: Meeting the Mentor (10 min)
**Beat:** Naming. "Let me show you who you already are."

- Quick essence identification exercise (condensed version of Essence Mirror)
- Could be: card selection, archetype quiz, or facilitator reads the room and reflects
- The gift: vocabulary they've never had for their experience
- "You're not broken. You're a [archetype] whose voice was suppressed."
- Introduce the future self concept: "The version of you who already did this is trying to talk to you"

**Must hit:** "I feel so seen." The moment they get NAMED and it lands. Goosebumps, nodding, maybe tears.

---

### Stage 5: Crossing the Threshold (15 min)
**Beat:** First action. "You're going to feel alive for the first time in a while."

- A live courage challenge. In the room. Right now.
- Must be calibrated: scary enough to be real, safe enough to survive
- Examples: share your Stage 3 "thing" with a stranger. Stand up and say it out loud. Make eye contact with someone for 60 seconds.
- The Vibe Rise moment: they feel it in their BODY, not just their mind
- Post-challenge: "How did that feel?" Name the state (Vibe Rise/Fun/Pressure)
- Identity statement: "Now that I did that, I've proven I'm someone who..."

**Must hit:** At least one person in the room feels Vibe Rise. The taste of another world. "THAT'S what this feels like."

---

### Stage 6: Tests, Allies, Enemies (15 min)
**Beat:** Repetition + community. "You're not alone in this. And it gets easier."

- Second challenge (slightly harder than the first — build on the threshold)
- Pair up: do it WITH someone. The ally structure.
- Introduce the protective voices: "There's a reason this is hard. Let me name the 5 characters..."
- Quick identification: "Which voice showed up just now when I asked you to do that?"
- The league/competition element: friendly challenge between pairs or groups
- Normalise the "enemies": they're not evil, they're old protectors

**Must hit:** Bonding. The room becomes a team, not strangers. They've done two scary things together. Laughter. Energy up.

---

### Stage 7: Approach to the Inmost Cave (15 min)
**Beat:** Pattern reveal. "Now I'm going to show you what's REALLY keeping you stuck."

- Guided reflection: "The thing you wrote in Stage 3... the voice that appeared in Stage 6... they're connected."
- Facilitate the connection: "Your [protective voice] developed because..."
- Origin story mapping: brief exercise tracing the voice to its first appearance
- "When was the first time being [visible/imperfect/authentic/vulnerable/still] felt dangerous?"
- The room gets quiet again. Different quiet from Stage 1. Deeper.
- Frame what comes next: "You've found the cave. The thing inside isn't as big as you think."

**Must hit:** The "oh" moment. They see the PATTERN, not just the symptom. "That's why I can't..."

---

### Stage 8: The Ordeal (20 min)
**Beat:** The reconsolidation moment. "Let's dissolve it. Right here."

- This is the core of Huzz's method. The memory reconsolidation container.
- Guided experience (breathwork? movement? somatic release? facilitated vulnerability exercise?)
- The structure: Safety → Activation → Mismatch + Discharge → Integration → Verification
- The old pattern is confronted in a NEW context (safe, witnessed, with community)
- The mismatch: "Last time this happened, you were alone / small / powerless. This time you're not."
- The discharge: the body releases what the mind couldn't
- NOT therapy. NOT re-traumatisation. A SHIFT in how the memory is held.

**Must hit:** Something physically shifts. Tears, laughter, shaking, deep breath, stillness. The body lets go of something it was holding. At least 60% of the room should feel a tangible release.

---

### Stage 9: The Reward / Flow Statement (10 min)
**Beat:** Clarity. "Now that the block is gone, what do you see?"

- Post-reconsolidation, the fog clears. Curiosities that seemed separate now connect.
- Guided exercise: "Look at the thing you wrote in Stage 3. Does it look different now?"
- Prompt: "What's the thread that connects everything you care about?"
- Flow Statement creation: "I [verb] [who] by [how] so that [transformation]"
- Or simpler: "The path only I can walk is ___"
- Share with partner/group (the ally from Stage 6)
- The naming makes it real. Once spoken, it exists.

**Must hit:** At least one person in the room says their Flow Statement and the room RESPONDS. Nods. "Yes." Energy. The statement lands because it's TRUE, not because it's polished.

---

### Stage 10: The Road Back / Aligned Action (10 min)
**Beat:** Direction. "Now what do you DO with this on Monday morning?"

- Three paths presented (no hierarchy):
  - Path A: Take your Flow Statement to your current role. "Here's what I need to change."
  - Path B: Use it as a compass for a new role. "Here's what I'm looking for."
  - Path C: Build something around it. "Here's what I'm creating."
- Quick exercise: "Which path? Write down ONE action for this week."
- The action must be concrete, time-bound, and small enough to actually do
- Introduce the Proof Harvester concept: "Everything you just experienced? That's your credibility."

**Must hit:** Everyone leaves with a SPECIFIC next step, not a vague intention. Written down. Shared with an ally who will check in.

---

### Stage 11: Resurrection (5 min)
**Beat:** Proof. "You already have what you need."

- Reflect back what just happened: "2 hours ago you walked in stuck. Right now you have a Flow Statement and a next step."
- Frame the journey they just did AS the credibility: "You went through the 12 stages in this room. That experience? It's the proof."
- For Path C people: "Your story IS your marketing. What you felt tonight? Your clients need to feel that."
- Identity reinforcement: "You're not the same person who walked in."

**Must hit:** The contrast between who they were at Stage 1 and who they are now is VISIBLE to them. "I actually am different."

---

### Stage 12: Return with the Elixir (5 min)
**Beat:** Mission. "You leave here with something the world needs."

- Close the loop: "The Matrix is still out there. Your desk will still be there Monday. But YOU are different."
- The elixir = the Flow Statement + the felt experience + the proof that another state exists
- Community anchor: "The people in this room walked the same journey. Stay connected."
- Introduce Vibe Rise app / next steps (natural, not salesy): "If you want to keep going..."
- Final moment: everyone stands, shares their Flow Statement in one sentence with the room
- The last person speaks. Silence. Then energy.

**Must hit:** They leave ACTIVATED, not just informed. The felt sense of "I know what to do and I believe I can" persists past the car park, past bedtime, into Monday morning.

## Key Principles

1. **Build trust before vulnerability.** Stages 1-4 are EARNING the right to ask for Stage 8.
2. **Body before mind.** The threshold (Stage 5) must be FELT, not just understood.
3. **The room is the ally.** Pair-work in Stage 6 creates the safety container for Stage 8.
4. **Don't explain the framework.** They don't need to know it's "Campbell's 12 stages." They just need to FEEL the arc.
5. **The Flow Statement must be SPOKEN, not just written.** Speaking it makes it real. The room witnessing makes it stick.
6. **Leave with action, not just insight.** Stage 10 must produce a concrete next step or the transformation evaporates.
7. **The facilitator's job is to HOLD, not PERFORM.** The participants are the heroes. You are the mentor.
