# Phase 1 Intro Slides — v2

## Design Notes
- Mobile-first, full-screen slides
- Swipe or tap to advance
- Purple→gold gradient background
- Each slide: one idea, one visual, minimal text
- Tone: exciting, simple, zero jargon
- Target: someone who just landed on the app or /try/experience-game
- 5 slides total. Arc: wonder → purpose → belief → mechanism → action

---

## Slide 1 — Wonder

**Visual:** Globe or world illustration, glowing with warmth

**Text:**
> Life is the most magical game in the world.

---

## Slide 2 — Purpose

**Visual:** Person in different experiences (montage: dancing, cooking, climbing, laughing with friends)

**Text:**
> The purpose of the game?

> Have experiences you love.

---

## Slide 3 — Belief

**Visual:** Branching paths diverging, one path glowing warmly

**Text:**
> We believe there's a life path that is uniquely yours.

---

## Slide 4 — Mechanism

**Visual:** App UI hint, playful game-like elements, progress indicators

**Text:**
> This app gamifies finding that path.

---

## Slide 5 — Invitation (CTA)

**Visual:** Empty dome/radar shape waiting to be filled, pulsing gently

**Text:**
> Let's start with what you've already tried.

**Button:** Start playing →

---

## Open Questions

1. Do we name the game? "The Experience Game"? Or just let the framing speak?
2. Should slide 1 reference "Find My Flow" branding or stay brand-free for the /try/ lead magnet version?
3. Animation between slides? Gentle build from cool to warm colours across the 5 slides.
4. Slide 4 — "gamifies" is a specific word. Does the audience know it? Alternative: "This app turns finding that path into a game."
