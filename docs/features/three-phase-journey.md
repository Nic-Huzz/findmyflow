# The Three-Phase Journey

## The Game

Life is the most magical game in the world. The purpose of the game is to have experiences you love. We believe there is a life path that is uniquely yours. This app turns finding that path into a game.

## The Measurement

The nervous system is the constant measurement across all three phases. Not salary, title, or company logo. How your body responds to an experience tells you more about your path than any career test.

Four states:
- **Vibe Rise** — "I feel ALIVE doing this"
- **Fun** — "Yeah, that was good"  
- **Stressful** — "I can do it but it costs me"
- **Uninterested** — "Not for me"

---

## Phase 1: Discovery — "What lights me up?"

**The question:** What experiences do I love?

**The game:** The Experience Dome is a bucket list of ~380 experiences across 12 branches of human experience (Movement, Healing, Food, Bonds, Fire, Play, Story, Rest, Status, Shelter, Threat, Tools). Users tick what they've experienced, rate each with their nervous system response, and see their unique dome shape emerge.

**Courage at this phase:** Trying something new. Every unticked experience is an invitation.

**The score:** How many experiences you've tried + how many put you in a Vibe Rise state.

**When you're ready to move on:** You know which branches light you up. You have a dome shape. You're no longer guessing what makes you come alive.

**Product:** Free. Available at `/experience-game` and `/try/experience-game` (lead magnet). No sign-up required to play. Sign-up to save results.

---

## Phase 2: Expansion — "How far can I take what I love?"

**The question:** Can I expand my capacity within the experiences I love?

**The game:** You know what lights you up. Now courage is about expanding the DIMENSIONS of those experiences. The same experience, pushed further: more people, higher stakes, new locations, greater vulnerability, charging money for the first time.

**The primary focus:** Improving your craft, ability, and skill. Showing up enough to prove to yourself that you want to do this as a career.

**The 7 expansion dimensions** (craft first, scale follows naturally):

*Craft dimensions (primary):*
1. **Duration** — from 10 minutes to full days to multi-day retreats
2. **Frequency** — from once to weekly to daily practice
3. **Medium** — from in-person to online to hybrid to recorded
4. **People** — from solo to small group to crowd (skill-wise)

*Scale dimensions (secondary, grow organically through craft):*
5. **Money** — from free to charging to premium pricing
6. **Location** — from your living room to new cities to new countries
7. **Independence** — from employed to freelance to running your own thing

The scale dimensions grow as a byproduct of craft. As you get better and show up consistently, word of mouth, attendee prestige, and revenue follow. Phase 2 tracks these as courage milestones ("I charged for the first time"), not business metrics.

**The line between Phase 2 and Phase 3:**
- Phase 2 = "Can I?" (courage, nervous system, identity)
- Phase 3 = "How do I systematize it?" (funnels, CRM, operations)
- "I charged someone" = Phase 2 courage challenge
- "I need to optimize my pricing tiers" = Phase 3 strategy

**Courage at this phase:** Expanding what your nervous system can handle. "I love breathwork" becomes "Can I facilitate breathwork for 50 strangers?" The nervous system is still the measurement: does expansion put you in Vibe Rise or push you into Stress?

**The score:** Depth within your lit-up experiences. Measured by life path progress, quest completion, courage challenges completed, and the Zone Matrix (Action Score x Clarity).

**Key app features:** Quest Board, Courage Challenges, Healing Flows, Tune Tab (daily maintenance), Fantasy League, Life Paths, Zone Assessments, Weekly Review.

**When you're ready to move on:** You've shown up enough to prove to yourself that you want this as a career. You have proof: courage challenges completed, NS data, skill XP. The decision to move to Phase 3 is self-declared, not a threshold the app decides for you.

**Product:** Find My Flow app. Free trial, then paid subscription.

## Phase 3: Build — "How do I make a living from this?"

**The question:** How do I build a sustainable career around the experiences that light me up?

**The game:** You know what lights you up (Phase 1) and you've expanded your capacity (Phase 2). Now you build the business, brand, or career that lets you live it full-time.

**Key tools:** Business Accelerator (7 stages), Offer Stack (attraction to continuity), CRM, Content Pipeline, Scale Score, Creator Playbook (Remarkable Results → Reach → Growth → Scale).

**Courage at this phase:** Systematizing, scaling, leading. Building the machine that lets you do what you love full-time. Showing up publicly as an authority in your domain.

**The score:** Scale Score, Creator XP, revenue metrics, funnel performance.

**When you're ready to move on:** You have your first graduate. Someone else's life shifted because you showed up as your Flow. The 3% chain fires.

**Product:** Scale Portal (Creator app). Separate app at `create.nichuzz.com`. Paid ($499 setup + $99/mo).

## The Insight That Unlocked This Structure

The original app tried to do everything in one product. Discovery, expansion, and building were mixed together. The three-phase structure emerged from realising:

- **Discovery and Expansion are perpendicular.** Discovery = WHAT lights you up (breadth). Expansion = HOW FAR you can take it (depth). You need breadth before depth makes sense.
- **The dome is fuel, not direction.** Your dome shape shows what recharges you. Your career direction comes from Skill x Problem (Phase 2-3), not from the dome itself.
- **Courage changes meaning at each phase.** Phase 1 courage = trying new things. Phase 2 courage = going deeper. Phase 3 courage = going public. Same nervous system, different challenge.
