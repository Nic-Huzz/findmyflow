# Dome of Safety — Feature Spec

> **Status:** Dimension alignment in progress. Sections marked ✅ are locked in. Sections marked 🔄 still need alignment.

## Concept

The Dome of Safety is a visual map of the user's nervous system comfort zone across multiple life dimensions. It answers: "Where can I operate while staying regulated?"

Every courage challenge pushes one or more dimensions. The user's NS state response (captured during/after completion) tells us whether they integrated that expansion or just survived it. Over time, the dome grows — not from doing hard things, but from doing hard things and recapturing regulation afterwards.

**Core loop:**
1. User creates a courage challenge, tags 1-3 dimensions
2. They EXPECT to feel stressed going in (that's why it's a courage challenge)
3. They complete it → NS check-in: "How did that feel during?"
4. If they recaptured **Vibe Rise or Fun** during/after → dome EXPANDS on those dimensions. This was an aligned path. Next time they face the same level, their NS already knows it survived → less pre-stress.
5. If they stayed in **Stressed** → the edge is VISIBLE but dome hasn't integrated. Growth happened, but the NS hasn't settled yet.

**Dome vs Ambitions:** The dome shows where you ARE safe. Experience ambitions (V2 roadmap) become dots OUTSIDE the dome — where you want to go. The gap between dome edge and ambition dot = the stepping stone sequence of courage challenges.

## The 9 Dimensions

### Why these 9

The original 7 dimensions (duration, frequency, medium, people, money, location, independence) were a mix of logistics and genuine NS edges. Through alignment we:

- **Dropped** Duration, Frequency, Medium — these are experience parameters (how long, how often, what format), not nervous system triggers. They stay as tags on experiences (V2 ambitions roadmap) but don't belong as dome spokes.
- **Added** Vulnerability, Stakes, Rarity, Identity — the biggest NS triggers that were missing.
- **Reframed** Money from "financial exposure" to specifically "what you charge" (investing falls under Stakes).
- **Added** Business Model as its own dimension — the progression from employment to certification IS a comfort zone edge, not just a mapping table.

## ✅ Locked-In Scales

### People (Numeric, no ceiling, log scale)

**Question:** "How many people were involved or watching?"

No artificial ceiling. Raw number input (users enter any number). Plotted on log scale for the dome viz. The following are visual reference markers on the scale, not preset levels:

`1 → 5 → 10 → 20 → 50 → 100 → 250 → 500 → 1,000 → 5,000 → 10,000 → 100,000+`

The dome edge = the highest number at which the user completed a challenge AND recaptured Vibe Rise or Fun.

### Money (Numeric, no ceiling, log scale)

**Question:** "How much did you ask someone to pay?"

Specifically about CHARGING, not investing. Investing = financial risk = belongs under Stakes. The NS trigger here is the act of asking someone to exchange money for what you offer. Users enter any amount. Visual reference markers:

`$0 → $10 → $20 → $50 → $100 → $200 → $500 → $1,000 → $2,000 → $5,000 → $10,000+`

### Vulnerability (5 levels — shields removed)

**Question:** "How much could you hide?"

Vulnerability is META to the action, not about what content you share. It's about how many shields (expertise, a screen, a role, a script, a team) were between you and judgment. Removing shields = increasing vulnerability.

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Fully shielded | Anonymous, scripted, behind a screen, within a team |
| 2 | Name on it | Your name is attached but you're behind structure |
| 3 | Face visible | In person or on camera, but within a professional context |
| 4 | Unscripted | Just you, no preparation or role to fall back on |
| 5 | Unmasked | Doing something that reveals a part of you that's normally private (body, raw ability, deep beliefs) |

**Examples from Huzz's challenges:**
- Cold messaging on social media = 2 (name on it, behind a screen)
- Handing out flyers = 3 (face visible, but there's a mission)
- Monument Rave = 4 (unscripted, leading something unusual in public)
- Post abs photo = 5 (body exposed, normally private)
- Shirt rip at Harvard = 5 (body + raw energy, in a prestigious setting)

### Stakes (4 levels)

**Question:** "What was at risk?"

Covers all types of risk: financial investment, reputation, relationships, career. Money dimension covers what you CHARGE; Stakes covers what you could LOSE.

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Nothing to lose | Worst case, it's awkward for a moment |
| 2 | Ego on the line | Embarrassment, rejection, small money lost |
| 3 | Real consequences | Reputation, meaningful money, relationship strained |
| 4 | Major bet | Significant money, career move, public commitment, hard to undo |

**Examples from Huzz's challenges:**
- Handing out flyers = 1 (nothing to lose, just awkward)
- Cold messaging = 2 (ego bruise if ignored)
- Pitching Wietse & Richard = 3 (real relationship/business consequence)
- Buy 100 headsets for Europe = 4 (major financial commitment)

### Rarity (5 levels)

**Question:** "How normal is this in the world?"

NOT "how new is this to me" (personal novelty correlates with being outside your dome, but isn't a separate dimension — the dome already tracks your regulated capacity boundary). This is about social proof — how much permission exists in the world for this action. As you find your flow and create your category, you move toward rare/unheard of. The NS trigger: doing something with no blueprint means no social proof to lean on.

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Everyone does this | Normal, socially accepted, proven path |
| 2 | Common in your world | Your peers do it, but mainstream wouldn't |
| 3 | Uncommon | Some people do it, but you'd have to explain it |
| 4 | Rare | Most people have never seen this done |
| 5 | Unheard of | You might be the first, no blueprint exists |

**Examples:**
- Running ads = 1 (everyone does this)
- Running a group program = 2 (common in creator/wellness world)
- Monument Rave = 4 (rare, people don't know what this is)
- Shirt rip at Harvard = 5 (unheard of)

### Identity (5 levels — surprise/external perception)

**Question:** "How much does this feel like you?"

The NS trigger with identity is external: it's scary because of how others perceive the shift, not just how you feel internally. The scale measures how much your action disrupts other people's model of who you are.

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Nobody blinks | This is obviously you, no one questions it |
| 2 | Raised eyebrows | People notice, it's slightly unexpected |
| 3 | "That's not like you" | People close to you call it out |
| 4 | "What happened to you?" | People who know you genuinely don't recognise this version |
| 5 | You become the story | People talk about it when you're not in the room |

**Examples from Huzz's challenges:**
- Monument Rave (now, after many) = 1 (obviously him)
- Cold messaging = 2 (raised eyebrows)
- Post abs photo = 3-4 ("That's not like you" / "What happened?")
- Shirt rip at Harvard = 5 (people are literally telling that story)

**Key insight:** The dome captures identity integration over time. What was a 5 when you first did it can become a 1 as your identity absorbs the new behavior.

## Dome Expansion Logic

### The Integration Signal

The stress going IN is expected and healthy — that's the growth happening. The question is whether the nervous system recaptures regulation during/after.

```
Pre-challenge:  User expects stress (that's why it's a courage challenge)
During/After:   NS check-in captures one of 4 states
                ┌─────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐
                │ Vibe Rise 🔥 │ Dome EXPANDS — aligned path, integrated  │
                │ Fun ☺️       │ Dome EXPANDS — aligned path, integrated  │
                │ Stressed 😰  │ Edge VISIBLE — growth happened, not yet  │
                │              │ integrated. Dome unchanged.              │
                │ Bored 😶     │ Not an edge — wrong dimension tagged     │
                └─────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Computing the Dome Edge

For each dimension, the dome edge = the highest level at which the user completed a challenge AND recaptured Vibe Rise or Fun.

```javascript
function getDomeEdge(userId, dimension) {
  // NOTE: after_state lives on nervous_system_checkins, NOT groan_challenges.
  // Must JOIN via nervous_system_checkins.source_challenge_id = groan_challenges.id
  const challenges = getCompletedChallengesWithNS(userId)
    .filter(c => c.expansion_dimensions.includes(dimension))
    .filter(c => c.ns_after_state === 'vibe_rise' || c.ns_after_state === 'ventral')

  // For numeric dimensions: max value
  // For qualitative dimensions: highest level index
  return max(challenges.map(c => c.dimension_values[dimension]))
}
```

### Edge vs Dome (Two-Layer Visual)

- **Inner dome (solid fill)**: Integrated capacity. You can operate here while staying regulated.
- **Edge ring (dashed/translucent)**: You've been here, your NS handled it, but you haven't integrated yet. Growth zone.

### Bored = Wrong Dimension

If a user completes a challenge and reports Bored, that dimension wasn't the real stretch. The dome is already past that level on that axis, or they tagged the wrong dimension.

## Business Model Mapping

### The Insight

Your dome of safety determines which business models you can comfortably operate. The dome literally maps your business model readiness.

### Business Model as a Dimension

Business Model is a spoke on the dome, not a mapping table. "What level of business model are you comfortable operating at?" is a nervous system question. Someone comfortable at Per Session might be terrified of Group Program. That's an edge.

The 7-level ladder (Work for Someone → Per Session → Group Program → Content → Digital Product → Membership → Certification) becomes the scale for this spoke.

### Dome Minimums per Business Model

ADDITIONALLY, each business model has minimum requirements across OTHER dimensions. When the user's dome meets those minimums, the model is achievable from a regulated nervous system.

Minimums are thresholds (>=), not ranges. User's dome edge must meet or exceed each value.

| Business Model | People >= | Money >= | Vulnerability >= | Stakes >= | Rarity >= | Identity >= | Independence >= |
|----------------|-----------|----------|------------------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Work for Someone | — | — | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Per Session | 5 | $20 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| Group Program | 8 | $100 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Content | 100 | $0 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Digital Product | — | $27 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Membership | 10 | $27 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Certification | 3 | $2,000 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

### Courage Challenge Suggestions

When a user's dome is 1 dimension away from a business model's requirements, suggest targeted challenges:

> "Your dome covers everything for Group Programs except Vulnerability (currently 'name on it', needs 'face visible'). Want a courage challenge to practise showing up on camera?"

### Nathan Barry's Ladder of Wealth Creation (Reference Frame)

Barry's universal ladder (Time for Money → Own Service → Productised Service → Products → Investing) maps to the same dome expansion pattern. Each rung requires progressively more expansion across dimensions. The dome makes abstract career advice concrete and personalised.

## Data Model

### New: `dimension_values` on courage challenges

```sql
ALTER TABLE groan_challenges
  ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS dimension_values jsonb DEFAULT '{}';

-- Example stored value:
-- { "people": 20, "money": 200, "vulnerability": 3, "stakes": 2 }
-- Numeric dimensions store raw numbers, qualitative store level index (1-based)
```

Existing `expansion_dimensions` (text[]) stays as quick-lookup for which dimensions were tagged. `dimension_values` adds the specific level.

### New: `dome_snapshots` table (Sprint D, optional)

```sql
CREATE TABLE dome_snapshots (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid REFERENCES auth.users ON DELETE CASCADE,
  snapshot_date date NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_DATE,
  dome_edges jsonb NOT NULL,
  edge_zone jsonb DEFAULT '{}',
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now(),
  UNIQUE(user_id, snapshot_date)
);
```

### Existing tables used (no changes needed)

- `groan_challenges.expansion_dimensions` — text[] of tagged dimensions
- `nervous_system_checkins.after_state` — NS state post-completion, linked via `source_challenge_id` FK to `groan_challenges.id`
- `nervous_system_checkins.before_state` — NS state pre-challenge (confirms the "expected stress" signal)
- `quest_experiences` — ambition dots (V2 roadmap)
