### "Finding Flow" Across Ancient Cultures

Every ancient culture had a word for the state FindMyFlow helps people access:

- **Eudaimonia** (Greek, Aristotle): fulfilling your *daimon* (true nature). Not happiness as a feeling, but flourishing through living as you were inherently intended to live. Activity, not a state. *Connection: the Sprouter diagonal. Self-actualisation isn't a destination, it's living in alignment.*
- **Ikigai** (Japanese, Heian period, 794 CE): "a reason for being." What makes life worth living. Linked to longevity in Okinawa. *Connection: play-skills discovery. The intersection of what lights you up and what the world needs.*
- **Wu Wei** (Chinese, Taoism, Lao Tzu): "effortless action." Not inaction but supreme activity with supreme relaxation. Aligned with the Tao. *Connection: the sweet spot in every corridor. Not forcing, not freezing.*
- **Dharma** (Sanskrit, Hindu/Buddhist): from *dhr* meaning "to hold/support." Your sacred duty. *Sva-dharma* = your personal dharma shaped by who you specifically are. When aligned, brings joy. *Connection: Root & Reach. Your Waterfall is your sva-dharma.*
- **Mana** (Hawaiian): spiritual energy, life force. Increases through alignment with *pono* (righteousness, balance). Diminishes through misalignment. *Connection: Flow Compass energy tracking. Mana flows when you're in the sweet spot.*
- **Songlines** (Aboriginal Australian, Dreaming): paths across the land marking the routes of creator-beings. You navigate by singing the song. Your birthplace determines your custodial path. *Connection: play-skills ARE your songline. The path that was always yours.*

### Ancient Healing Rituals as Accidental Reconsolidation

Every ancient culture designed containers that accidentally met the 3 reconsolidation conditions. Shift Architecture is the modern, intentional version of what shamans, monks, and elders have been doing for thousands of years:

- **Soul Retrieval** (Shamanic, global): reactivation (access the wound) → return the lost fragment (mismatch) → reintegration (new encoding). Modern parallel: IFS unburdening.
- **Sweat Lodge** (Native American): container (safety) → somatic activation (heat, breath) → altered state (mismatch through body experience) → discharge (sweating, release).
- **Catharsis** (Greek, Aristotle): theatre activates the emotional encoding. The drama provides a mismatch (the character's fate contradicts the audience's fear). Emotional release follows.
- **Vipassana** (Buddhist): body scanning = somatic activation. Observing without reacting = mismatch (the prediction "this feeling is dangerous" is contradicted by surviving it). Equanimity = new encoding.
- **Dreamtime Ceremonies** (Aboriginal Australian): re-entering creation stories = reactivation. Ceremonial re-enactment provides the container for processing. Community witnessing = mismatch + repetition.
