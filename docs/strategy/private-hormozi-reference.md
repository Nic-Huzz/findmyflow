## Hormozi's Three Books: How They Feed Into Each Other

### $100M Offers (Book 1): WHAT do I sell?

Creates the core offer. The Grand Slam Offer.

- Value Equation: Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice
- 5-step process: Dream outcome, list every obstacle, convert to solutions, delivery vehicles, trim + stack
- Premium pricing, guarantees, scarcity, naming
- Output: One irresistible offer

### $100M Leads (Book 2): WHO do I sell it to?

Creates the pipeline that feeds the offer.

- Lead Magnets: Free solution to narrow problem, reveals bigger problem, points to Grand Slam
- Core Four: Warm Outreach + Content + Cold Outreach + Paid Ads
- Rule of 100: 100 outreaches or 100 minutes of content or $100 in ads, daily
- More > Better > New growth cycle
- Output: Predictable flow of people seeing the offer

### $100M Money Models (Book 3): HOW do I maximize it?

Creates the revenue architecture around the offer.

- Offer Sequence: Attraction Offer (get them in) > Core Offer (Grand Slam) > Upsell > Downsell > Continuity
- Three Stages: Get Cash (attraction) > Get More Cash (upsell/downsell) > Get The Most Cash (continuity)
- CFA Framework: CAC, LTGP, Payback Period. Goal: one customer funds acquisition of two more within 30 days
- Output: Self-funding revenue machine

### The Flywheel

The Grand Slam Offer is the nucleus. Book 1 builds it. Book 2 delivers eyeballs to it. Book 3 wraps it in a revenue ecosystem. Each book solves the constraint the previous one creates.

## Hormozi's Signature Models (Structural Reference)

| Book | Signature Model | Format | Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| $100M Offers | **Value Equation** | Fraction (4 variables) | "How do I know my offer is good enough?" |
| $100M Leads | **Core Four** | 2x2 matrix | "Where should I get attention?" |
| $100M Money Models | **Offer Sequence** | Linear pipeline | "How do I make the most money per customer?" |

### What makes these models work:
- **Napkin-drawable.** Can be sketched in under 30 seconds.
- **Immediately diagnostic.** User can locate themselves on the model within a minute.
- **Actionable.** Each position on the model implies a specific next move.
- **One question each.** The model answers exactly one question, not three.
- **Different format per book.** Equation, matrix, pipeline. Keeps the brain engaged.

### FindMyFlow model design targets:
- Same simplicity (napkin test)
- Same diagnostic power (where am I?)
- Same actionability (what do I do next?)
- Different formats across the three parts
- Must stand alone with zero Hormozi reference

## Business Stage Reordering Note

Current stage order puts Money Models (Stage 4) before Offer Creation (Stage 5). In Hormozi's sequence, Offers come first, then Leads, then Money Models. Consider reordering:

1. Validation
2. Product Creation
3. Testing
4. **Offer Creation** (Grand Slam)
5. **Campaign/Leads**
6. **Money Models** (Attraction, Upsell, Downsell, Continuity)
7. Launch + Optimize
