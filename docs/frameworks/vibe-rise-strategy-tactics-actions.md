## STRATEGY (The Big Bets)

### 1. Category: Courage, Gamified
We own courage as a product category. Nobody else measures it. Nobody gamifies it. Nobody shows your comfort zone as a shape that grows. Nike Run Club for courage.

### 2. Philosophy: School of Experience
The School of Theory (university, courses, coaching) says knowledge is the bottleneck. We say courage is. You don't consume your way to the life you want. You create it.

### 3. Positioning: Huzz IS the category
The person people think of when someone says "I'm quitting my job." The app is the answer when they ask "how?" Dance is the Lightning Strike. The app converts the attention.

### 4. Qualifier: For careers you don't find in any course catalogue — because you create them
Not for mapped careers (lawyer, doctor, engineer). For careers that don't have a map.

### 5. Belief line: "You don't rise to the level of your ambitions. You fall to the level of your comfort zone."

## TACTICS (The Approaches)

### Tactic 1: Monument Rave Lightning Strike (Challenger Coming-Out Party)
**What:** Concentrated 2-4 week content burst using Monument Rave footage, each piece framed with the School of Experience narrative.
**Why:** Nobody knows the category exists. This makes it exist.
**Energy:** Cult initiation + educative + fun.

### Tactic 2: Content Pyramid (Top / Middle / Base)
**What:** Top (spectacle, 1x/week) + Middle (educate, 1-2x/week) + Base (personal, 1x/week).
**Why:** Currently have Top and Base but zero Middle. Middle is the missing bridge that converts followers to believers.

### Tactic 3: Zero CAC Flywheel
**What:** You live it → you share it → people ask "how?" → you hand them the app → their data feeds your content → repeat.
**Why:** No budget. The person IS the distribution.

### Tactic 4: ManyChat Lead Capture
**What:** Every piece of content ends with "DM [KEYWORD]" → ManyChat auto-sends Ambition Radar link → email captured → app funnel.
**Why:** Instagram doesn't allow links in Reels. ManyChat is the cash register.

### Tactic 5: Three Lead Magnets (All Live)
**What:** Experience Game (The Seeker), Aliveness Quiz (The Seeker in pain), Ambition Radar (The Stuck).
**Why:** Each intercepts from a different School of Theory tool.

### Tactic 6: Value Ladder
**What:** Free (lead magnets) → App (low cost) → Events (workshops, retreats) → Coaching ($10K, year-long).
**Why:** Supers ascend naturally. Each tier ladders to the next.
