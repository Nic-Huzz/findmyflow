# Zone Cal x Dome: Product Architecture Overview

*For agents working on the Progress Tab, journey UI, or any feature that tells the user's story.*

---

## The Two Instruments

The app has two core measurement instruments. Everything else in the product serves these two.

### Zone Cal = The Map (where you are on the aliveness journey)

An 8-dimension radar arranged as a **journey in sequence**. The dimensions fill clockwise — a healthy shape fills in order. Doing the BUILD spokes before the REPAIR spokes = building on sand.

**REPAIR Phase (Inner Work) — "Remove what was installed"**

| # | Spoke | Question | What it measures |
|---|-------|----------|------------------|
| 1 | **Identity** | "How clearly can you name who you are underneath your roles?" | Authentic vs constructed self |
| 2 | **Vulnerability** | "Can you let yourself be seen?" | Capacity for being seen |
| 3 | **Direction** | "What's yours to create?" | Clarity of purpose |
| 4 | **Worth** | "Do you believe you deserve what you're building toward?" | THE HINGE — inner critic / permission |

**BUILD Phase (Outer Work) — "Construct what is yours"**

| # | Spoke | Question | What it measures |
|---|-------|----------|------------------|
| 5 | **Growth** | "Am I stretching at the right level?" | Calibrated challenge |
| 6 | **Output** | "Am I building without burning out?" | Sustainable creation |
| 7 | **Risk** | "Am I betting on things that light me up?" | Aligned risk-taking |
| 8 | **Play** | "Does this feel like play or obligation?" | Integration — emergent when all others calibrate |

**Worth is THE HINGE.** It's where inner work transitions to outer work. Without clearing the inner critic, Growth and Output collapse immediately. If someone's shape has every spoke at 4-5 except Worth — that's "The Hinge Block" diagnosis.

**The Zone Cal shape maps to the three app phases:**

| Phase | Zone Cal Shape | Aliveness State |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Phase 1: Discovery** | Repair spokes forming (Identity, Vulnerability, Direction, Worth) | Suppressed — "I know it's not this" |
| **Phase 2: Expansion** | Worth hinge clearing, Build spokes growing (Growth, Output, Risk) | Coming online — courage + healing reopening channels |
| **Phase 3: Living it** | All spokes high, Play emerges | Alive — "even the hard parts feel meaningful" |

**Named diagnoses from the shape:**

| Pattern | Name | One-liner |
|---------|------|-----------|
| All low | **The Crack** | "Everything is about to change." |
| Repair high, Build low | **Head Full of Dreams** | "You know everything. You do nothing." |
| Build high, Repair low | **Misguided Hustle** | "You're building fast. On someone else's foundation." |
| Worth is single lowest | **The Hinge Block** | "Everything else is growing. This is the ceiling." |
| Play is single lowest | **Grinding** | "You're doing everything right. It just doesn't feel alive." |
| High Direction, low Output/Risk, direction is "helping others" | **The Premature Teacher** | "You're teaching because it feels safer than creating." |
| All high, balanced | **Your Flow** | "You're on the diagonal. Keep going." |

### Dome of Safety = The Constraint (what's blocking progress on the map)

An 8-dimension radar measuring **nervous system capacity** — what your NS will actually let you DO while staying regulated.

| # | Dimension | Scale | What it measures |
|---|-----------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | **People** | 1-12 (1→100K) | How many people can you perform/express in front of? |
| 2 | **Money** | 1-11 ($0→$10K) | How much money at stake before your NS flips? |
| 3 | **Vulnerability** | 1-5 | How visible/exposed can you be? |
| 4 | **Stakes** | 1-4 | What consequences can you handle? |
| 5 | **Rarity** | 1-5 | How unusual can your actions be? |
| 6 | **Identity** | 1-5 | How different from your old self? |
| 7 | **Context** | 1-5 | How unfamiliar can the environment be? |
| 8 | **Business** | 1-5 | What commitment level can you sustain? |

**The Dome is the CEILING on Zone Cal.** Your Zone Cal spoke can't extend past what your Dome allows. If Dome Vulnerability is 2/5, Zone Cal Vulnerability is capped there — no matter how much self-knowledge you have, your NS won't let you act on it.

**Two layers:**
- **Inner dome (solid):** Integrated capacity — challenges where after_state = vibe_rise or ventral. This is REAL capacity.
- **Edge ring (dashed):** Growth zone — challenges where after_state = sympathetic. Survived but not yet integrated. Capacity that's there under load but fragile.

**Dome dimensions don't map 1:1 to Zone Cal dimensions:**

| Zone Cal Spoke | Dome Ceiling | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Identity | Direct match |
| Vulnerability | Vulnerability | Direct match |
| Direction | — | No NS ceiling (self-knowledge, not capacity) |
| Worth | — | No NS ceiling (bodyguard issue, not capacity) |
| Growth | Stakes + Rarity | Dome caps stretch level |
| Output | Business | Dome caps sustainable creation |
| Risk | Money + Stakes | Dome caps how much you bet |
| Play | — | Emergent (no single ceiling) |

**Key insight: Direction, Worth, and Play have NO dome ceiling.** They're blocked by protective voices, not by nervous system capacity. The Dome can't fix Worth. Only the 5Rs (healing) can retire the bodyguard.

## The Game

```
Expand the dome (courage + healing)
    → Zone Cal spokes can extend further
        → you progress through the phases
            → you feel more alive
```

**Two mechanisms expand the dome:**
1. **Courage** — expands capacity through exposure. Inhibitory learning: you predicted catastrophe at 8/10, reality delivered 1/10, the prediction updates. The NS window widens.
2. **Healing** — removes the block via the 5Rs (Recognise → Reactivate → Release → Rewire → Return, held inside Reconnect). Retires the protective voice that's keeping the dome small.

Courage handles the dimensions WITH a NS ceiling (People, Money, Vulnerability, Stakes, Rarity, Identity, Context, Business).
Healing handles the dimensions WITHOUT a NS ceiling (Direction, Worth, Play — blocked by bodyguards, not capacity).

## Supporting Instruments (not radars — diagnostic layers)

### Vibe Rise Radar — The Compass
6 dimensions: Permission, Safety, Freedom, Connection, Engagement, Joy.
Not a separate instrument — it's a **checkpoint within the Zone Cal journey**. "Is this step making you more alive or less?" Used at events (before/after snapshot) and as a life-path diagnostic. Correlates with Zone Cal position: Phase 1 = low aliveness, Phase 3 = high aliveness.

### Four Channels (Life Fuel) — Fuel Diagnostics
4 channels: Choice, Connection, Mastery, Meaning.
Collected in the app as data that explains WHY certain Zone Cal spokes or Vibe Rise dimensions are low. Not presented to users as a separate radar — it's the fuel system underneath.

## The Delivery Split

| | X-axis (capacity) | Y-axis (non-resistance) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | How much activation the NS can hold | Whether you need the situation to be different |
| **Delivered by** | App (challenges, tracking, streaks) | Events (the room, recognition, the unconditional witness) |
| **Progresses by** | Reps, dose-response | Insight, non-accumulating |
| **Measurable** | Yes — the dome grows | Poorly, and measuring may destroy it |

Together = the diagonal to Equanimity. Neither alone gets you there.

## How This Maps to the Current Progress Tab

The Progress Tab already tells a narrative that aligns with this architecture:

| Progress Tab Section | Maps to | What it shows |
|---------------------|---------|---------------|
| **Hero Card** (stage 1-12) | Zone Cal journey position | Where you are on the aliveness journey |
| **Essence Card** | Identity anchor | Who you are underneath the protection (Zone Cal spoke 1) |
| **Voice Section** | The block — what keeps the Dome small | Which protective voice is active + trending |
| **Evidence Section** | Dome expansion proof | Courage challenges completed + dimensions grown + NS state |
| **Your Paths** | Per-quest Dome progress | Dome radar per life path |
| **Income Card** | Phase 3 signal | You're being paid for what makes you alive |

**The story the Progress Tab tells:**
1. Here's where you are on the journey (Hero → Zone Cal)
2. Here's who you really are underneath (Essence)
3. Here's what's been getting in the way (Voice → the block keeping Dome small)
4. Here's what you did about it this week (Evidence → Dome expanding)
5. Here's how far each path has come (Paths → per-quest Dome)
6. Here's the proof it's working (Income → Phase 3)

**What might need updating for the aliveness pivot:**
- Hero Card could reference Zone Cal diagnoses ("You're in The Hinge Block — Worth is your next spoke")
- Evidence section could show Zone Cal progress alongside Dome growth ("this challenge moved your Output spoke from 3 to 4")
- The narrative bridge texts could reference aliveness explicitly ("your aliveness channels are reopening")
- A Zone Cal snapshot could appear periodically (monthly?) showing shape change over time
