# FindMyFlow x Category Pirates
*Mapping the overlap between two frameworks. Original analysis: Huzz Hurrell / FindMyFlow. April 2026.*

---

## The Core Thesis

Category Pirates says: **"Whoever frames the problem owns the solution."** You don't compete in an existing category. You create a new one where you're the only answer.

FindMyFlow says: **Your remarkable trigger creates a tribe.** The norm you challenge becomes the belief your audience shares. That shared belief becomes distribution.

These are the same mechanism. Category Pirates calls it **category design**. FindMyFlow calls it finding your **movement**.

A movement is: the norm you challenge (the old category) + the different future you propose (the new category) + the people who join you (the tribe/superconsumers) + the visible behavior that proves it (the Lightning Strike).

## The 3 Movement Questions

### The Discovery Process

Three questions that draw out a creator's movement in language they'd actually use over a coffee. These do the work of six Category Pirates frameworks (POV structure, DAM formula, One-Sentence Strategy, Breakthrough Game, Category Violence, Missionaries vs Mercenaries) in plain language.

**Question 1: "How does the world currently solve this problem?"**

Names the **old category**. CP calls this the "legacy category." The thing people are currently shopping for. The demand you're going to DAM.

*Example: "Therapy. One-on-one. Private. Serious. Expensive."*

**Question 2: "What's wrong with that?"**

CP's **"Frame Different Problem"** (Part 1 of the POV structure). Also **Category Violence**: naming what's broken about the old category. CP says "if your POV doesn't make incumbents uncomfortable, you're competing, not creating." Q2 is where the creator commits to that discomfort.

*Example: "It's isolating. People got wounded in groups but heal alone. And most people can't afford it or won't try it because it feels too heavy."*

This answer is a perfect example of CP's "reject the premise" principle. It's not saying therapy is bad. It's saying the PREMISE (that healing is individual) is wrong. That's a category-level argument, not a product-level one.

**Question 3: "How do you solve it?"**

CP's **"Evangelize Different Future"** (Part 2 of the POV structure). This IS the new category. The movement.

*Example: "Dancing with strangers at sunrise. Community. Play. Fun."*

**Question 4 (implicit, required for conversion): "What does someone do next?"**

CP's Part 3: Bridge with Your Offer. Mechanic 4: "Make the cash register sing."

*Example: "Come to [Experience Name] on [Date]."*

Without Q4, the movement is inspiring but doesn't convert. Every time a creator articulates Q1-Q3 (in content, in conversation, on a Strike), it needs to land on Q4.

### How the 3 Questions Map to CP Frameworks

| CP Framework | What It Does | Which Question Does It |
|---|---|---|
| **POV Structure Part 1** (Frame Different Problem) | Name what others miss | Q2: "What's wrong with that?" |
| **POV Structure Part 2** (Evangelize Different Future) | Paint the escape | Q3: "How do you solve it?" |
| **POV Structure Part 3** (Bridge with Offer) | Connect to solution | Q4: "What does someone do next?" |
| **DAM the Demand** | Intercept old-category shoppers and redirect | Q1 → Q2 → Q3 is the full redirect |
| **One-Sentence Strategy** | Reject premise, paint escape, be visceral | Q2 rejects, Q3 paints, both are visceral because they come from the creator's wound |
| **Category Violence** | Attack the old category's assumptions | Q2 IS category violence |
| **Breakthrough Game Q1** | "What problem would you solve differently?" | Q1 + Q2 + Q3 combined |
| **Missionaries vs Mercenaries** | Create new vs fight over old | Q1 describes the mercenary approach. Q3 describes the missionary approach. |
| **Law #8** ("Whoever frames the problem owns the solution") | The framing defines your category | Q2 IS the framing. How you describe what's wrong DEFINES your new category. |

### What the 3 Questions Generate

**The DAM statement writes itself:**

*"People searching for [Q1] think they need [Q1]. But [Q2]. What they actually need is [Q3]."*

"People searching for therapy think they need one-on-one private sessions. But people got wounded in groups and heal alone, and most can't afford it. What they actually need is to dance with strangers at sunrise."

**The One-Sentence Strategy writes itself:**

Must reject the premise (Q2 does this), paint the escape (Q3 does this), be visceral (both are, because they come from the creator's own wound, not strategic analysis).

**The Lightning Strike direction writes itself:**

The Strike's job is to make Q2 undeniable and Q3 visible. The Strike IS Q3 done in a public space where the market can't ignore it.

**The content thesis writes itself:**

Every piece of content educates about Q2 (what's wrong with the old way) and demonstrates Q3 (what the new way looks like). The content generator uses Q1/Q2/Q3 as DNA for every post, DM, and email.

### The Role of Life Map Data

The 3 questions are the PRIMARY input. The creator answers them directly. They know their movement.

Life Map data and remarkable triggers then VALIDATE the answers:

*"Does your Life Map support this? Your wounds point to [isolation, paywalled healing, seriousness]. Your skills point to [performance energy, framework design, community facilitation]. This movement is consistent with your data."*

If the 3 questions and the Life Map data align: strong signal. If they diverge: worth exploring why. Maybe the creator is articulating what they WANT the movement to be, not what their experience actually supports. That tension is a useful conversation for the group call.

This is a stronger architecture than having AI infer the movement from data and hoping it's right. The creator draws out their own movement. The data confirms it.

| Without DAM | With DAM |

The DAM statement is the strategic north star. The Lightning Strike is the moment it becomes undeniable.

## Lightning Strikes for Experience Creators

### Why the Current Group Call Design Isn't Enough

The existing call concept (see `docs/creator-flywheel.md`) is structured around **accountability**: green/red on checklist items, set intentions, debrief 3%. That's Ground Game. It's necessary but it's not what makes people talk. Nobody screenshots their checklist progress and posts it. Nobody tells their friend "you have to join this group, we set intentions every Thursday."

Lightning Strikes reframes the call from "did you do your homework?" to **"what moment are you designing?"**

### The 5 Strike Types Mapped to Experience Creators

| Type | What It Looks Like for a Facilitator |
|---|---|
| **Stunt** | Show up at Bali Sunday Market doing breathwork demos. Run a 2-minute "micro-shift" on the street. Film it. |
| **Fight** | Pick an enemy publicly. "Retreats that make you feel good for 3 days then nothing changes." Post about it. Name the old category. |
| **Scarcity** | "6 spots. No waitlist. If it's full, it's full." Real constraint, not manufactured. |
| **Functional** | Free 15-minute taster session before the paid experience. Let them FEEL the shift, don't describe it. |
| **Culture-Creating** | A ritual attendees take home. A phrase they start using. Something that spreads without you pushing it. |

### The Data-to-Strike Pipeline

The app already captures everything needed to design a personalized Lightning Strike. Each data source answers a different Strike design question:

| Strike Design Question | Data Source | What It Tells You |
|---|---|---|
| **Am I ready for a Strike?** | Scope Map position | Stream/Lake = not yet (build trust first). Waterfall = ready. River = go big (Category King). |
| **What's my Strike ABOUT?** | Remarkable Triggers (from Life Map + creator matching) | Your movement = your Strike's message. The thing the market needs to hear. |
| **What TYPE of Strike?** | Remarkable trigger pattern | Rule Broken → **Fight**. Unexpected Combo → **Stunt**. Stupid Simplicity → **Functional** demo. Impossible Result → **Functional** proof. Extreme Action → **Culture-Creating**. |
| **What's my resistance?** | Protective Archetype | The archetype = the internal rule that makes the Strike feel like a groan. The Strike lives on the other side of that resistance. |
| **HOW do I execute it?** | Play Profile DNA | Firestarter: one bold day, all-in. Sage: design the perfect moment. Dealmaker: co-create with allies. |
| **Who's it FOR?** | CRM repeat attendees | Superconsumers = repeat attendees. Design the Strike for them to spread. |
| **What already works?** | 3% notes across experiences | The compound pattern reveals what to amplify, not invent. |
| **Where's the edge?** | Groan reflections (highest scary scores) | The challenge that scares them most is closest to their most powerful Strike. |

### Protective Archetype → Strike Resistance

The protective archetype tells you **what kind of Strike will feel impossible**, which is exactly where the most powerful one lives:

| Archetype | Internalized Rule | The Strike They Resist | Why It's Their Best Strike |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Performer** | "Worth = polished output" | Do something raw and unfinished in public. Show the mess. | Audiences trust imperfection more than polish. The rawness IS the movement made visible. |
| **Perfectionist** | "Not ready yet" | Announce the date before the plan exists. Commit publicly. | The commitment creates the energy. Waiting for readiness is the old category. |
| **Ghost** | "Stay invisible until safe" | Be impossible to ignore. Fill a physical space with your message. | Visibility IS the Strike. For a Ghost, being seen at scale is the extreme action. |
| **Controller** | "Control or it falls apart" | Let the audience shape the experience. Surrender the script. | The loss of control is what makes it a moment people talk about. |

A Performer's Lightning Strike looks nothing like a Ghost's. The app knows which archetype they are. The Strike recommendation writes itself.

### The Revised Call Structure (Strike Lab)

**Before the call** (app flow, "Design Your Strike"):

1. App reads creator's data (Scope Map, remarkable triggers, protective archetype, Play Profile, CRM)
2. Surfaces their movement: *"Based on your Life Map, your movement is: [Playful Healing]."*
3. Surfaces the resistance: *"Your Perfectionist will tell you this isn't ready. That's the signal it's right."*
4. Frames the execution: *"You're a Firestarter. Design a Strike you can execute in one day, not one you plan for a month."*
5. Surfaces superconsumers: *"You have 4 repeat attendees. Design the Strike for THEM to share."*
6. Creator picks a Strike type or the app recommends one based on trigger pattern
7. Creator writes one sentence: "My Strike is: ___"
8. That sentence becomes a Play-List challenge with "next group call" as deadline

**On the call (60-90 min):**

```
1. STRIKE DEBRIEF (15 min)
   "What did you try? What happened?"
   App shows: reach, signups, WOM indicators

2. 3% COMPOUND (10 min)
   "What's your 3% from last experience?"
   Quick, operational, stays in the loop

3. STRIKE DESIGN (30 min)
   One member hot-seats their next Lightning Strike
   Group sharpens it using CP's 4 mechanics:
   - Who are your Superconsumers? (repeat attendees)
   - What's the exciting reason to talk?
   - What's the moment the market can't ignore?
   - How does it make the cash register sing?

4. COMMITMENTS (10 min)
   Each person: one Strike action + one Ground Game task
   Both become Play-List challenges with deadline
```

### The Monthly Rhythm

## Lightning Strikes as Play-List Challenges

### The Realisation

Lightning Strikes aren't a separate feature. They're what Play-List challenges become when connected to the creator's movement.

The Play-List already has everything:

| Play-List Infrastructure | What It Does | How It Maps to Strikes |
|---|---|---|
| Skills × 5 visibility layers | Graduated exposure matrix | Movement × Strike type matrix |
| Scary score + wahoo score | Maps courage and excitement | High scary + high wahoo = Strike territory |
| Essence zone (scary 7+ and wahoo 7+) | "This IS your essence" | The essence zone IS the Strike zone |
| DNA personalization (DO_IT / THINK_IT / MAKE_IT) | Adapts HOW to execute | Same: Firestarter blitzes, Sage designs, Dealmaker networks |
| Completion flow (I Did It → Reflection → Voice Check-in → Compass → Confetti) | Captures the learning | Strike adds: WOM reflection ("did anyone share this?") |
| Proof mechanism | Screenshot or text proof of completion | Strike proof: reach metrics, new follows, attendee signups |
| Checklist → challenge bridge | Marketing to-dos become courage challenges | Strike bridge: movement-level actions become challenges |

### What Makes a Challenge a Lightning Strike

A Lightning Strike is a Play-List challenge where:

1. **It's connected to the movement**, not just a task. "DM 10 people" is Ground Game. "DM 10 people and tell them why [old category] doesn't work and what [your movement] offers instead" is a Strike.
2. **It's designed for Superconsumers to share.** Regular challenges are about the creator doing something. Strikes are about creating something the audience can't stop talking about.
3. **It lives in the essence zone.** Scary 7+ AND wahoo 7+. If it's not scary, it won't punch through. If it's not exciting, they won't do it.
4. **It has WOM as the success metric**, not just completion. Did anyone remark on it? Did repeat attendees share it? Did new people show up because of it?

The existing essence zone scoring already identifies this territory:

```
High scary + High wahoo = "This IS your essence"
```

That's the Lightning Strike zone. The app already calculates it. It just doesn't call it that yet.

### The Visibility Layers Already Graduate Toward Strikes

The 5 layers are a courage ladder:

```
Screen (difficulty 1)     → Post content, share online
Live (difficulty 2)       → Real-time interaction, go live
Money (difficulty 3)      → Ask for payment, state your value
Vulnerable (difficulty 4) → Share your real story, admit struggle
Authority (difficulty 5)  → Claim expertise, position as leader
```

Lightning Strikes live at **Vulnerable and Authority**. They're the challenges at the top of the ladder that, when connected to the movement, create WOM.

But the current examples at those layers are individual actions:
- Vulnerable: "Share a failure publicly" 
- Authority: "Write 'I'm an expert in...' on your profile"

A Lightning Strike at those layers, connected to the movement, looks different:
- Vulnerable Strike: "Share the wound that started your movement. The specific moment the old category failed you. Film it. Post it."
- Authority Strike: "Run a free 15-minute demo that lets 20 strangers experience your movement in real time. Film their reactions."

Same layer. Same courage mechanics. But the movement gives it a point. It's not just "be vulnerable." It's "be vulnerable ABOUT the thing your tribe needs to hear."
