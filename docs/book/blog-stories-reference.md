# Blog Stories Reference (for Book Scene Beats)

*Extracted from Notion blog posts, Jul 2026. Source of autobiographical stories for Find Your Flow chapters.*

## Key Stories by Chapter

### Ch 5 (Before the Crack) — Footy + Rainbow
- Junior Australian Rules footballer, showered in accolades due to early growth spurt
- "The words of affirmation I received over my talent warmed my soul. I oriented my whole identity around my ability."
- Wore every colour of the rainbow in fluoro

### Ch 10 (You Learn What Works) — Footy Shame Spiral
- "As my peers began to catch up and surpass me, I had an identity crisis"
- "What was once a game became an exam, every training an assessment"
- "When I failed to live up to expectations, I entered a shame spiral"
- Led to partying: "How could anyone fault me when they saw how much fun I was having?"

### Ch 12 (Friends Decide) — Jackass Era + Venice
- Instagram renown for Jackass-type videos
- Venice bridge jump: "jumped off the tallest bridge in Venice, in the middle of winter, at 4am in underwear and poncho"
- Dad: "What's coming first, maturity or a body bag?"
- "My drunk tomfoolery was my armour"

### Ch 22 (What Comes Next) — Errol + Lockdown
- Meeting Errol: "rural property, bootcamp for entrepreneurs, gentleman in his 70s, white wispy hair"
- "I was shocked to find I struggled to set goals. I never actually set goals before, I just warped myself as I moved between circumstances"
- Lockdown: "First time I couldn't go out on weekends and get my hit of social approval"
- "I acknowledged Saturday nights at a pub wasn't fun. I'd go 6 weeks without seeing best friends."
- Sculptor method: "chip away at personas like a sculptor with a block of marble"

### Ch 23 (The Flood) — Glasses on Your Head
- Post-Covid: "The excitement, the buzz, the energy that once defined me appeared a distant memory"
- "The social butterfly that once flourished in large groups could be found leaving engagement parties early"
- Every minute optimised: "cursing about training starting four minutes late"
- Dad visits Thailand: "I hope you find what you're looking for. I think your glasses might be on your head."

### Ch 31 (One Test) — Hugh Van Cuylenburg DM
- Messaged Hugh Van Cuylenburg (Resilience Project founder) on Instagram
- Inner voice: "YOU IDIOT! IF HE OPENS THIS YOU TOTALLY JUST WASTED 5 SECONDS OF HIS LIFE"
- Meditated for 20 min to calm down, checked phone: TWO one-minute audio replies
- "He was now running 15 minutes late for a meeting because my keynote was so good"
- "I couldn't wipe the smile off my face. Someone whose books I've read loved my keynote."

### Ch 35 (Wound Is Your Credential) — ManCave Rejection
- Applied to ManCave (healthy masculinity workshops for teenage boys)
- "Everyone quipped I'd be perfect for the role"
- Group interview: "In trying to impress I became overzealous, constantly first to speak"
- Got rejected. Shame spiral.
- Reframe: "Perhaps the ManCave job was too perfect? I already had the skills. It wouldn't challenge me."
- Uncle asks: "Why don't you go into schools?" Groan: "I threw my head back and covered my face, groaning."
- Called Redlands headmaster (old school). "Resounding yes."
- The wound (being rejected for vulnerability work) became the credential (building his own)

### Ch 38 (Essence vs Protective) — The Two Voices
- "I never had an issue with judgement directed at 'crazy Nic' because that was simply a mask"
- "Any judgement could be rationalised away. Thought he was an idiot? Me too. Didn't like him? I didn't love the bloke either."
- "But now that I was being myself. If you ridicule that person, I'd receive it as a condemnation of my soul."

### Ch 45-46 (Reconnect + Release) — Black Box Full Detail
- Holotropic breathwork: "in, out, in, out, in, out"
- 5 min: tingling shooting up arms. 10 min: urge to shake. 15 min: space opens in chest
- "A feeling shot up from deep inside me. Pure, vulnerable love. Four years since it last warmed my body."
- "My breath felt like a shovel. Digging deep inside me."
- Flashed to May 2018: "inseparable. Even to walk to the kitchen I had my arms wrapped around her waist from behind, stepping in unison"
- Dec 2018 San Francisco: "Sunday morning Facetime. The minute she picked up I could tell something was wrong. She had slept with someone else."
- "My heart immediately shattered. My rational mind kicked into overdrive. I too had kissed someone."
- "My mind became a dictator over my emotions. Not one tear was shed."
- Opera House concert: "I literally visualised putting the emotion into a black box and burying it deep inside me"
- "I wasn't only burying the grief. I was burying the part of me that was able to feel pure love."
- 4 years numb to love with family and next partner
- Post-session journal: "like coaxing an old friend out of a hole they were scared to leave"

### Ch 57 (Comfort Zone) — Jackass as Comfort
- Venice bridge jumps, partying, Instagram stunts
- "High excitement, low fear" — it felt wild but was actually the safest thing because it was the MASK
- "My drunk tomfoolery was my armour. Any judgement could be rationalised away."

### Ch 67 (Live) — Instagram Live Ambush
- Friend Nick doing 10 Instagram Live interviews in 10 days
- Agreed to help, assuming only Nick's followers would see
- "Little did I know every single one of my 1,200 followers would receive a notification"
- "Fear gripped my bones: high school acquaintances, sporting peers, alcohol-fuelled escapade friends"
- "I would have honestly preferred to walk naked down the busiest street in Sydney"
- Nick probed deeper: purpose, vulnerability, "finding your Zest"

### Ch 69 (Vulnerable) — Accepted As Real Self
- "To my shock, by putting myself out there I became more accepted than 'Jackass Nic' ever was"
- "A few friends were inspired by the way I've put myself out there"
- "My fear of social isolation was simply a facade. An irrational fear by my prehistoric brain software."

### Ch 21 (Essence Still There) — Buddhist Monastery
- Thailand hike to Buddhist monastery
- Journaled on a rock: "I feel like I'm a playful person but there's been no space for me to play"
- "I feel trapped and rigid in my body. As if my spirit is trying to break free but I've reined it in and put it in a cage"
- "Sitting on that rock I realised what I was searching for was myself"
- "In Thailand, where there was no expectation of who I was, I felt free to be myself"

### Ch 64 (The Groan) — Hugh DM + Cocktail
- "This perfect cocktail of pain and anticipation"
- "Painful because I'm forcing myself to cross pre-existing boundaries. Anticipation for the growth and the possibility of what doors may open."
