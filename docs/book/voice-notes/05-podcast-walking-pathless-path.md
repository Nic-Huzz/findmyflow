# Voice Note 05: Podcast — Walking the Pathless Path (Jan 2024)

## New Material (not in book deck or previous sources)

### 1. The $200 Crying-Looking-at-the-Sky Scene (MAJOR)

> "There was one moment about six weeks in, just showing up once or twice a week on this beach, where I made over like $200 in an hour. And I remember looking up the sky and I was like crying. I was like, I've never made more money in an hour, but having more fun."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 74 (The Diagonal)** or a new scene near it. This is the moment the diagonal became REAL, not theoretical. The disbelief that fun and income could coexist. Could be the Scene beat for Ch 74, which currently has a generic "Walking the diagonal. Not a dramatic moment. A Tuesday." This IS the dramatic moment.

### 2. The Instagram Live Vulnerability Hangover (MAJOR)

> "I said yes because I thought jumping on the Instagram live would just be his followers. I didn't realise that me jumping on, all my followers at the time would also get a notification... I just start seeing names pop up of this person joined. This person joined. High school friends, people from footy, people who just knew me from my drunk antics. And I was like, inside I was freaking the F out... Afterwards I hung up and I just went into my bed and curled up into a ball and I just wanted to hide away."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 38 (Essence vs Protective)** — this scene is ALREADY in Ch 38's Scene beat in the book deck ("Nick's Instagram Live. I agreed to help, assuming only his followers would see."). This podcast version provides the FULL emotional detail (vulnerability hangover, curling up in bed, seeing names pop up). Use to ENRICH the existing Ch 38 scene. NOT Ch 68 (Screen), which has a different scene (week 1 Instagram story).

Also connects to: "Those antics were my armor, my protection to say you don't really know who I am. You can think this about me, but I'm someone else deep down, but I'm not going to show you that person. The first time I actually showed people that person, I no longer had the armor to protect me."

### 3. The Girlfriend's Bed / Body Signal Moment

> "I was sitting on my now girlfriend's bed. It was in the morning and the sun's coming through the door and she goes, 'Nic, can you just remind Mark that you're going on this unpaid sabbatical to do your breathwork training, but you're coming back and you're really committed to the company.' My body in that moment went, 'Nic, it's not a big shock.'"

**Where it goes**: Setup for **Ch 53 (One Scary Thing a Week)** or transition from Part 2 → Part 3. This is the 2023 quit decision, NOT the 2020 crack (Ch 4). The body overriding the stories after 3 years of being stuck. "As soon as I stopped listening to those stories and started listening to my body, the signal was the easiest and most obvious decision to make in the world."

### 4. "If TikTok Was Around, I'd Either Be Dead or in an LA Mansion"

> "I used to make a joke that if TikTok was around, I'd either be dead or in an LA mansion. Dead because I would have pushed myself to do something so dumb I would have killed myself, or an LA mansion because what I was doing was so extreme and entertaining that I would have got a million followers."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 10 (You Learn What Works)** or **Ch 8 (Then Something Happened)**. The escalation of the mask. How far the performing self went. Vivid, honest, slightly dark. The book needs this kind of honesty.

### 5. The Headsets Origin + Recurring Dream

> "I had these silent disco headsets dating back from pre-COVID when I used to always have kick-ons at my house... And I had this weird recurring dream for about a year in advance where I was popping up starting silent discos in random places in public."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 77 (Silent Discos)**. Origin story of the tool. The dream as foreshadowing.

### 6. The Armor Metaphor (Expanded)

> "Those antics in a way were my armor, my protection to say you don't really know who I am. You can think this about me, but I'm someone else deep down, but I'm not going to show you that person."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 15 (The Protector's Job)**. This IS the protector's job, in one sentence. The armor protects by making you a moving target. If they're judging the clown, they're not judging the real you.

### 7. "Doing It Because It Was So Easy" — The Conditioning Against Ease

> "The reason I thought it needed to be a side thing was 'cause it was so easy. And my whole life, work has to be hard. You're gonna work hard for your money. It's not gonna be easy. The biggest challenge right now is just accepting that, hey Nick, you can have fun, find work easy and still be rewarded for it."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 1 (The Promise)** or **Ch 74 (The Diagonal)**. The conditioning that joy = not serious. This is a belief that needs to be named in the book. The promise includes "work = sacrifice." The diagonal disproves it.

### 8. Friends Saying "You Haven't Changed"

> "Only recently did friends begin to share saying, 'Hey Nick, we actually don't think you've changed. We think your essence is still the same, but just how you're showing up is different. Because you're still doing extreme and funny things, but instead of doing it because you're running away out of fear, you're now doing it to spread joy.'"

**Where it goes**: **Ch 75 (Essence Louder Than Protection)** or **Ch 73 (The Proof)**. External validation that the essence was always there. The same energy, different fuel source (love vs fear).

### 9. "People Would Rather Feel Safe and Be Unhappy Than Potentially Be Unsafe and Be Happy"

**Where it goes**: **Ch 62 (You Don't Rise to Your Ambitions)**. Companion thesis. Could be a screenshot line.

### 10. The Fear of Authentic Expression

> "The breath work and the disco, that IS me. And so to do it and be judged for it is the scary thing in the world... it feels like a condemnation of your soul because it's such an authentic thing for you."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 54 (The Essence Test)** or **Ch 55 (Essence Zone)**. Why essence-zone activities are scarier than random challenges. The stakes are higher when it's actually you.

### 11. "I Still Wrestle With Tying My Worth to the Value I Create"

> "Do you think you've fully overcome that shame? I definitely still wrestle with tying my worth to the value that I create."

**Where it goes**: **Ch 74 (The Diagonal)** or **Ch 80 (You're Somewhere in This Story)**. Honest, unresolved. The book should NOT pretend he's finished. This is the diagonal in practice — still walking it.
