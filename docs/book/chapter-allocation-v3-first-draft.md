## Observations

**What's working:**
- Stage 3 (Refusal) is the biggest stage with 14 chapters — makes sense, this is the 3-year stuck period where all the self-knowledge accumulates
- Stage 4 (Meeting the Mentor) as Ease & Resistance / Flow framework feels right — the "mentor" is a concept, not a person
- Stage 5 (Crossing the Threshold) is tight and action-focused: groan → expand dome → scary things → compound → identity flip
- Stage 6 (Tests) = the healing toolkit (4Rs + memory reconsolidation + one sentence)
- Ikigai/Tao/Eudaimonia between The Questions and Essence is a strong bridge — "these traditions already knew"

**What needs attention:**
- 25 unassigned chapters — most are self-actualisation and finding-my-flow themed (Stages 9-12 territory)
- Stages 7-12 are very thin (2, 1, 1, 3, 0, 0 chapters). The unassigned chapters likely fill these.
- The Essence Test quadrants (54-58) are all unassigned — do they stay as separate chapters or merge?
- Protective voice chapters (38-40) are unassigned — probably Stage 5 or 6 territory
- The big self-actualisation block (72-77) is all unassigned — Stage 9-11 territory
- Creation is Excavation, Surface Area for Magic, Love vs Fear still homeless

**Questions for next pass:**
- Do the 25 unassigned chapters go into Stages 7-12?
- Should some unassigned chapters be cut or merged further?
- Are Stages 7 and 8 too thin, or are they genuinely short (the ordeal is one moment)?
