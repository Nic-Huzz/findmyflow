## Key design decisions

1. **Ikigai appears twice**: Stage 2 as curiosity gap, Stage 4 referenced inside the Flow theory chapter
2. **Essence at end of Stage 3**: Hope after installation. End of healing.
3. **Stage 3 = what happened (healing)**. Stage 4 = what to do (flow). Stage 5 = doing it (crossing).
4. **Stage 4 = pure framework**: Theory → path → internal reconnection → external flow. No stuck chapters.
5. **Stage 5 has two tools**: Top down (fear challenge, expanding safety) + bottom up (4Rs, clearing blockages). Both needed.
6. **The Challenge Ahead opens Stage 5**: "Healing comes from action not awareness" as the thesis before the stuck sequence.
7. **Stage 6 = pure story**: No frameworks. Real examples of internal flow, external flow, allies, enemies.
8. **Stage 7 = curiosity, not crisis**: The approach is deliberate expansion, not fear. "Mushrooms make the fake trees disappear."
9. **Nervous System States moved to Stage 9**: Learned after the ego death, not before.
10. **3% appears twice**: Introduced in Stage 4 (concept), full chapter in Stage 5 (compound curve).
11. **Personal Monopoly absorbs Unique Combination**: One chapter.
12. **Stages 10-12**: The story is still being written. Europe tour, Opera House homecoming, the book itself.
