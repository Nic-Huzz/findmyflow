# Chapter 0: Life is Magic

I believe there's a magic flow to everyone's life. A path only you can walk, through your curiosities, your experiences, the people you meet, the serendipities that show up when you stop forcing things and start paying attention.

The universe communicates with us every single day. The problem is it doesn't use words. It uses ease and resistance. Ease is like the magic. You ask for something and it happens. The right person shows up. The opportunity lands. You're in the right place at the right time. Resistance is when no matter what you do, no matter how hard you try, nothing goes to plan.

I believe resistance is the universe's way of whispering, hey, there's a better path.

The first month I spent in Bali revealed something I'd never felt before. That life was magic. That there's a crazy flow to the serendipities and coincidences. The people you meet, the opportunities that arise. The spontaneity and seasonality of life. The surface area that creates the possibility for life to do its magic thing.

And then I went back to the office.

Every fibre of my being screamed at me. This wasn't where I was supposed to be. Like a lion finally freed from its cage, roaring and refusing to be put back. One place. Same people. Every day. The routine had shrunk the surface area for magic to almost nothing.

But I'm getting ahead of myself.

Before Bali, before the lion, before any of this, there was an earthquake. And it changed everything.

It was like an earthquake hit. And every single building in the city came tumbling down. But the buildings weren't buildings. They were beliefs. About how the world worked.

Seventeen years of my life had been spent building to this point. Thirteen years of school. Four years of university. Years of unpaid internships and casual jobs. All to get this dream job.

A job that just twelve months before, as an intern, I'd looked at the whiteboard with all the employees' faces on it and just wished. Wished mine was up there too.

Twelve months later, that wish was true.

Within a month, I wasn't feeling the happiness, the joy, the fulfilment I thought was promised. The dream job. The full-time contract. I felt nothing.

This couldn't be it, could it?

I thought there was something wrong with me. Everyone around me seemed perfectly content. And I felt like I couldn't talk to anyone about it. Like no one else would understand. I felt like the only person in the world who felt this way.

But as life has proven to do, again after again after again, it gave me exactly what I needed to figure it out. And what I figured out over the next five years is what this book is about.

.

*This is the tutorial. The app is the game. Your life is the playthrough.*

## Editorial Notes

### What this chapter does
- Opens with the THESIS (life is magic, ease and resistance) before the crack
- The reader learns what was GAINED (magic, flow, serendipity) before learning what was LOST (the earthquake)
- Introduces Ease & Resistance as the central theme from page one
- Uses the lion moment (Voice Note 02, Feb 2023) and surface area (Voice Note 03) as flash-forward before rewinding to the earthquake
- Ends on a hook ("what I figured out is what this book is about") not a resolution

### Voice sources used
- Voice Note 03: "Life was magic. There's a crazy flow to the serendipities, coincidences."
- Voice Note 02: "Like a lion finally freed from its cage. Roaring and refusing to be put back."

- Voice Note 01: The earthquake, the whiteboard, the isolation.

### Structure
1. **Open**: Life is magic. Ease and resistance. (Thesis)
2. **Flash-forward**: Bali. The lion. Surface area for magic. (What the diagonal feels like)
3. **Rewind**: The earthquake. The crack. The isolation. (Where the story starts)
4. **Hook**: What I figured out is what this book is about. (Pull into Ch 1)

### Power-up delivered
**Ease & Resistance** — introduced here as the central theme. The reader has the lens before they have the story.
