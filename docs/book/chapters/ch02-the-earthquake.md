## What this chapter must do

1. **Hook:** The reader must see themselves in the first sentence. This is the "I screenshot this and send it to a friend" chapter.
2. **The turn:** The promise delivered everything it said it would. And it changed nothing inside. The earthquake isn't losing — it's winning and feeling nothing.
3. **The landing:** Plant the seed that the system was rigged. Not maliciously. Just wrongly. The external was never going to fix the internal.

## How to write it

1. Read the epigraph options above. Pick the one that feels truest.
2. Read your raw material. Let it sit for a minute.
3. Open a voice memo or start typing as if you're telling a friend at 1am: "So I got this job, right, and..."
4. Don't edit. Don't structure. Let it be messy.
5. Then shape it: opening line (the hook), the turn (the moment it cracked), the landing (what it meant).
6. Cut until every sentence earns its place. Target: 1-3 pages.
7. Read aloud. If you cringe at a phrase, it's not yours. Remove it.
