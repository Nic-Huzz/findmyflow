# Chapter 3: The Grind Without the Goal

**Status:** Draft v1 (needs voice memo polish)
**Seed:** "As an intern, the daily grind was worth it because I was suffering towards something."
**Hero Stage:** 2 — Call to Adventure (the WHY behind the crack)
**Job:** Explain the mechanism. External validation was the engine. The goal was the anaesthetic.

---

As an intern, the daily grind was worth it.

I was suffering towards a goal. Full-time employee. The title. The salary. The status that came with it. Every boring day, every late night, every bit of work I didn't enjoy was justified because it was getting me somewhere.

I realise now the whole thing was driven by exterior motivations. Impress my friends. Make my parents proud. Give myself a sense of self-worth I didn't have on my own.

I saw them as check boxes: earn a healthy income, be accepted by my friends, make my parents proud. When your "who" is simply a byproduct of your environment, you have no control.

The title was supposed to provide that.

And for about a week, it did.

But now I had it. And there was nothing else to suffer towards. No next rung. No promotion to dream about. Just the work. The same work I never actually enjoyed. Without the goal to justify it.

The grind without the goal.

That's what cracked me open. Not a dramatic event. Not a breakdown. Just the quiet realisation that the suffering was the whole thing. It wasn't the price of admission. It was the ride.
