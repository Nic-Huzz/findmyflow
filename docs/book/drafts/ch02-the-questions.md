# Chapter 2: The Questions

**Status:** Draft v1

**Hero Stage:** 2 — Call to Adventure

---

Who am I? What's my purpose? What am I doing with my life?

I had no answers. Not even the beginning of answers.

And the timing couldn't have been worse. Two weeks after the earthquake hit, the world locked down. I was 23, a big partier. The weekend was my escape. Loud music, dancing, not thinking. The one place the questions couldn't reach me.

Now that was gone.

No pubs. No parties. No distraction. Just me and these questions in a room that suddenly felt very small.

The questions didn't arrive politely. They detonated. And without the weekend to numb them, they wouldn't stop.
