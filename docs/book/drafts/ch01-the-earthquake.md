# Chapter 1: The Earthquake

**Status:** Draft v1
**Seed:** "I thought there was something wrong with me."
**Hero Stage:** 1 — The Matrix
**Job:** Open the wound. The reader goes "that's me."

---

I thought there was something wrong with me.

I'd spent my whole life building to this moment. 13 years of school. 4 years of uni. Another year of unpaid internships and contract work.

A year ago I sat in this exact chair as an intern, staring at the whiteboard with all the employees' faces on it, wishing more than anything that mine was up there too.

Now it was.

And I felt nothing.

No happiness. No excitement. No fulfilment. None of the things I thought were promised by a dream job. The thought of doing this five days a week for the next 40 years horrified me.

It was like an earthquake hit, and every building in the city came tumbling down. But the buildings were my beliefs about how the world worked.

I'd trusted this path. Grinded. Suffered. Did everything it asked of me. And the promise of happiness at the end simply wasn't there.

But I was so confused. My parents, my friends, everyone I knew lived like this. No one else spoke about feeling this way.

So what was wrong with me?
