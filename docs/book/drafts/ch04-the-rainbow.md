# Chapter 4: The Rainbow

**Status:** Draft v1
**Seed:** "Zest." / "By the time I was 22 that rainbow was gone."
**Hero Stage:** 3 — What Was Lost
**Job:** Show the kid who had inherent self-worth. Show the three moments that took it.

---

"Zest." That was the word Dad used to describe me on my 18th birthday.

Growing up I was a walking rainbow. You name a colour and I wore it in fluro. I had so much energy I got tested for ADHD on multiple occasions, always to be told I didn't have it. I uploaded videos of me doing interpretive dances on Facebook. Every Christmas my family hosted "Goolwa Idol" where I would dance as my performance for the night.

By the time I was 22 that rainbow was gone. I now only wore beer logos.

Three moments took it from me.

At 9, our primary school put together a boy dancing group. Me and my friends all tried out. Every single one got chosen except me. Bye bye love of dancing.

At 13, my joyful, carefree nature was expressed through rainbow clothes. But teenage boys being teenage boys, I got laughed at. Called names. Comment by comment, emotional splinter by emotional splinter, that rainbow disappeared.

Then as an intern at my dream job, the CEO didn't want to hire me full-time because he thought I was too playful to be a serious employee. I always saw my playfulness as my superpower. In that moment it became the thing to hide.

It doesn't matter what these moments are. What matters is we hate how we felt in them. The shame, the embarrassment, the fear. We never want to feel it again.

No more rainbow. No more playfulness. No more dancing. All the things that light me up and make me, me.

Gone.
