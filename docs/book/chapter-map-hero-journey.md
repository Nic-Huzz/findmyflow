## Editor Notes for Huzz

**When recording, know your chapter's JOB:**
- If the job is "open the wound" — DON'T resolve it. Leave the reader in the question.
- If the job is "name X" — give them vocabulary they've never had for their experience.
- If the job is "proof chapter" — just tell the story. No lesson. The reader draws their own.
- If the job is "the mechanism" — this is where 29-year-old Nic speaks. The reframe. The insight.
- If the job is "the close" — land it in one line. The brick for the colosseum.

**The rule:** Scene + Tension = 23-year-old Nic (living it). Turn + Land = 29-year-old Nic (the reframe). Never mix them. The confusion earns the clarity.
