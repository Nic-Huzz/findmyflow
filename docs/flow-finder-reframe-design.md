# Flow Finder Reframe: "What Do You Want to Be When You Grow Up?"

> **Status**: Exploring
> **Affects**: Flow Finder Skills, Problems, Personas, MindSpace/Curiosity Compass
> **Core idea**: Reframe the business-y language of Flow Finder into playful, essence-connected language that ties back to the Essence Mirror

---

## The Problem

The current Flow Finder uses language that feels corporate and disconnected from the emotional journey:
- **Skills** → "What are you good at?"
- **Problems** → "What problems do you solve?"
- **Personas** → "Who is your ideal customer?"

This language assumes the user already has a business mindset. But most users at Level 1 (Identity) are still reconnecting with who they are. They just discovered their essence archetype. The jump from "You are fire with a heartbeat" to "What problems do you solve?" is jarring.

## The Reframe

**Umbrella**: "What do you want to be when you grow up?"

This question is playful, disarming, and directly connects to the inner child theme from the Essence Mirror hook slides ("Think about you as a kid and what you loved to do"). It gives users permission to dream without the pressure of having a "business plan."

### Category Reframes

| Current | Reframed | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| **Skills**: "What are you good at?" | **"What did you love doing as a kid?"** | Connects to inner child, bypasses impostor syndrome. Skills aren't what you learned, they're what you couldn't stop doing. |
| **Problems**: "What problems do you solve?" | **"What breaks your heart about the world?"** | Connects to purpose and empathy. Problems aren't business opportunities, they're things you care about deeply enough to act on. |
| **Personas**: "Who is your ideal customer?" | **"Who do you want to help?"** | Removes the transactional framing. Service isn't about "customers", it's about the people whose pain you understand because you've lived it. |

### How It Connects to the Journey

```
Essence Mirror: "Who were you before the world told you who to be?"
    ↓
Curiosity Compass: "What do you want to be when you grow up?"
    ↓
    Skills:   "What did you love doing?"     → What you're naturally drawn to
    Problems: "What breaks your heart?"       → What you care about enough to act on
    Personas: "Who do you want to help?"      → Who you understand because you've been there
    ↓
Integration: "This is your Nikigai. Your essence expressed in service."
```

The whole arc becomes a story of return:
1. **Essence Mirror** — Remember who you are
2. **Curiosity Compass** — Remember what you loved
3. **Zone Diagnosis** — See what stopped you
4. **Shadow Work** — Understand why you hid
5. **Courage Challenges** — Start expressing it again

## Implementation Options

### Option A: Rename Only
Just change the labels and question prompts in the existing flows. Minimal code change, immediate impact.

- Flow Finder Skills → "Curiosity Compass: What You Loved"
- Flow Finder Problems → "Curiosity Compass: What Breaks Your Heart"
- Flow Finder Personas → "Curiosity Compass: Who You Want to Help"

### Option B: Consolidate into Curiosity Compass
Merge all three Flow Finder flows into one unified "Curiosity Compass" experience. Instead of three separate quests, it's one journey with three chapters.

- Chapter 1: "What did you love doing?" (skills extraction via AI conversation)
- Chapter 2: "What breaks your heart?" (problems extraction)
- Chapter 3: "Who do you want to help?" (persona extraction)
- Finale: "This is what you want to be when you grow up" (integration/nikigai reveal)

### Option C: Reframe + New Entry Point
Keep the existing flows but create a new "What do you want to be when you grow up?" wrapper that introduces them with the childhood framing, then launches each as a sub-quest.

## Language Tone Guide

**Avoid:**
- Business jargon: "niche", "ideal customer", "target market", "value proposition"
- Productivity language: "optimize", "leverage", "scale"
- Clinical language: "assessment", "diagnostic", "evaluate"

**Use:**
- Childhood language: "play", "curious", "wonder", "dream", "imagine"
- Heart language: "care about", "breaks your heart", "lights you up", "can't stop thinking about"
- Growth language: "becoming", "discovering", "remembering", "reconnecting"

**The principle:** If a 10-year-old would understand the question, it's right. If it sounds like a LinkedIn post, rewrite it.

## Open Questions

1. **MindSpace AI conversation** — The current MindSpace flow uses AI to extract skills/problems/personas through conversation. The AI prompts would need reframing too. How much of the conversation structure changes vs just the framing?

2. **Existing data** — Users who already completed Flow Finder with the old framing. Do we re-prompt them with the new language, or leave their data as-is?

3. **Level placement** — Currently Flow Finder Skills is Level 0 (always accessible). With the reframe, should "What did you love doing?" be part of Level 1 (Identity) since it's about self-knowledge? Or keep it as pre-level work?

4. **The "grow up" framing** — Does "What do you want to be when you grow up?" work for all personas? Vibe Seekers (disconnected) might love it. Movement Makers (already building) might find it condescending. Do we need persona-aware framing?

5. **Integration with Essence Mirror** — The Essence Mirror already identifies their superpower and vision. Could the Curiosity Compass use that data to prime the conversation? "Your essence is the Heart Alchemist. You transform pain into gold. So... what pain have you seen that you wish someone would transform?"

## Connection to Essence Archetypes

Each archetype has natural curiosity patterns. The Curiosity Compass could use the user's essence to personalize the exploration:

| Archetype | "What did you love?" prompt | "What breaks your heart?" prompt |
|-----------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Radiant Rebel | "What rules did you love breaking?" | "What injustice makes you want to shout?" |
| Playful Creator | "What did you love making?" | "What would be more fun if someone redesigned it?" |
| Heart Alchemist | "What emotions did you love exploring?" | "What pain do you wish someone would finally name?" |
| Compassionate Leader | "Who did you love taking care of?" | "Who gets left behind that shouldn't?" |
| Mystic Messenger | "What invisible things did you love noticing?" | "What truth is everyone ignoring?" |

This personalization would make the Curiosity Compass feel like a natural extension of the Essence Mirror, not a separate assessment.
