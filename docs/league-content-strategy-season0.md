# Vibe Rise League — Content Strategy (Season 0: Huzz as Test Case)

**Status:** Draft
**Purpose:** Execute the league playbook solo to prove the concept, then use the content produced to pitch Season 1 participants.

---

## Content Structure

### DAILY: Tune Actions (Stories / Short Posts)
Low production. Consistent. The streak IS the content.

**Format:** 15-30s story or simple static post
**Cadence:** Every day, same time

| Tune Action | Content Format | Example |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Morning practice | Story: clip of breathwork/cold plunge/movement | "Day 12. Cold plunge. State: Activated → Safe." |
| State check-in | Story: text overlay on selfie | "Today's state: Vibe Rise. First time this week." |
| Drain audit | Story: honest text post | "Drain today: 3 hours on admin that could be automated." |
| Evening reflection | Story: one sentence | "One thing I noticed about myself today." |

**Why this works:** One is boring. 30 in a row is a transformation documentary. The 75 Hard effect.

---

### WEEKLY: Healing Actions + Explainers (1 per week)
Medium production. Vulnerable. The depth play.

**Format:** 60-90s reel or carousel
**Cadence:** Once per week

Share what you recognised, not the wound. Queer Eye principle: empathy-first, recognition-forward.

| Week | Healing Content | Explainer Layer |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | "I recognised I've been using busyness as a Controller pattern" | What the Controller pattern is, how it shows up for creators |
| 2 | "I found the rule I was following that isn't mine" | How inherited rules keep experience creators small |
| 3 | "I processed why I undercharge" | The link between self-worth wounds and pricing |
| 4 | "What shifted after 4 weeks of this practice" | Before/after honest assessment |

**Why this works:** Explainer = educational. Personal story = relatable. Together = someone who's done the work AND can teach it.

---

### WEEKLY: Wahoo Actions + BTS (1 per week)
Raw footage. High drama. The spectacle play.

**Format:** 60s-3min reel, BTS style (shaky cam fine, real > polished)
**Cadence:** Once per week

Structure: **Setup** (what you're about to do and why it scares you) → **Action** (footage of doing it) → **Reveal** (how it felt, was that a Wahoo?)

| Week | Wahoo | BTS Content |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Share actual revenue numbers publicly | Film writing the post, hesitating, hitting publish. Film reactions. |
| 2 | Go live and teach "Blow Up Your Brand" to strangers | Film the prep, nerves, the live itself, debrief after |
| 3 | Run a free Shift Architecture experience, invite strangers | Film setup, room filling, session, one participant's reaction |
| 4 | Open applications for Season 1 publicly | Film building the pitch, sending it, waiting for responses |

**Why this works:** This IS Drive to Survive. Audience sees fear, decision, and aftermath. That's what makes it followable.

---

### WEEKLY: Business Topic Posts (1 per week)
Higher production. Strategic. The authority play.

**Format:** Carousel (graph carousel format) or 2-3min talking-head reel
**Cadence:** Once per week, tied to /create playbook theme

| Week | Theme | Topic Post |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1: Pay Rent | "How to fund your movement before anyone notices" | Carousel: 5 revenue models real creators used |
| 2: Blow Up | "The one rule you're following that's keeping you invisible" | Carousel: Remarkable angle framework, Two Worlds, rule statement |
| 3: Scale | "What to give away vs what to charge for" | Carousel: 3-layer model (attraction/core/continuity) with examples |
| 4: Launch | "What 4 weeks of this playbook actually produced" | Carousel: Before/after metrics, content created, what shifted |

**Why this works:** Posts that get saved and shared. Positions you as framework builder, not just challenge participant.

## /create × 7-Day Challenge Relationship

/create is the **playbook** (what to build). The 7-day challenge is the **execution engine**.

- `/create` Playbook gives Wahoos direction: "launch your attraction offer" not "do something brave"
- `/create` Inner Game surfaces the blocks: visibility limits, income limits, wounds, limiting beliefs
- Healing category clears those blocks
- Wahoo category executes the playbook publicly
- Tune category maintains capacity

### /create Portal Structure (Implemented)

**Identity Tab** has two sub-tabs:

**Playbook** (business-building):
- Blow Up Brand (remarkable angle, rule break, tribe)
- Your Model (Pay Rent + Scale Income layers)

**Inner Game** (capacity-building):
- Play Profile (how you work best)
- Know Your Ceiling (nervous system visibility + income limits)
- Wound Map (origin story, the wound behind the work)
- Limiting Beliefs (rewire pricing/visibility/launch blocks)

Same flows accessible from both /create portal AND the levels journey tab. Shared data.
