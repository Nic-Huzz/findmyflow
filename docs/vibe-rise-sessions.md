# Vibe Rise Sessions

## The Category

Vibe Rise isn't a wellness event. It's not therapy, not fitness, not a retreat. It's a new category.

**The premise the self-help industry gets wrong:** "You need to figure out what you want." Therapy, coaching, meditation apps, self-help books all assume the problem is knowledge. Find your passion. Discover your purpose. Set better goals.

**The truth:** You already know what you want. You've known for years. You're in Head Full of Dreams. The problem isn't that you can't see the life. The problem is your body says "not safe" every time you try to move toward it. Your cone of safety keeps you in careers, relationships, and patterns that feel survivable but unfulfilling.

**What Vibe Rise does:** Expands the range of life paths that feel possible. The paths that used to feel like Pressure become Fun. The ones that felt impossible become Vibe Rise. Not through thinking harder. Through training your body to feel safe in bigger arenas.

**One-sentence strategy:** "You already know the life you want. Your body won't let you go get it. Vibe Rise expands what feels safe until it does."

**DAM statement:** "You think you need therapy or a meditation app. But you already know what you want. Your body just won't let you go get it. What you actually need is a weekly practice that expands what feels safe. That's Vibe Rise."

## The Gap: Fitness x Wellness x Ambition

Three industries train three parts of you. None of them talk to each other.

| | Fitness | Wellness | Ambition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | CrossFit, cold plunge, rucking | Breathwork, therapy, meditation | Masterminds, coaching, networking |
| **Trains** | The body | The nervous system | The mind |
| **Doesn't** | Change what feels safe | Give you courage to act | Give you the body or safety |

Your gym doesn't know your life goals. Your therapist doesn't know your courage challenges. Your mastermind doesn't know your nervous system state.

Separately, each caps out. A strong body without a regulated nervous system still avoids risk. A regulated nervous system without courage still stays small. Ambition without safety burns out.

**Vibe Rise sits at the intersection:**
- Connection circle = the mastermind (ambition, life path accountability)
- Healing experience = the wellness (breathwork, somatic, NS regulation)
- Wahoo experience = the fitness-adjacent courage (cold plunge, physical challenge, public vulnerability)
- Identity close = what ties them together (who are you becoming across ALL three?)

The cone of safety expands when all three compound: fitness expands it physically, wellness expands it emotionally, courage expands it socially. Together they produce life path expansion that none of them achieve alone.

## The Session Format

Weekly. 90-120 minutes. 15-30 people. Outdoors where possible (beach, park, fire pit).

### Structure

```
1. CONNECTION (20 min)
   Life path circle. No small talk.
   - Wins: "What life path progress happened this week?"
   - Learnings: "What did you discover about yourself?"
   - Surprises: "What felt safe this week that didn't before?"
   → Purpose: builds the tribe, creates accountability, makes cone expansion visible

2. HEALING EXPERIENCE (25-30 min)
   One facilitated healing/regulation practice.
   Delivered by the host OR a special guest.
   - Examples: breathwork journey, guided somatic release,
     sound bath, nervous system regulation, cacao ceremony
   → Purpose: expands the cone of safety from the inside (removes what's holding it small)

3. WAHOO EXPERIENCE (25-30 min)
   One courage challenge that pushes the boundary.
   Delivered by a COMMUNITY MEMBER who shares their experience.
   - Examples: cold plunge (beach), public vulnerability share,
     freestyle dance, first-time facilitation, live pitch to the group
   → Purpose: expands the cone of safety from the outside (proves you can handle more)

4. CLOSE (15 min)
   Circle format. Each person: one sentence.
   "After today, I'm someone who ___"
   → Purpose: identity formation. This is where the TRIBAL pillar activates.
```

### The Community Member Wahoo

The wahoo experience is delivered by a COMMUNITY MEMBER, not the host. This is deliberate:

1. **3% chain activation** — the person facilitating their wahoo IS training to become a facilitator. Every session produces potential future hosts.
2. **Proof of concept** — when a community member leads the cold plunge or shares their vulnerability, it's more powerful than a professional doing it. "If they can do it, I can do it."
3. **Progressive exposure** — leading a wahoo for 20 people IS a wahoo on the Authority layer of the Wahoo Map. The facilitator is expanding THEIR cone while expanding the group's.
4. **Content engine** — the community member's wahoo is inherently remarkable. "A first-time facilitator led us through a cold plunge on the beach at 6am." That's a story the group tells unprompted.

### The App Bridge (Strava Model)

Between sessions, the app serves as the progress ledger:

| What happens in-person | What the app tracks between sessions |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Connection circle (wins/learnings/surprises) | Quest progress, tasks completed |
| Healing experience | Tune Tab practices, Capacity Score |
| Wahoo experience | Wahoo Map completions, layer progress |
| Identity close ("I'm someone who ___") | Identity statements saved, streak |

**The flywheel:** In-person session creates the L3 communal experience. App tracks L1-L2 daily practice between sessions. Next session, the group sees each other's progress. Accountability + visibility + community = habit formation.

Like Strava for runners: the app doesn't replace the run. It makes the run visible to your crew.

| Variant | Healing Element | Wahoo Element | Best Location |
|---------|----------------|---------------|:---:|

| **Forest Rise** | Forest bathing / nature immersion | Barefoot trail run | Forest/bush, morning |

Each variant merges 2-3 branches from the Phase 3 Modality Map. This is the Merge Pattern operationalised.

## Relationship to the App (Nike Run Club / Strava Model)

The app is the progress ledger between sessions. Not a replacement for the in-person experience. A bridge that makes your growth visible to your crew.

**What Strava does for runners, Vibe Rise does for life path expansion:**
- Strava tracks runs → crew sees your weekly mileage → accountability + identity
- Vibe Rise tracks courage + healing + practices → crew sees your cone expansion → accountability + identity

**Key insight:** Digital works as bridge to physical community, not replacement. Strava users in clubs are 2x more likely to train weekly (Strava 2025 data). The app connects to sessions because your session crew ARE the people you're in a fantasy competition against.
