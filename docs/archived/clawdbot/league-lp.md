## SECTION 10: FAQ

```
Frequently Asked Questions
```

**Q: What if I'm just starting my business?**
A: Perfect. Business Efficiency scores *depth per challenge*, not how far along you are. A founder at Stage 1 (Validation) competes on equal footing with someone at Stage 5 (Offers). What matters is how many challenges you complete and how deep you go.

**Q: What if I don't have time?**
A: 30 minutes a day. That's one podcast episode. One gym session. One Netflix episode. If your team is counting on you, you'll find 30 minutes.

**Q: Can I pick my team?**
A: No. Teams are assigned to create balance — mixed stages, mixed archetypes, mixed businesses. The diversity is part of the design. Your teammate's strength is your blind spot.

**Q: What's the scoring based on?**
A: Real actions inside FindMyFlow: completing business challenges, doing Groan (fear) challenges, daily healing practices, voice reflections, and creating content. No self-reporting. No honour system on the core 4. Content is verified via screenshots.

**Q: What if I fall behind?**
A: Your team will notice. That's the point. This isn't an app you can ghost — you have 2 people counting on your points this week.

**Q: Is this just for entrepreneurs?**
A: It's for anyone building something — a business, a side project, a creative practice, or honestly, a better relationship with themselves. The business stages adapt to where you are.

**Q: What happens after Season 1?**
A: If it works — Season 2, bigger, louder, with alumni captains drafting new teams. If it doesn't — we learn, we iterate, we go again. This is a beta. You're the founding players.

## PAGE-LEVEL DESIGN NOTES

### Colour Palette
- Primary: Deep purple (#5e17eb) — FindMyFlow brand
- Accent: Gold (#ffdd27) — for CTAs, "Season 1", highlights
- Background: Alternating dark (purple-black) and light (warm cream) sections
- Category colours: Each of the 5 categories gets a unique accent (business blue, play green, healing pink, voice amber, content teal)

### Typography
- Headlines: Large serif or display font (contrast with body)
- Body: Clean sans-serif, 18-20px
- Numbers/stats: Monospace or bold sans — sports broadcast feel

### Animations
- Scroll-triggered reveals for each section
- Hero: subtle particle grid (Matrix reference)
- Category cards: slight float/hover effect
- Scorecard: animated counting up of numbers
- CTA button: gentle pulse

### Mobile Priorities
- Hero → Problem → Reframe → Categories (swipe) → How It Works → CTA
- Collapse FAQ into accordion
- Sticky "Apply" button at bottom of screen after hero scroll
- All images optimised, lazy-loaded

### Conversion Flow
1. Land on /league
2. Scroll through story
3. Click "Apply for Season 1"
4. → Earthquake Quiz (earthquake-quiz.vercel.app or embedded)
5. → After quiz results: "Want to compete? Apply below"
6. → Short application form: Name, email, quiz results (auto-filled), "Why do you want in?" (one line), business stage
7. → Confirmation: "We'll announce teams on [DATE]. Check your email."
