# Why I Created This — Slide Drafts

## Draft 1: The Hero's Journey

**1. THE ORDINARY WORLD**
13 years of school. Years at uni. Part-time jobs and internships. All because at the end of it there was supposed to be a golden carrot — happiness, fulfillment, and all the rest.

Why else does everyone follow this path?

**2. THE BETRAYAL**
I followed that path and it proved untrue.

The most painful experience of my life was failing to find fulfillment in what I thought was a dream job. I felt fucking betrayed.

**3. THE EARTHQUAKE**
The best way I can describe it — it's like an earthquake hit my city of understanding.

Every belief I had about the world that I adopted from others came tumbling down.

**4. THE WANDERING**
For five years, day after day, I've been rebuilding that city brick by brick — based on my own experiences, not from what other people told me.

**5. THE REVELATION**
> "You don't rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems." — James Clear

In that moment I realized there must be a system that leads to any goal. I saw it as my mission to figure out exactly what that system is.

**6. THE TRIALS**
This app is by no means version one.

1. Rudder Life Purpose Program
2. Overcome Fierce
3. The Growth Game
4. Find My Flow

Each version built on blood, sweat, tears — shit that actually worked when it came to monetizing my mission and finding independence.

**7. THE DEEPEST CAVE**
I realized we don't rise to the level of our ambitions — we fall to the level that feels safe.

Often it's not a lack of knowledge or desire. Our nervous system doesn't feel safe taking the action we know we need to take.

**8. THE ELIXIR**
All the business steps to launch your thing — married with the most useful healing exercises I've come across on my journey. Wrapped in a game.

We're not just making healing fun. We're making business fun.

**9. THE RETURN**
I see my role in life as being an energizer bunny — empowering everyone to have fun all the fucking time.

Because what else would there be to do all day?
