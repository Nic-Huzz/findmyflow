# PlaySkill Taxonomy Revision Rationale

Date: 2026-04-07
Input: 712 high-confidence positive tags across 275 people, overlaid with 164 anti-tags
Scope: Revise the 60 skill strings (12 categories x 5 strings). Categories held fixed per product decision.

## 1. Summary

The bottom-up clustering produced 60 natural clusters from 712 quotes. 52 of those clusters mapped cleanly to a single existing category (purity >= 80%). 4 clusters split across categories (the real gaps). 4 clusters were orphans. Anti-tags were scanned separately and produced one additional major cluster (anti-visibility) that the positive corpus could not express.

**Counts:**
- Kept as-is (pure rename): 46 of 60
- Refined meaning: 3
- Renamed (split): 4
- Retired: 3 (Redesign something ugly, Diagnose why something is not working, Move someone from stuck to action, Clean up something no one wants to touch, Write something in your actual voice)
- New strings: 5 (Trust a felt pattern before the numbers prove it, Stay the course when no one else will, Coordinate a federation without controlling it, Know what is good before others see it, Choose to work without being seen, Put your body where your argument is)

Net change: 6 strings replaced, 54 unchanged.

## 2. Per-category walkthrough

### clarifying (64 quotes, 5 strings) — NO CHANGES
All five strings earned their place. Clarifying is the strongest-supported category in the corpus. The 5 strings are clean primitives with no internal overlap.

### analyzing (69 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 MERGE, 1 NEW
- Merged: Diagnose-why-not-working + Investigate-weird-anomaly into "Investigate a weird anomaly until it cracks". Only 7 tags held the diagnose label and 5 of them fit investigate cleanly. The merge frees a slot for the analyzing gap.
- New: "Trust a felt pattern before the numbers prove it". Resolves the 10+ founders anti-tagged on analyzing (Lauder, Branson, Blakely, Wolfe Herd, Disney, Land, Jobs-on-taste, McClintock's maize-feel, Goodall's naming chimps). Gives the intuitive-primary pattern readers a positive home.

### strategizing (45 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 NEW
- New: "Stay the course when no one else will". Resolves the long-arc discipline gap (McClintock 35 yrs, Goodall 60 yrs, Jekyll 400 commissions, Szymborska 6 poems/yr, Hadid's 16 paper-architect years, Murakami, Butler, Attenborough). Placed in strategizing because the underlying move is a STRATEGIC commitment to an unusually long time horizon.
- Kept: the other 4 strings unchanged.

### organizing (35 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 MERGE, 1 NEW
- Merged: "Clean up something no one wants to touch" into "Turn a mess into a system". Only 3 anchors for clean-up and all 3 fit mess-into-system.
- New: "Coordinate a federation without controlling it". Resolves the federation-vs-systematize gap. Covers Alinsky's IAF, Dorothy Day's 30+ Houses, Mackenzie Scott's grants, Branson's Virgin federation, Jimmy Carter's observer network, Maathai's tree nurseries, Ella Baker's distributed NAACP branches.

### building (33 quotes, 5 strings) — NO CHANGES
All five strings anchored cleanly. Lowest-count category but the primitives are sharp.

### designing (38 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 SPLIT
- Replaced: "Redesign something ugly" (only 2 anchors, oddly negative framing) with "Know what is good before others see it". Resolves the taste / aesthetic judgment gap. Covers Jobs, Chanel, Ive, Arnault, Rams, Hara, Ando, Vignelli, Castiglioni, Kurosawa, Wong Kar-wai. Names the aesthetic authority move cleanly.

### creating (81 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 REFINEMENT
- Refined: "Invent a new idea from scratch" now explicitly means "coin a new concept or method" to differentiate it from the product-oriented "Make something that did not exist yesterday". Anchored by Hedy Lamarr (patent), Mary Ainsworth (Strange Situation procedure), Janusz Korczak (democratic children's republic).
- Creating also absorbs most of the retired "Write something in your actual voice" tags via "Fill a blank page with something real" — see expressing notes.

### expressing (121 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 SPLIT
THIS IS THE LARGEST CHANGE IN THE PROPOSAL.
- Retired: "Write something in your actual voice" (27 anchors, sprawling across incompatible sub-meanings). The bulk of its tags (~18) migrate to creating/"Fill a blank page with something real" which fits daily-practice writers like Morrison, Angelou, Baldwin, Murakami, Patti Smith, Mukherjee. A handful (~4) migrate to "Say the thing people are thinking but will not" (Kae Tempest, Susan Fowler, Robin Wall Kimmerer, Rebecca Solnit). The remaining ~5 become positive anchors for:
- New: "Choose to work without being seen". Resolves the anti-visibility gap that 18+ anti-tags pointed to. Rockefeller, Thorp, Knight, Munger, Land, Feeney, McClintock, Pearl Kazin Bell, Szymborska, Attenborough. Gives the deliberate-invisibility founders and artists a positive home.

### connecting (45 quotes, 5 strings) — NO CHANGES (but 2 flags)
- "Introduce two people who should know each other" has only 2 high-confidence anchors. Kept because the intuition is real but flagged.
- "Be the glue in a scattered team" has only 3 anchors. Also flagged.
- The other 3 strings anchor cleanly.

### influencing (41 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 SPLIT
- Replaced: "Move someone from stuck to action" (sprawled across motivational-speaker quotes and body-as-argument quotes). Split: body-as-argument half becomes new string; exhortation half merges into "Rally a group behind an idea".
- New: "Put your body where your argument is". Resolves the body-as-argument gap. Gandhi, Maathai, Chavez, Greta, Politkovskaya, Pat Summitt, Jaime Escalante.

### nurturing (54 quotes, 5 strings) — NO CHANGES
All five strings anchor cleanly. "Hold space for a hard moment" is the strongest cluster in the category (21 anchors) and absorbs the refuse-to-leave and harms-way cases (Korczak, Sendler, Farmer, Tubman) naturally — the original taggers' instinct was correct.

### synthesizing (86 quotes, 5 strings) — 1 REFINEMENT
- Refined: "Connect dots across two worlds" now explicitly includes translation as the archetype (for Grossman, Rabassa, Steiner, Heaney, Achebe's Igbo-English). Meaning refinement, not a new string. 5 translator quotes is below the dedicated-slot threshold.

## 4. Mutual-exclusivity gate results

Sampled 100 random quotes. Single-match 82, multi-match 13, zero-match 5. Gate passes (multi-match below 15 ceiling, zero-match below 10 ceiling) with one warning: the synthesizing category accounts for most of the multi-match count because "Find the bigger pattern under the noise" and "Build a framework that holds it all" are genuinely cousin primitives.

Zero-match cases were all edge quotes where the closest-fit string was ambiguous rather than absent.

## 5. Category-level flags (advisory, not changes)

1. **Synthesizing internal overlap**: Pattern-under-noise and framework-that-holds-it-all overlap ~40%. Candidate for sharpening in a v2.
2. **Connecting floor**: 2 of 5 strings are below the 5-anchor threshold at high confidence. The category's 5-slot constraint is harder to fill than the other categories.
3. **Expressing sprawl**: 121 quotes makes expressing 3x the size of building (33). It could naturally split into public-voice vs personal-voice. Constraint-blocked.
4. **Strategizing top-heaviness**: 2 strings take 32 of 45 quotes. The other 3 strings are thin.
5. **Orphan**: Mary Catherine Bateson's "Composing a Life" doesn't fit any category cleanly.

## 6. Migration notes

1. **"Write something in your actual voice" (27 tags)** — largest migration. ~18 to creating/Fill a blank page, ~4 to expressing/Say the thing, ~5 to the new expressing/Choose to work without being seen. Full per-person routing in the migration map JSON.
2. **"Diagnose why something is not working" (7 tags)** — merge into analyzing/Investigate a weird anomaly until it cracks. 5 fit cleanly. Walter Chrysler and Kobe Bryant cases need manual review (self-diagnosis).
3. **"Move someone from stuck to action" (8 tags)** — split. Body-as-argument half to influencing/Put your body where your argument is. Exhortation half to influencing/Rally a group behind an idea.
4. **"Clean up something no one wants to touch" (3 tags)** — merge into organizing/Turn a mess into a system. All 3 fit cleanly.
5. **"Redesign something ugly" (2 tags)** — rewrite to designing/Know what is good before others see it. Also needs ~10 new positive tags for Jobs, Rams, Hara, Ando, Vignelli, Castiglioni, etc.

Additional new-string tagging obligations (to seed anchors for user-matching):
- "Trust a felt pattern before the numbers prove it" — ~10-14 new positive tags
- "Stay the course when no one else will" — ~8-12 new positive tags
- "Coordinate a federation without controlling it" — ~7-10 new positive tags

Total rework estimate: ~115 tag edits across 275 people. Approximately 9% of the 1252-item corpus.

## 7. Open questions for human review

1. **Is retiring "Write something in your actual voice" the right call?** This is the single most contentious decision. The string is beloved as a tagline and appears in 27 anchors — but those anchors cover diary writers, protest essayists, body-witnesses and daily-practice writers. The string is popular because it's vague, which is exactly why it's a bad taxonomy string. Alternative: keep it and retire a different expressing string (e.g. demote "Turn a message into a moment" which overlaps with "Perform, present, or hit record" at 8 of 17 anchors). Requires human judgment call.

2. **Should "Stay the course when no one else will" live in strategizing or nurturing?** I placed it in strategizing because the move reads as CALCULATED patience with the long game as the edge. But the warm/relational reading (patience as an ethical virtue) would fit nurturing. Evidence split 60/40 toward strategizing.

3. **Is "Trust a felt pattern before the numbers prove it" the right framing for the intuition primitive, or should it be "Make decisions with your gut"?** The more testable framing (pattern-before-proof) is more precise but less relatable. The gut framing is more relatable but conflates intuition with snap judgments. I chose precision.

4. **Should the translator cluster get its own slot?** 5 anchors is below my threshold but the cluster is conceptually distinct enough that splitting synthesizing/"Connect dots" might be the right call if the taggers add 3-5 more translator entries in a second pass. Defer to v2.

5. **Connecting has 2 weak strings** (Introduce two people = 2 anchors, Be the glue = 3 anchors). Should connecting shrink to 3 strings, or should the 2 weak ones be replaced with stronger primitives pulled from the corpus? Candidates: "Remember the names of people who are usually invisible" (Joyce Banda, Fred Rogers' correspondence, Pearl Kazin Bell's quoting-lines-back). But this is a v2 decision.

6. **The refuse-to-leave / stand-in-harms-way cases** (Korczak, Sendler, Farmer, Tubman, Paul Farmer's TB walk) are absorbed into "Hold space for a hard moment" per the taggers' original instinct. A separate string ("Refuse to leave") would also work and would give the moral-stand cases their own identity. 6 anchors is right at the threshold. Defer to v2 unless feedback says this absorption loses too much.
