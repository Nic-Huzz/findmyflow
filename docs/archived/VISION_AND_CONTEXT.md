# FindMyFlow - Vision & Context

> Last updated: January 2026

## The Big Picture

FindMyFlow is a complete "burnt-out professional → thriving business owner" transformation system. It uniquely combines:

- **Inner Work Track** - Nervous system healing, archetype discovery, protective voice work
- **Discovery Track** - Skills, problems, persona identification (Nikigai framework)
- **Business Building Track** - Money Models, offers, funnels, lead magnets (Hormozi-inspired)
- **Execution Track** - CRM, sales pipeline, ascension engine, content system
- **Accountability Track** - 7-day challenges, gamification, Zarlo AI coaching

**The unique angle**: You can't build a sustainable business while running on trauma patterns. Most business tools ignore the human; most personal development ignores the business. FindMyFlow bridges both.

## Two CRM Flows

1. **Sales Pipeline** (Pre-customer)
   - Lead → Booked → Meeting → Won/Lost
   - Traditional sales tracking

2. **Value Ladder / Ascension Engine** (Post-customer)
   - Tracks where customers are on the Money Model
   - Attraction → Core → Upsell/Downsell → Continuity
   - Suggests next ascension actions

**Key insight**: "Won" means different things:
- Attraction offer win = Lead captured (not a sale)
- Core offer win = Actual sale
- Upsell/Continuity win = Expanded relationship
