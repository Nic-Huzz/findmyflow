# Europe Tour Content Plan

## Kenj (Agency) — 9 Videos

### Arc Videos (3)
1. **Tour Intro** — The journey, the van, the mission. 20-30s.
2. **Mid-Tour Check-in** — Raw reflection halfway through. 20-30s.
3. **End-of-Tour Highlight** — Full montage payoff. 20-30s.

### Venue Highlights (6)
One 20-30s highlight reel per monument event. Crowd reactions, headphones-on moment, dancing. Cinematic, punchy, shareable. First venue doubles as the "proof of concept" moment.

### Fantasy League — 4 Videos Per Week (16 per season)

| Day | Video | What it looks like | Length |
|-----|-------|--------------------|--------|
| Monday | **Matchup Preview** | Player photo + biz one-liner, side-by-side. "This week's matchup" energy. | 15s |
| Mid-week | **My Check-in** | Me to camera. What I'm personally working through. A healing insight, a Wahoo I did, what came up. Authentic, not polished. My own journey as the case study. | 30-60s |
| Friday | **Weekly Highlights** | Compilation reel. Best Wahoo moments + healing breakthroughs from all players. Their footage, quick cuts. The "episode." | 30-45s |
| Sunday | **Results Recap** | Scoreboard, who won which categories, standings update, season narrative. Screen grabs from the app + voiceover. | 15-20s |

Players submit their own footage for the highlights reel.
