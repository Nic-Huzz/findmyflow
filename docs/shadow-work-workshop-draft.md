## Workshop Summary (5 Acts)

### Act 1 — What Are Shadows? (slides 1-6)

Shadows are any part of ourselves we suppress. Two reasons we suppress:

1. Society or culture told us it's "wrong" or "bad"
2. It's a trait we've hidden because we've been hurt emotionally

**Exercise:** Write down 5 things you suppress about yourself (interests, desires, thoughts).

Prompt questions:
- "What situations make you play smaller than you truly are?"
- "Where in your life do you push, perform, or prove something?"
- "Where do you avoid saying how you really feel?"
- "What trait do you hide because you believe it won't be accepted?"

Key principle: **shadows can't survive in the light** — sharing circles throughout the workshop bring shadows into the open.

**Exercise:** Think about sharing this shadow with the world. What are you scared would happen if you did?

---

### Act 2 — Root Emotion Mapping (slides 7-14)

Almost all shadows stem from a fear of feeling one of 3 root emotions:

| Emotion | Inner belief |
|---------|-------------|
| **Shame** | "Something is wrong with me" |
| **Guilt** | "I shouldn't be this way" |
| **Fear** | "If I express this, I'll lose safety" |

**Exercise:** Label each of your 5 shadow traits with its root emotion.

Reference: Emotional Frequencies chart (Hawkins scale) — shame (20), guilt (30), fear (100) sit at the very bottom. The workshop implicitly shows that shadow work raises your emotional frequency.

Nobody likes feeling shame, fear, guilt. So to hide these shadows we adopt **protective behaviours**.

---

### Act 3 — Protective Archetypes (slides 15-20)

The 5 Protective Archetypes (already in FindMyFlow):

| Archetype | Behaviour |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Ghost** | Disappears, withdraws, avoids being seen |
| **Performer** | Puts on a mask, seeks external validation |
| **Controller** | Manages everything to feel safe |
| **Perfectionist** | Nothing is ever good enough, delays action |
| **People Pleaser** | Sacrifices own needs to be liked |

**Exercise:** Map each shadow trait to the protective archetype it activates.

Example from workshop:
- "Not worth their time" (shame) → **Ghost**
- "Looking but not telling anyone" (guilt) → **Performer**
- "Weak boundaries because I want to be liked" (fear) → **People Pleaser**

Summary so far:
- Our shadows are parts of ourselves we suppress
- We suppress them to protect ourselves from shame, fear, embarrassment
- We use protective archetypes as our armour
- Visualise: anytime you're a protective archetype, it's creating a shadow to hide a part of you

---

### Act 4 — Suppressed Essence (slides 21-35)

**The twist:** Sometimes authentic parts of ourselves become a shadow.

> "Remember a shadow is any part of ourselves we suppress. But why would we suppress our authentic parts?"

**Exercise:** Think about yourself as a kid. Things you used to love to do. Now imagine getting teased, made fun of, or rejected for those things. How would that feel?

Answer: Horrible. Shameful. Embarrassed.

And we never want to feel that way, so we suppress that part of us.

**Exercise:** Identify a suppressed essence trait — something you would love to do or experience but hold back from.

- What are all the actions required to do or experience that thing?
- What's stopping you from doing them?
- Instead of expressing that essence, which protective archetype have you embodied?

---

### Act 5 — Timeline & Origin Story (slides 36-51)

> "I'm assuming you didn't wake up today and decide to be this protective archetype... So there's something from the time you were a kid to today, that has caused it to show-up."

**Exercise — Timeline visualisation:**
1. Think of a time in your life where you embodied this essence or shadow archetype
2. What happened back then? A particular moment or series of events?
3. If you're okay to revisit... how did you feel afterwards? What emotions arose? (Shame, embarrassment, fear?)
4. What made you feel that way?

**Connect the dots:**

> This experience you identified growing-up...
> Was so impactful — you hated the way it made you feel so much...
> You now hide away the version of you who wants to do it...
> And become the protective archetype instead.

**Personal examples from workshop:**
- **Shadow trait:** Being rejected from women growing-up → hated the shame → became someone who shy'd away from pretty girls → became a **Ghost**
- **Essence trait:** Being teased about dressing as a rainbow growing-up → hated the embarrassment → stopped wearing colourful clothes, wore beer labels instead → became the **Performer**

**Resolution:**

> So why do our shadows exist? Because they are parts of us that were made to feel unaccepted. So we hide them away instead of embracing them.
>
> Even our authentic parts can become shadows. Because in fact all shadows ARE authentic parts!

### Design Notes

**Teach steps:**
- Read-only, no input required
- Gold text on purple gradient background (matching the workshop's Huzz branding and FindMyFlow's brand)
- Just a "Continue" button
- These are emotional pacing beats, not data collection

**Input steps:**
- Use existing `useSteppedForm` pattern
- Step 3 produces an array of shadow objects that steps 4, 6, 8 all annotate (same items, adding fields)
- Validation: minimum 1 shadow trait in step 3, everything else optional

**Step 13 (Connect the Dots):**
- Auto-generated from captured data — the "mirror moment"
- This is the most impactful step — the user sees their own pattern laid out clearly
- Template: "This experience of **{step12.event}** made you feel **{step12.emotion}**. To protect yourself, you started hiding **{step10.essence_trait || step3.shadow[0]}** and became the **{step10.archetype || step8.archetypes[0]}** instead."

**Sharing circles → journaling:**
- The live workshop has 7 sharing circles where participants speak to the group
- In-app, these become the input steps themselves — writing it down = bringing it to light
- Optional: "Share with Zarlo" button on teach steps (AI as safe witness)


### Open Questions

1. **Repeat completions:** Should users be able to redo this flow with different shadows each time, or is it one-and-done that builds a growing shadow inventory?
2. **Privacy:** Shadow data is deeply personal. Should it be flagged as extra-private in the DB (not included in any exports/summaries unless explicitly opted in)?
3. **Zarlo integration:** Should Zarlo be able to reference shadow work data in conversations? ("I notice your Ghost tends to show up when you're facing visibility challenges...")
4. **Groan Matrix generation:** Should completing shadow work auto-generate groan challenges targeting those specific shadows?
