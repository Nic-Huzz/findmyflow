# Remarkable Sequence Analysis
*Testing the hypothesis: all 5 triggers exist for every remarkable creator, possibly in sequence.*
*20 creators across 6 experience categories. April 2026.*

---

## The Hypothesis

All 5 remarkable triggers are present for every creator who achieved significant recognition:
1. **Rule Broken** — did what the field says not to
2. **Unexpected Combination** — combined things that don't belong together
3. **Extreme Degree** — committed to an absurd level
4. **Stupid Simplicity** — found the one simple truth
5. **Impossible Result** — produced evidence that defies belief

Possible sequence: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 (breaking rules creates combinations, extreme commitment reveals simplicity, simplicity produces impossible results).

## Results

- **All 5 clearly present:** 11 out of 20 (55%)
- **4 out of 5 present:** 9 out of 20 (45%)
- **Fewer than 4:** 0 out of 20 (0%)

**Every creator has at least 4 of 5 triggers.** The ones with only 4 are missing either Rule Broken (Ali Abdaal, James Clear) or Stupid Simplicity (Jay Shetty, Esther Perel) or have PARTIAL versions.

**The trigger that's ALWAYS present:** Extreme Degree (19/20, only Holly Whitaker is partial) and Impossible Result (18/20). These two seem non-negotiable.

**The trigger most often partial:** Stupid Simplicity (5 partial) and Rule Broken (3 partial). These may be outcomes that emerge LATER rather than ingredients that must be present.

## Observations on the Sequence

The proposed sequence (Rule Broken → Unexpected Combination → Extreme Degree → Stupid Simplicity → Impossible Result) holds for the 11 strongest cases. For the other 9:

- **James Clear, Simon Sinek, Ali Abdaal:** The sequence is 3 → 4 → 5 (extreme degree → simplicity → impossible result) WITHOUT a strong rule-break or unexpected combination. They were remarkably consistent and found simplicity through commitment. The rule they "broke" was the ATTENTION ECONOMY itself (they refused to chase trends).

- **Jay Shetty:** Strong on 1-2 (monk → Instagram) but weaker on 3-4 (shorter commitment, less distilled insight). He may have achieved scale through distribution (celebrity connections) more than through the full 5-trigger path.

- **Holly Whitaker:** Strong on 1-2 (feminist AA critique) but shorter timeline and smaller scale. She may still be in the MIDDLE of the sequence.

**The pattern that emerges:** The 5 triggers may not all need to be INTENTIONAL. Some emerge as byproducts:
- You break a rule (intentional)
- That creates an unexpected combination (byproduct of the rule-break)
- You commit to extreme degree (intentional)
- Stupid simplicity emerges FROM the extreme commitment (byproduct)
- Impossible result follows from simplicity meeting trust (byproduct)

**Intentional ingredients: 1, 3 (rule-break and extreme commitment)**
**Emergent outcomes: 2, 4, 5 (unexpected combination, simplicity, impossible results)**

This is significant for users: you only need to DO two things (break a rule and commit extremely). The other three HAPPEN as a result.

## Open Questions

2. Is "Impossible Result" the same as "Attention" on the Marketing Sweet Spot? If so, it's not a trigger, it's the Y-axis.
