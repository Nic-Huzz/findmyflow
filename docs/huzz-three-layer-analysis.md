## The Three Layers

### Attraction: Content that teaches your frameworks

Play-skills driving this layer:
- "Making the complex simple enough that anyone can hold it"
- "Naming things so they stick"
- "Telling the story the room needs to hear"
- "Bridging two worlds that don't know they need each other"

Your attraction IS your teaching. Instagram carousels explaining emotional splinters. The rainbow clothes story. The Scope Map diagram. Every piece of content is a mini-framework delivered in your voice. Not inspirational quotes. Framework drops.

The format that fits: short-form content where one framework concept is explained through one personal story. The LEGO analogy for tech. The rainbow clothes for emotional splinters. The Groan Matrix for courage. Each post teaches one thing using language a Bali tourist can feel in their body.

### Core: Live experiences where people feel the framework in their body

Play-skills driving this layer:
- "Getting a room to lean forward"
- "Creating the moment something finally clicks"
- "Holding space for a conversation nobody else will host"
- "Making the moment itself the message"
- "Walking in with ENERGY and turning audiences into participants within minutes"

The core isn't a course. It's a live container (Healing Compass, Rewiring Limiting Beliefs, Shaking Breathwork) where the framework stops being intellectual and becomes embodied. You're at your best when you turn a room of strangers into participants within minutes.

The format that fits: 60-120 minute live workshops with a clear arc (Shift Architecture 6-phase). High energy opening, somatic activation, embodied mismatch, discharge, integration. The talent show compression skill means you can also do short-form powerful moments (festival sets, pop-ups, guest spots).

### Continuity: The frameworks themselves, packaged to run without you

Play-skills driving this layer:
- "Building a framework others can use without you"
- "Building a system that runs without you in the room"
- "Seeing a future nobody else sees and building the rules to get there"
- "Remixing frameworks nobody thought to combine into one original system"
- "Generating original IP by thinking out loud with an AI"

This is FindMyFlow (the app), Shift Architecture (licensable methodology), and eventually a book. The IP scales. You don't.

The format that fits: app-first delivery (FindMyFlow), methodology documentation (Shift Architecture), certification/licensing (teach others to run your workshops), and eventually a book that captures the frameworks in permanent form.

## What the Data Reveals

**Performing (3) > Connecting (2).** You're not primarily a space-holder. You're a performer who creates moments. The talent show story, the Pipe Cleaner, walking in with ENERGY. Core offer should lean toward events with a stage presence, not intimate circles. Healing But Fun festivals, not small silent retreats.

**Leading skills are systems-based, not people-based.** "Building a system that runs without you in the room" and "Leading an AI team as a solo founder." Scale = technology + licensable IP, not hiring facilitators.

**Creating is the dominant category (6 skills).** The thing that lights you up most is making new things: frameworks, names, remixes, IP. This means the business should be structured so creation time is protected. The danger is spending all time on delivery (Core) and admin, leaving no time for the Creating that actually differentiates you.

**Storytelling skills are specifically about turning personal experience into universal truth.** "Turning my own exit route into a map others can follow." This is the Attraction engine: your life IS the content. Not researched content. Lived content.
