Capacity Score (0-100)

Capacity = (Safety / 10) x (Expression / 10) x 100

Examples:
• Safety 8, Expression 3 = 24 — "Safe but flat. Try a Wahoo."
• Safety 3, Expression 7 = 21 — "Anxious hustle. Do your practices."
• Safety 9, Expression 8 = 72 — "Vibe Rise. You're fully alive."
• Safety 5, Expression 5 = 25 — "Balanced but moderate. Push both axes."

The multiplication means both axes must be high for a high score. Safety 10, Expression 0 = 0. This is by design: safety without expression is not Vibe Rise.

Dot Position on 2x2

X position = Expression / 10 (0% = left edge, 100% = right edge)
Y position = (10 - Safety) / 10 (0% = top edge, 100% = bottom edge)

Trail dots show previous 3 weeks. The trajectory is often more meaningful than the current position. A dot moving from bottom-left to top-right across weeks shows real nervous system capacity growth.
