Journey Visual Ideas

6 concepts for combining or separating the Flow Map + Journey section. Scroll to compare.

Idea 1

The Constellation

Each milestone becomes a star. Connected by invisible threads, your journey forms a unique constellation in a personal night sky. Compass directions place stars in different quadrants.

Combines Both
Flow Redirect

Your constellation is rising northeast — a pattern of curiosity-driven momentum. The cluster near Flow suggests you're finding your rhythm.

Strengths
Beautiful, unique to each user. Compass + journey become one living visual. Deeply immersive.
Tradeoffs
More abstract — harder to read exact stats at a glance. Needs enough data points to look good.

Idea 5

The Pulse

Your energy as a heartbeat monitor. Peaks = Flow moments. Valleys = Rest periods. Quest completions and groans are marked pulse events. The overall trend shows your growth trajectory.

Combines Both

Your pulse is climbing — more time in the Flow Zone this week than last. The dip on Day 8 was healthy rest, not regression.

Strengths
Instantly readable. Energy levels are visceral — you FEEL the ups and downs. Trend line shows growth without needing stats. Very modern / health-app aesthetic.
Tradeoffs
Could feel clinical if not styled warmly. Doesn't show the "direction" aspect of compass as clearly.
Idea 6

The Seasons Wheel

A circular calendar where each week is a slice. Colors show your compass direction that week. Inner rings show quest/groan completions. Reveals rhythm and seasonal patterns. Ties into weekly planning phases (Push, Flow, Rest, Launch).
