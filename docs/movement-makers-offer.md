## The 5 Enhancers

### Scarcity
- 10 experience creators per monthly cohort
- When it's full, it's full

### Urgency
- Cohort rolling: "Next cohort starts [date]. Miss it, wait until next month."
- Experience date: "Your workshop is in 12 days. The checklist won't work itself." (real urgency from THEIR schedule)

### Bonuses (Value Stack)
The 9 app features listed above, enumerated individually so the value-to-price gap is undeniable.

### Guarantees (all three stacked)
1. **Anti-guarantee** (front door filter): "This is for facilitators who are ready to build. If you're still figuring out whether you want to run experiences, start with the free app."
2. **Unconditional** (removes risk at signup): "Try the first month. If it's not for you, full refund."
3. **Conditional** (rewards commitment): "Complete 3 checklists, run 1 experience through the system. If your attendance doesn't grow, next month is free."

### Naming
- **Product name:** Movement Makers
- **Tagline:** Get Paid To Have Fun
- **Identity:** Members are "Movement Makers" (what they put in their bio)
- **The promise:** Movement works on two levels: physical movement (dance, breathwork, somatic) AND cultural movement (building something bigger than one workshop)

## Who It's For

Experienced facilitators who:
- Already run workshops, retreats, breathwork, circles, or dance events
- Are confident in their facilitation but resist the business side
- Want to compound, not repeat (each experience building on the last)
- Are ready to build, not still figuring out if they want to

## Who It's NOT For

- Beginners who haven't run any experiences yet (start with the free app)
- People looking for another course to consume (this is a doing program)
- People who want to be told exactly what to do (this gives you the system, you run it)
