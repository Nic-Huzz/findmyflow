# Belief Frequency Map

> 80 safety contracts enriched with Hawkins consciousness levels, nervous system states, dominant emotions, and antidote beliefs. Reference document for future integration into the Vibe Rise belief system.

## How to Read This

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Safety Contract** | The fear-format belief tested via sway test (`nervousSystemBeliefs.js`) |
| **Hawkins** | Proposed Map of Consciousness level (logarithmic 1-1000) |
| **Emotion** | Dominant emotion holding this belief in place |
| **NS State** | Polyvagal nervous system state (`dorsal` = shutdown, `sympathetic` = fight/flight) |
| **Antidote** | The belief that replaces the contract when healed |
| **Antidote Hawkins** | Consciousness level of the antidote belief |
| **Antidote NS** | Nervous system state the antidote lives in (`ventral` = safe, `vibe_rise` = flow) |

### The Threshold

Everything below **200 (Courage)** tests as contraction. Everything above tests as expansion. The sway test is measuring which side of this line each belief lives on.

### Key Patterns

1. **Fear dominates.** 54% of all safety contracts live at Hawkins 100 (Fear/Sympathetic). This is the single most common frequency holding people back from crossing the 200 Courage threshold.

2. **Shame and Guilt form the foundation.** 26% of contracts (21 total) live at 20-30 (Shame/Guilt, Dorsal). These are the hardest to shift because dorsal shutdown means the person can't think their way out. The body has to move first.

3. **Most antidotes land in Ventral, not Vibe Rise.** The goal isn't to leap to 500 (Love). It's to cross 200 (Courage) and stabilise in 250-400 (Neutrality to Reason). Only 11 antidotes sit in Vibe Rise territory, and these are the deepest identity-level rewires ("I am enough", "My power is my safety", "My full power is exactly what's needed").

4. **Perfection, Worthiness, and Belonging wounds go deepest.** Average Hawkins levels by wound type: Perfection (52.5), Worthiness (63), Belonging (69), Visibility (86.5), Abundance (88.5), Safety (97.5), Impact (96), Stability (122.5). The three deepest wound types have the most Dorsal (shutdown) contracts, suggesting they may need somatic work before cognitive reframing can land.

5. **Pride-level contracts are the trickiest.** 6 contracts sit at 175 (Pride/Sympathetic). These feel like strength ("I delegate, things will be done wrong" or "I claim my value, I'll be seen as arrogant") but are actually protection. Pride is the last barrier before Courage. Users in this band may resist sway testing because the belief feels justified.

## Polyvagal Bridge (Maps to Vibe Rise 4-State System)

| Vibe Rise State | RP Score | Hawkins Range | Belief Band | What the Body Does |
|----------------|----------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Dorsal (Shutdown) | -2 | 20-75 | Collapse beliefs: shame, guilt, grief, apathy | Freeze, dissociate, go numb, withdraw, "I can't" |
| Sympathetic (Fight/Flight) | -1 | 100-175 | Protection beliefs: fear, anger, pride, desire | Hypervigilance, control, prove, defend, "I must" |
| Ventral (Safe) | +1 | 200-400 | Safety beliefs: courage, acceptance, reason | Grounded, present, open, willing, "I can" |
| Vibe Rise (Flow) | +2 | 500+ | Expansion beliefs: love, joy, peace | Flow state, creation, connection, "I am" |

**The Sway Test measures which side of 200 a belief lives on.** When the body sways "yes" to a fear statement, that contract is active below 200. The Wahoo challenges move the body across the 200 threshold through action (mismatch experience), and the Limiting Belief Rewire traces the contract back to its origin (memory reconsolidation).

## Integration Notes

- This data could enrich `nervousSystemBeliefs.js` as structured objects rather than plain strings
- A user's **Belief Frequency Score** = average Hawkins level of their active (sway-tested YES) contracts
- Tracking this score over time (as contracts are cleared via Limiting Belief Rewire or Wahoo challenges) gives a measurable "consciousness journey"
- The antidote beliefs are candidates for a future "affirmation" or "rewire confirmation" step in the flows
- Wound types that sit predominantly in Dorsal (Perfection, Worthiness, Belonging) may need different healing modalities than Sympathetic-dominant wounds (Stability, Safety, Impact). Somatic/breathwork for Dorsal. Courage challenges for Sympathetic.
