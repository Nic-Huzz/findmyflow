# Remarkable Event Bio Generator

## How to use this prompt

Feed it: the creator's name, their rule break, their unexpected combination, their simple truth, and their evidence. It outputs an event bio that makes the tribe think "hell yes."

## Why this works

Most event bios list credentials: "Dr. Jane Smith is a bestselling author, keynote speaker, and consultant who has worked with Fortune 500 companies..."

Nobody reads that. Nobody shares that. Nobody feels "hell yes" about that.

A remarkable bio communicates THREE things:
1. **The rule being broken** — this is what creates tribe identification ("I believe this too")
2. **The evidence it works** — this is what creates trust ("and they proved it")
3. **The simple truth** — this is what people remember and share ("their whole thing is...")

The format: Lead with the rule break (the thing that makes people say "wait, what?"). Follow with the evidence (the impossible result). Close with the simple truth (the sentence people will repeat).

## The Prompt

```
You write event bios that make people say "hell yes, I'm going."

Most event bios list credentials. Nobody shares credentials. People share STORIES about rule-breakers.

A great event bio does three things:
1. Opens with the rule they broke (the "wait, what?" moment)
2. Proves it worked (the impossible result)
3. Delivers their one simple truth (the sentence the audience will repeat to friends)

FORMAT:
- 3-4 sentences maximum
- First sentence: the rule break as a story beat. Written as gossip. "The [person] who [rule-breaking action]."
- Second sentence: the evidence it worked. Specific. Numbers or outcomes.
- Third sentence: their simple truth. The one-liner people quote.
- No em dashes, semicolons, or double hyphens
- No credential lists (no "bestselling author, keynote speaker, consultant")
- No superlatives ("world-renowned", "leading expert", "visionary")
- Write it as something you'd text a friend: "You HAVE to hear this person"

CONTEXT ABOUT THE PERSON:
- Name: {name}
- The rule they broke: {rule_break}
- How they broke it (what they actually did): {rule_break_action}
- The unexpected combination in their work: {unexpected_combination}
- Their simple truth (the sentence people remember): {simple_truth}
- Their impossible result (the evidence): {impossible_result}
- What they'll be talking about at this event: {event_topic}

Write the event bio.
```

## Examples (manually written from our data)

### Brene Brown
**Credential bio (what most events write):**
"Dr. Brené Brown is a research professor at the University of Houston, #1 New York Times bestselling author of six books, and host of two Spotify podcasts. Her TED talk on vulnerability is one of the most viewed in the world."

**Remarkable bio (what makes people say "hell yes"):**
"Brene Brown spent 8 years researching the thing nobody wants to talk about: shame. Then she got on a TED stage and talked about her own. 60 million people watched. Her whole thing is one sentence: vulnerability is not weakness, it is our most accurate measure of courage."

### Wim Hof
**Credential bio:**
"Wim Hof is a Dutch motivational speaker, extreme athlete, and holder of 26 Guinness World Records known for his ability to withstand extreme cold."

**Remarkable bio:**
"Wim Hof started sitting in freezing water every day after his wife died. He kept doing it for 24 years. Then scientists at Radboud University proved his breathing technique lets ordinary people control their immune system. His whole method fits on an index card: breathe and get cold."

### Gabor Mate
**Credential bio:**
"Dr. Gabor Maté is a bestselling author, physician, and internationally renowned speaker on addiction, trauma, childhood development, and the relationship of stress and illness."

**Remarkable bio:**
"Gabor Mate spent 12 years as a doctor in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside, working with the most addicted people in North America. Instead of asking why they used drugs, he asked why they were in pain. That one question changed how the world thinks about addiction. His documentary was watched by 2 million people in 6 days."

### Myles Horton
**Credential bio:**
"Myles Horton was an American educator and activist who founded the Highlander Folk School in Tennessee in 1932."

**Remarkable bio:**
"Myles Horton opened a school in rural Tennessee in 1932 built on one rule: the students already know the answers. The government shut it down. He reopened it the same year. Rosa Parks attended four months before the bus boycott. His school trained the leaders of every major American social movement of the 20th century."

### Marie Forleo
**Credential bio:**
"Marie Forleo is a life coach, motivational speaker, author, and web television host. She was named by Oprah as a thought leader for the next generation."

**Remarkable bio:**
"Marie Forleo was a bartender who started teaching business on her laptop with no business degree. 80,000 students and $100 million later, her entire philosophy fits in three words: Everything is Figureoutable."
