# Fantasy League — Content Category + Template System

## The 5th Category: Content Score

Every piece of content a player posts = points. The league promotes itself through its players.

### Scoring

| Action | Points | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Post a template (any platform) | 10 | Must tag #FindMyFlowLeague |
| Post a custom update | 15 | Original content about their journey |
| Video update (story/reel) | 25 | Face on camera = higher points |
| Tag/mention another squad | 10 | Drives cross-squad banter |
| Get engagement (10+ likes/comments) | 5 bonus | Rewards quality not just volume |
| Share daily leaderboard | 5 | Easy points, spreads awareness |
| Weekly squad recap video (60 sec) | 30 | The "match report" — one per squad per week |

**Weekly cap:** 100 points max per person (prevents spam, rewards quality)

**Verification:** Screenshot or link posted to squad Telegram group. Huzz/Sol verifies.

#### Template 13: "Huzz IG Live — Weekly Review" (hosted by Huzz)
```
[STRUCTURE — 20-30 min live]

1. RECAP (5 min)
   - This week's results: who won, who lost, category breakdowns
   - Leaderboard update — who's on top, who's climbing

2. HIGHLIGHTS (10 min)
   - Best Groan of the week — bring the player on if possible
   - Biggest business milestone hit
   - Best voice callout moment
   - "Play of the week" — one moment that stood out

3. HOT SEAT (5-10 min)
   - Pull in 1-2 players to talk about their week
   - "What did your protective voice say?"
   - "What's your groan for next week?"

4. PREVIEW (5 min)
   - Next week's matchups
   - Trash talk / predictions
   - Challenge the audience: "Take the Earthquake Quiz before next week"

[Save to IG, clip highlights for Reels/TikTok]
```

**Why this works:**
- Weekly appointment viewing (people tune in to see results)
- Players feel seen (being mentioned on a live)
- Clips from the live = 3-4 pieces of content for the week
- Audience sees real people transforming = social proof
- Quiz CTA at the end drives new leads every week

## Auto-Generated Content (Sol produces weekly)

### Monday: Matchup Announcement
- Graphic with team logos/names + matchup bracket
- Posted to main league Telegram + each squad can share

### Daily: Leaderboard Update
- Simple text or graphic showing current weekly category scores
- "LIVE SCORES — Day [X]"
- Shows which team is winning each category RIGHT NOW
- Creates urgency: "Team Flow is trailing in Healing by 12 points with 2 days left"

### Sunday Night: Match Report
- Full graphic: category-by-category breakdown
- MVP of each category
- "Moment of the week" (best Groan story)
- League table update

### End of Season: Season Recap
- Compile best moments from all 4 weeks
- Stats: total groans completed, total voice wins, biggest business milestone
- Before/after earthquake scores
- Champion crowned

## Platform Strategy

Players post wherever they're most active:
- **Instagram** (stories + reels) — visual templates, groan videos
- **LinkedIn** — more professional angle, "what I learned building in public"
- **TikTok** — groan challenge videos, raw/real energy
- **Twitter/X** — leaderboard shares, trash talk, quick updates
- **Telegram** (league group) — daily banter, verification screenshots

**All tagged #FindMyFlowLeague** — creates a searchable content trail
