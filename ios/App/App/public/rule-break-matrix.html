Five Patterns the Matrix Reveals

1. Orange Dominance

~70% of Rule Break Tree nodes are Orange. The entire modern era, from the Industrial Revolution to Silicon Valley, is an Orange project: efficiency, metrics, competition, scale.

2. Green Is the Current Wave

3. Yellow Is the Blow-Up Zone

Yellow entries are newest and most transformative. Lean manufacturing, mRNA vaccines, transformer AI. Yellow integrates ALL previous levels. Phase 3 products that blow up are Yellow disguised as Green.

4. Phase 3 = SD Transition

Phase 3 isn't just "going back to nature." It's a developmental transition: Green reacts to Orange excess, Yellow integrates everything. CrossFit activates 6 SD levels simultaneously. That's why it's a blow-up.

5. Bigger Jump = Bigger Break

Within-level breaks are incremental. Cross-level breaks are disruptive. Multi-level integrations are paradigm shifts. Count how many levels your product speaks to. Fewer than 3 = limited. 5+ = blow-up conditions.
