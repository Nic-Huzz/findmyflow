Three line states:

Coloured You did it AND it feels safe now. Inside the cone. This is real progress — your safety expanded to match your actions.

Grey solid You did it but it still scares you. Beyond the cone. "Head Full of Dreams" — action without safety. Healing flows move these into the coloured zone.

Dashed Haven't done it yet. The ghost of your possible future.

The progress bar shows both: grey fill = % done, coloured fill = % safe. The gap between them is your growth edge. Closing that gap IS the app's purpose.

Data inputs: "How did that feel?" (4-state) + "Better/As expected/Worse" after each courage challenge determine whether the cone expands. Vibe Rise + Better = cone grows. Pressure + Worse = cone stays, healing prompt appears.
